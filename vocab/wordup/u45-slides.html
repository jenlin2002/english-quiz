重點整理

說法中文例子
spend + 時間／錢 + V-ing／on花時間或錢做…spend 2 hours reading ／ spend $50 on a book
stop + V-ing / stop to V停止做 ／ 停下來去做stop eating ／ stop to eat
can't stand受不了can't stand the heat
stay up熬夜stay up late
go straight直走go straight two blocks
strange / stranger奇怪的 ／ 陌生人a strange man ／ a stranger
step by step一步一步learn step by step
steal-stole-stolen、stand-stood-stood、stick-stuck-stuck；stationery 不可數。
