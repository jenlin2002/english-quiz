重點整理

說法中文例子
make + 人 + 原形 / 形容詞使某人…make me laugh ／ make me happy
marry + 人 / get married和某人結婚 ／ 結婚marry Tom ／ get married in June
be mad at + 人生某人的氣mad at his brother
What's the matter?怎麼了？What's the matter with you?
male / female男性的 ／ 女性的a male nurse
manner / manners方式 ／ 禮貌good manners
How many + 複數名詞多少個How many apples?
man → men、mango → mangoes。
