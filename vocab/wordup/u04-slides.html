WORD UP「B-I」的 34 個字（baby 到 beard）。紅色＝要複習，藍色標籤＝WORD UP 標熟記、還要在這裡考對。

每個字都有 🔊，先聽發音、跟著唸，再看例句。

單字 1–4

👶
baby
嬰兒n.
The baby is crying.
寶寶在哭。
🔙
back
背部；後面；回來n./adv.
I will be back in ten minutes.
我十分鐘後回來。
⏪
backward
向後adv.
Can you count backward from ten?
你會從十倒數嗎？
👎
bad
壞的；不好的adj.
Eating too much candy is bad for your teeth.
吃太多糖果對牙齒不好。

單字 5–8

🏸

羽毛球n.

我們放學後打羽毛球。
👜
bag

Put your books in your bag.
把書放進你的包包。
🧁
bake
烘焙；烤v.
Mom is baking a cake for my birthday.
媽媽在烤我的生日蛋糕。
🥐
bakery
麵包店n.
I bought some bread at the bakery.
我在麵包店買了一些麵包。

常考片語

說法中文例子
come back / be back回來I'll be back soon.
take a bath洗澡take a bath before bed
play + 球類（不加 the）打球play badminton ／ play baseball
bark at + 人對…吠叫The dog barks at me.
can't bear受不了I can't bear the heat.
on a daily basis每天exercise on a daily basis
樂器要加 the：play the drums；球類不加：play basketball。
