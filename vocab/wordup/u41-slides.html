重點整理

說法中文例子
safe / safety安全的（形容詞）／ 安全（名詞）a safe place ／ safety first
on sale / for sale特價中 ／ 出售中on sale for 50% off
the same as和…一樣the same as mine
be scared of害怕…scared of the dark
search for尋找search for my keys
seem + 形容詞 / to V似乎…seem happy ／ seem to know
seldom很少（頻率低）I seldom watch TV.
see-saw-seen、say-said-said、seek-sought-sought；scarf → scarves。
