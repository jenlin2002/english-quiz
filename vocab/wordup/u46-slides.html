重點整理

說法中文例子
succeed / success / successful成功（動詞／名詞／形容詞）succeed in winning ／ a big success ／ a successful man
suggest + V-ing建議做…suggest going home
such a + 形容詞 + 名詞如此…的…such a cute dog
surprise / surprised驚喜 ／ 人感到驚訝a big surprise ／ I was surprised.
sudden / suddenly突然的 ／ 突然地a sudden rain ／ Suddenly it rained.
support支持support my dream
go swimming去游泳go swimming in the sea
strike-struck、sweep-swept、swim-swam-swum、swing-swung。
