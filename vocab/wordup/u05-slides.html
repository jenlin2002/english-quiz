常考片語

說法中文例子
belong to + 人屬於某人The book belongs to me.
believe in相信有、信任Do you believe in ghosts?
at the beginning of在…的開始at the beginning of the year
blame + 人 + for + 事因某事責怪某人blame him for the mistake
a bit / a little一點點a bit cold
go to bed上床睡覺go to bed early
belong 不用進行式：This pen belongs to me.（不說 is belonging）

容易搞混的字

字用法例子
besides / except除了…還有（包含）／ 除了…以外（不包含）Besides Tom, Amy came too. ／ Everyone came except Tom.
belief / believe名詞 ／ 動詞my belief ／ I believe you.
begin / beginner / beginning開始 ／ 初學者 ／ 開端begin to learn ／ a beginner ／ the beginning
good-better-best好 ／ 較好 ／ 最好good ／ better than ／ the best
bit / bite一點點 ／ 咬（過去式也是 bit）a bit ／ The dog bit me.
