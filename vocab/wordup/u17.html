字用法例子

exam / examine考試 ／ 檢查pass the exam ／ examine the car
even / ever / every甚至 ／ 曾經 ／ 每一even kids ／ Have you ever…? ／ every day
European 唸 /jʊ/ 開頭，前面用 a：a European country。
FOCUS

常考句型

說法中文例子
enjoy + V-ing喜歡做…enjoy swimming
enjoy oneself玩得開心We enjoyed ourselves at the party.
形容詞 + enough + to V夠…可以…old enough to drive
explain + 事 + to + 人向某人解釋某事explain the problem to me
Have you ever + p.p.?你曾經…過嗎？Have you ever seen snow?
for example例如Some fruits, for example apples, are red.
enter 後面直接接地點：enter the room（不說 enter into the room）。
