容易搞混的字

字用法例子
fail / failure失敗（動詞）／ 失敗（名詞）fail the exam ／ a big failure
few / a few很少（幾乎沒有）／ 一些Few people came. ／ A few people came.
fair / fare公平的、博覽會 ／ 車資a fair game ／ the bus fare
fee / fare費用（學費、入場費）／ 車資school fees ／ taxi fare
female / male女性的 ／ 男性的a female doctor ／ a male nurse
feel / fall感覺（felt）／ 落下（fell）I felt sick. ／ I fell down.
few 接可數名詞；little 接不可數名詞：a few books ／ a little water。
