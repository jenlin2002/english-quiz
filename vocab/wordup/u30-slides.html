每個字都有 🔊，先聽發音、跟著唸，再看例句。

單字 1–4

🙋
may
可以；可能aux.
May I come in?
我可以進來嗎？
🌷
May
五月n.
Mother's Day is in May.
母親節在五月。
🤷
maybe
也許adv.
Maybe it will rain tomorrow.
明天也許會下雨。
🍽️
meal
一餐n.
Breakfast is the most important meal.
早餐是最重要的一餐。

單字 5–8

❔
mean
意思是；刻薄的v./adj.
What does this word mean?
這個字是什麼意思？
📖
meaning
意思n.
Do you know the meaning of this word?
你知道這個字的意思嗎？
🚇
means
方法；手段n.
The MRT is a fast means of transportation.
捷運是快速的交通工具。
📏
measure
測量v.
Measure the length of the table.
量一下桌子的長度。

重點整理

說法中文例子
meat / meet肉 ／ 見面（同音）eat meat ／ meet friends
maybe / may be也許（副詞）／ 可能是（助動詞＋be）Maybe he is ill. ／ He may be ill.
Do you mind + V-ing?你介意…嗎？Do you mind closing the door?
mean / meaning意思是（動詞）／ 意思（名詞）What does it mean? ／ the meaning
miss想念 ／ 錯過miss you ／ miss the bus
in the middle of在…中間in the middle of the night
two million / millions of兩百萬 ／ 數百萬的millions of stars
mine 是所有格代名詞（= my + 名詞）：This book is mine.
