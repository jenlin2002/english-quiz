容易搞混的字

字用法例子
effect / effective影響（名詞）／ 有效的（形容詞）a good effect ／ an effective way
east / eastern東方 ／ 東部的in the east ／ eastern Taiwan
elect / election選出（動詞）／ 選舉（名詞）elect a leader ／ win the election
embarrassed / embarrassing人覺得尷尬 ／ 事情令人尷尬I was embarrassed. ／ an embarrassing moment
engine / engineer引擎 ／ 工程師a car engine ／ a software engineer
employ / employee / employer雇用 ／ 員工 ／ 雇主employ workers ／ a new employee ／ a kind employer

常考片語

說法中文例子
have an effect on對…有影響have an effect on health
make an effort努力make an effort to study
encourage + 人 + to V鼓勵某人做…encourage me to try
at the end of在…的末尾at the end of the year
in the end最後In the end, we won.
anything else / what else其他東西What else do you need?
elephant、eagle、egg、ear、engineer 開頭是母音，前面用 an。
