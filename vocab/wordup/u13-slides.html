單字 1–4

👨
dad
爸爸n.
My dad cooks dinner on Sundays.
我爸爸星期天煮晚餐。
📆
daily
每天的；每天adj./adv.
Exercise is part of my daily life.
運動是我日常生活的一部分。
🌪️
damage
損害v./n.
The typhoon damaged many houses.
颱風毀損了很多房子。
💃
dance
跳舞；舞蹈v./n.
Let's dance together!
我們一起跳舞吧！

一家人的字

字用法例子
die / dead / death死（動詞）／ 死的（形容詞）／ 死亡（名詞）die young ／ a dead tree ／ after his death
danger / dangerous危險（名詞）／ 危險的（形容詞）in danger ／ a dangerous road
decide / decision決定（動詞）／ 決定（名詞）decide to go ／ make a decision
democracy / democratic民主（名詞）／ 民主的（形容詞）a democracy ／ a democratic country
decrease / increase減少 ／ 增加decrease by 10% ／ increase quickly

常考片語

說法中文例子
deal with處理deal with the problem
depend on依靠、取決於It depends on you.
decide to V決定做…decide to join the club
deny + V-ing否認做過…deny taking the money
be in danger處於危險中The bird is in danger.
go to the dentist去看牙醫go to the dentist tomorrow
desert（沙漠，一個 s）和 dessert（甜點，兩個 s）很常考：甜點吃越多越好，所以 s 有兩個。
