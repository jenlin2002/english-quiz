重點整理

說法中文例子
sound / smell / taste + 形容詞聽起來 ／ 聞起來 ／ 嚐起來That sounds fun.
solve / solution解決（動詞）／ 解決辦法（名詞）solve the problem ／ a good solution
some / any肯定句 ／ 否定句、問句some milk ／ any milk?
as soon as一…就…Call me as soon as you arrive.
give a speech演講give a speech at school
south / southern南方 ／ 南部的southern Taiwan
sometimes有時候（頻率）I sometimes cook.
socks、sneakers、slippers 常用複數：a pair of socks。
