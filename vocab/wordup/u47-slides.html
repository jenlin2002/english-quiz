重點整理

說法中文例子
It takes + 人 + 時間 + to V某人花多少時間做…It takes me an hour to cook.
tell + 人 + to V叫某人做…tell him to wait
talk to / talk about跟…說話 ／ 談論talk to Amy about the trip
taste / smell + 形容詞嚐起來 ／ 聞起來taste sweet
take a taxi / take the MRT搭計程車 ／ 搭捷運take a taxi home
have a talent for有…的天分a talent for art
take care of / take a picture照顧 ／ 拍照take a picture of us
teach-taught、tell-told、take-took-taken、tear-tore-torn。
