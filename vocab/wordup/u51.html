說法中文例子
used to + 原形過去常常（現在不了）I used to cry a lot.
be used to + V-ing習慣於…I'm used to getting up early.
as usual照常He came late as usual.
on vacation度假中They are on vacation.
vote for投票給vote for Amy
a unique / a university / a unitu 唸 /ju/ 用 aa useful book
un- + 形容詞不…happy → unhappy
understand 的過去式 understood；underwear 不可數。
