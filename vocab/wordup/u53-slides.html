重點整理

說法中文例子
be willing to V願意做…willing to try
be worth + V-ing值得做…worth visiting
Would you like…?你想要…嗎？Would you like some water?
worry about擔心…worry about the test
wood / wooden木頭（名詞）／ 木製的（形容詞）made of wood ／ a wooden chair
What's wrong?怎麼了？What's wrong with your leg?
make a wish許願make a wish on your birthday
wife → wives、wolf → wolves、woman → women；win-won、write-wrote-written。
