UNIT 23 · VOCABULARY

職業

Occupations
27 個單字聽・唸・用

本單元單字一覽

各種職業和雇用相關的字，共 27 個。很多職業是在字尾加 -er（hunter）、-ist（artist、scientist）、-ian（magician、musician）。

artistassistantbabysitterbarbercowboydiplomatguidehairdresserhunterjournalistjudgemagicianmanagermechanicmodelmusicianownerpainterpresidentpriestsailorscientistservantvendorcompanyemployhire
每個字都有 🔊，先聽發音、跟著唸，再看例句。

單字 1–4

artist
藝術家；畫家n.
The artist painted a picture of the sea.
那位藝術家畫了一幅海的畫。
assistant
助理；助手n.
The teacher's assistant handed out the papers.
老師的助理發考卷。
babysitter
臨時保母n.
The babysitter played games with my little sister.
保母陪我妹妹玩遊戲。
barber
（男士）理髮師n.
The barber cut my hair very short.
理髮師把我的頭髮剪得很短。

單字 5–8

cowboy
牛仔n.
The cowboy rode his horse across the field.
那位牛仔騎馬穿過田野。
diplomat
外交官n.
A diplomat works for his country in other countries.
外交官在其他國家為自己的國家工作。
guide
導遊；嚮導；引導n. / v.
Our tour guide told us the history of the temple.
導遊告訴我們這座廟的歷史。
hairdresser
美髮師n.
My aunt is a hairdresser at a hair salon.
我阿姨是美髮店的美髮師。

單字 9–12

hunter
獵人n.
The hunter followed the deer into the forest.
獵人跟著鹿走進森林。
journalist
記者；新聞工作者n.
The journalist asked the mayor many questions.
那位記者問了市長很多問題。
judge
法官；裁判；判斷n. / v.
The judge listened to both sides.
法官聽了雙方的說法。
magician
魔術師n.
The magician pulled a rabbit out of his hat.
魔術師從帽子裡變出一隻兔子。

單字 13–16

manager
經理；管理人n.
I want to talk to the manager of the store.
我想找這家店的經理談談。
mechanic
技工；修車師傅n.
The mechanic fixed our car in one hour.
修車師傅一個小時就修好了我們的車。
model
模特兒；模型n.
The model walked down the runway in a red dress.
模特兒穿著紅洋裝走伸展台。
musician
音樂家；樂手n.
The street musician played the guitar.
街頭音樂家在彈吉他。

單字 17–20

owner
主人；擁有者n.
The owner of the dog thanked us for finding it.
狗的主人謝謝我們找到牠。
painter
畫家；油漆工n.
The painter painted our house blue.
油漆工把我們的房子漆成藍色。
president
總統；總裁；會長n.
The president gave a speech on TV.
總統在電視上發表演說。
priest
神父；牧師；祭司n.
The priest talked to the people in the church.
神父在教堂對大家說話。

單字 21–24

sailor
水手；船員n.
The sailors worked on the ship for months.
水手們在船上工作了好幾個月。
scientist
科學家n.
Scientists are studying the new virus.
科學家正在研究這種新病毒。
servant
僕人；傭人n.
In the story, the king had a hundred servants.
故事裡，國王有一百個僕人。
vendor
小販n.
The vendor sells hot sweet potatoes in winter.
那個小販冬天賣烤地瓜。

單字 25–27

company
公司；陪伴n.
My dad works for a computer company.
我爸爸在一家電腦公司上班。
employ
雇用v.
The factory employs 200 workers.
這家工廠雇用了兩百名工人。
hire
雇用；租用v.
We hired a guide to show us around.
我們雇了一位導遊帶我們參觀。

職業的字尾

字尾例子
-er／-orhunter、owner、manager、sailor
-istartist、journalist、scientist
-ianmagician、musician
-antassistant、servant
employ → employer（雇主）／employee（員工）：-er 是做這件事的人，-ee 是被這樣對待的人。
