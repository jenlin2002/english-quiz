Other verbs (3)
34 個單字聽・唸・用

本單元單字一覽

其他動詞第三部分，argue 到 increase，共 34 個。注意後面要接 V-ing 的動詞（avoid），以及成對的反義字 increase／decrease、appear／disappear。

arguearrangeassumeavoidbroadcastburstcalmcancelcomparecomplainconcerncontinuecreatedecreasedependdescribedetectdirectdisappeardiscoverdiscussemphasizeenvyexciteexpressfitfocusformgainhandleimproveincludeignoreincrease
每個字都有 🔊，先聽發音、跟著唸，再看例句。

單字 1–4

😠
argue
爭論；爭吵v.
My brother and I often argue about the TV.
我和哥哥常為了電視吵架。
💐
arrange
安排；整理v.
Mom arranged the flowers in a vase.
媽媽把花插在花瓶裡。
🧐
assume
以為；假定v.
I assumed you already knew.
我以為你已經知道了。
🚫
avoid
避免；避開v.
Try to avoid eating too much sugar.
盡量避免吃太多糖。

單字 5–8

📡
broadcast
廣播；播送v.
The game will be broadcast live on TV.
這場比賽會在電視上現場轉播。
💥
burst
爆炸；破裂；突然…v.
The balloon burst with a loud bang.
氣球砰的一聲爆了。
😌
calm
使平靜v.
Calm down and tell me what happened.
冷靜下來，告訴我發生了什麼事。
❌
cancel
取消v.
The game was canceled because of the rain.
比賽因為下雨取消了。

單字 9–12

⚖️
compare
比較v.
Don't compare yourself with others.
不要拿自己跟別人比較。
😒
complain
抱怨v.
He always complains about the food.
他總是抱怨食物。
😟
concern
使擔心；關係到v.
Mom is concerned about my health.
媽媽很擔心我的健康。
▶️
continue
繼續v.
Let's continue our story tomorrow.
我們明天繼續講故事吧。

單字 13–16

🎨
create
創造；創作v.
The artist created a beautiful painting.
那位畫家創作了一幅美麗的畫。
📉
decrease
減少；降低v.
The number of students is decreasing.
學生人數正在減少。
🤲
depend
依靠；取決於v.
Kids depend on their parents.
小孩依靠父母。
🗣️
describe
描述；形容v.
Can you describe the man you saw?
你能描述一下你看到的那個人嗎？

單字 17–20

🐕‍🦺
detect
發現；察覺；偵測v.
Dogs can detect smells that people can't.
狗能察覺人聞不到的氣味。
👮
direct
指揮；指引；導演v.
A police officer directed the traffic.
一位警察在指揮交通。
🫥
disappear
消失v.
The sun disappeared behind the clouds.
太陽消失在雲後面。
🧭
discover
發現v.
Columbus discovered America in 1492.
哥倫布在 1492 年發現美洲。

單字 21–24

💬
discuss
討論v.
Let's discuss the plan after class.
我們下課後討論這個計畫吧。
❗
emphasize
強調v.
The teacher emphasized the importance of reading.
老師強調閱讀的重要性。
😍
envy
羨慕；嫉妒v.
I envy you. You can travel all over the world.
我好羨慕你，你可以環遊世界。
🤩
excite
使興奮v.
The news excited everyone in the class.
這個消息讓全班都很興奮。

單字 25–28

💌
express
表達v.
It's hard for me to express my feelings.
要表達我的感受很難。
👟
fit
適合；（尺寸）合身v.
These shoes don't fit me. They're too small.
這雙鞋不合腳，太小了。
🎯
focus
集中；聚焦v.
Please focus on your homework.
請專心寫功課。
⭕
form
形成；組成v.
Let's form a circle and hold hands.
我們圍成一圈手牽手吧。

單字 29–32

📈
gain
得到；增加v.
He gained five kilos during the holidays.
他放假期間胖了五公斤。
🧰
handle
處理；應付v.
Can you handle this problem by yourself?
你能自己處理這個問題嗎？
📈
improve
改善；進步v.
Practice will improve your English.
練習會讓你的英文進步。
📦
include
包括v.
The price includes breakfast.
這個價錢包含早餐。

單字 33–34

🙈
ignore
忽視；不理會v.
He ignored me when I said hello.
我打招呼時他不理我。
⬆️
increase
增加v.
The price of eggs has increased.
雞蛋的價格上漲了。

意思相反

字反義字
increase 增加decrease 減少
appear 出現disappear 消失
continue 繼續cancel 取消
avoid 後面接 V-ing：avoid going out；discuss 後面直接接事情，不加 about。
