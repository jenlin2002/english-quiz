健康

Health
14 個單字聽・唸・用

本單元單字一覽

生病、受傷、看醫生會用到的字，共 14 個。注意 -ache 的病痛：stomachache、toothache。

dizzyillpainfulpalewoundcancerflustomachachetoothachecoughpainthroatcurerecover
每個字都有 🔊，先聽發音、跟著唸，再看例句。

單字 1–4

dizzy
頭暈的adj.
I felt dizzy after the roller coaster ride.
坐完雲霄飛車我覺得頭暈。
ill
生病的adj.
Mike is ill, so he stayed home today.
Mike 生病了，所以今天待在家。
painful
疼痛的；痛苦的adj.
My sore throat is very painful.
我的喉嚨痛得很厲害。
pale
蒼白的adj.
You look pale. Are you OK?
你臉色好蒼白，還好嗎？

單字 5–8

wound
傷口；使受傷n. / v.
The nurse cleaned the wound on my knee.
護士清理了我膝蓋上的傷口。
cancer
癌症；（大寫）巨蟹座n.
Smoking can cause cancer.
抽菸可能導致癌症。
flu
流行性感冒n.
Many students got the flu this winter.
今年冬天很多學生得了流感。
stomachache
胃痛；肚子痛n.
I ate too much ice cream and got a stomachache.
我吃太多冰淇淋，肚子痛了。

單字 9–12

toothache
牙痛n.
I have a toothache. I need to see a dentist.
我牙痛，需要去看牙醫。
cough
咳嗽v. / n.
The boy kept coughing all night.
那個男孩整晚一直咳嗽。
pain
疼痛n.
I have a pain in my back.
我背痛。
throat
喉嚨n.
Drink warm water for your sore throat.
喉嚨痛就喝溫開水。

單字 13–14

cure
治療；治好v. / n.
Doctors hope to cure the disease one day.
醫生希望有一天能治好這種病。
recover
恢復；康復v.
Grandpa is recovering from a cold.
爺爺感冒正在康復中。

-ache 疼痛

部位疼痛
stomach 胃stomachache 胃痛
tooth 牙齒toothache 牙痛
head 頭headache 頭痛
說「我…痛」用 I have a ___ache.；喉嚨痛說 I have a sore throat.
