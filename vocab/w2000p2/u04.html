VOCABULARY · 主題 2000 字 PART 2 UNIT 4

Health．健康

生病、受傷、看醫生會用到的字，共 14 個。注意 -ache 的病痛：stomachache、toothache。
📊 投影片📝 單元測驗（計點數）
共 14 個單字．點 🔊 聽發音
1dizzyadj.
頭暈的
feel dizzy 覺得頭暈
I felt dizzy after the roller coaster ride.坐完雲霄飛車我覺得頭暈。
2illadj.
生病的
= sick；ill 多放在 be 動詞後面
Mike is ill, so he stayed home today.Mike 生病了，所以今天待在家。
3painfuladj.
疼痛的；痛苦的
pain + -ful
My sore throat is very painful.我的喉嚨痛得很厲害。
4paleadj.
蒼白的
turn pale 臉色發白
You look pale. Are you OK?你臉色好蒼白，還好嗎？
5woundn. / v.
傷口；使受傷
讀 [wund]
The nurse cleaned the wound on my knee.護士清理了我膝蓋上的傷口。
6cancern.
癌症；（大寫）巨蟹座
lung cancer 肺癌
Smoking can cause cancer.抽菸可能導致癌症。
7flun.
流行性感冒
get the flu 得流感；cold 是一般感冒
Many students got the flu this winter.今年冬天很多學生得了流感。
8stomachachen.
胃痛；肚子痛
stomach + ache；have a stomachache
I ate too much ice cream and got a stomachache.我吃太多冰淇淋，肚子痛了。
9toothachen.
牙痛
tooth + ache
I have a toothache. I need to see a dentist.我牙痛，需要去看牙醫。
10coughv. / n.
咳嗽
gh 讀 [f]
The boy kept coughing all night.那個男孩整晚一直咳嗽。
11painn.
疼痛
have a pain in… …痛
I have a pain in my back.我背痛。
12throatn.
喉嚨
have a sore throat 喉嚨痛
Drink warm water for your sore throat.喉嚨痛就喝溫開水。
13curev. / n.
治療；治好
cure + 病／人
Doctors hope to cure the disease one day.醫生希望有一天能治好這種病。
14recoverv.
恢復；康復
recover from 從…康復
Grandpa is recovering from a cold.爺爺感冒正在康復中。
FOCUS

-ache 疼痛

部位疼痛
stomach 胃stomachache 胃痛
tooth 牙齒toothache 牙痛
head 頭headache 頭痛
說「我…痛」用 I have a ___ache.；喉嚨痛說 I have a sore throat.
