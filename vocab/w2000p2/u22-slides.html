節日與慶典

Holidays & festivals
16 個單字聽・唸・用

本單元單字一覽

台灣和西方的節日，還有文化、習俗、燈籠和回憶，共 16 個。節日名稱每個字的第一個字母大寫；「在某個節日」用 on（on Mother's Day）。

Chinese New YearNew Year's EveDouble Tenth DayDragon Boat FestivalLantern FestivalMoon FestivalTeacher's DayNew Year's DayMother's DayFather's DayThanksgivingValentine's Dayculturecustomlanternmemory
每個字都有 🔊，先聽發音、跟著唸，再看例句。

單字 1–4

Chinese New Year
農曆新年；春節n.
Kids get red envelopes at Chinese New Year.
過年時小孩會拿到紅包。
New Year's Eve
除夕；跨年夜n.
We watch fireworks on New Year's Eve.
我們在跨年夜看煙火。
Double Tenth Day
雙十節；國慶日n.
Double Tenth Day is Taiwan's National Day.
雙十節是台灣的國慶日。
Dragon Boat Festival
端午節n.
We watch dragon boat races at the Dragon Boat Festival.
端午節我們看龍舟比賽。

單字 5–8

Lantern Festival
元宵節n.
People eat sweet dumplings at the Lantern Festival.
元宵節大家吃湯圓。
Moon Festival
中秋節n.
Families eat moon cakes and pomelos at the Moon Festival.
中秋節家人一起吃月餅和柚子。
Teacher's Day
教師節n.
We made cards for our teachers on Teacher's Day.
教師節我們做卡片送老師。
New Year's Day
元旦n.
New Year's Day is January 1st.
元旦是一月一日。

單字 9–12

Mother's Day
母親節n.
I made breakfast for Mom on Mother's Day.
母親節我幫媽媽做早餐。
Father's Day
父親節n.
We took Dad out to dinner on Father's Day.
父親節我們帶爸爸出去吃晚餐。
Thanksgiving
感恩節n.
Americans eat turkey on Thanksgiving.
美國人感恩節吃火雞。
Valentine's Day
情人節n.
He gave her chocolate on Valentine's Day.
情人節他送她巧克力。

單字 13–16

culture
文化n.
I want to learn more about Japanese culture.
我想多了解日本文化。
🏯
custom
習俗；風俗n.
Giving red envelopes is a Chinese New Year custom.
發紅包是過年的習俗。
lantern
燈籠n.
The kids carried paper lanterns at night.
孩子們晚上提著紙燈籠。
memory
記憶；回憶n.
I have many happy memories of my childhood.
我有很多快樂的童年回憶。

節日在哪一天

節日時間
Chinese New Year農曆一月一日
Lantern Festival農曆一月十五日
Dragon Boat Festival農曆五月五日
Moon Festival農曆八月十五日
Double Tenth Day十月十日
「在某節日當天」用 on：on Mother's Day、on Valentine's Day。
