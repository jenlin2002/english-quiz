可數還是不可數？

可數（a／an、加 s）不可數（不加 s）不可數怎麼數
an apple → applesbreada piece of bread
an egg → eggswatera glass of water
a cookie → cookiesmilka bottle of milk
a sandwich → sandwichesteaa cup of tea
a tomato → tomatoesricea bowl of rice
a strawberry → strawberriessoupa bowl of soup
-o 結尾加 es：tomato → tomatoes；子音 + y 改 ies：strawberry → strawberries。chicken、fish 當「肉」時不可數：I had chicken for lunch.；fish 的複數多半還是 fish。

在餐廳點餐

英文中文
Can I see the menu, please?可以給我看菜單嗎？
May I take your order?可以幫您點餐了嗎？
I'd like a hamburger and fries.我要一個漢堡和薯條。
What would you like to drink?您想喝點什麼？
A glass of orange juice, please.請給我一杯柳橙汁。
For here or to go?內用還是外帶？
三餐前不加 a 或 the：have breakfast、eat lunch。描述味道用 taste + 形容詞：It tastes sweet.；I'm hungry／thirsty／full. 是餓、渴、飽。
