Other adverbs．其他副詞

頻率副詞（always、usually、often、sometimes、seldom、never）和其他常用副詞，共 36 個。頻率副詞放在 be 動詞後面、一般動詞前面。

共 36 個單字．點 🔊 聽發音
🔁
1alwaysadv.
總是
100%
Lily always wears red shoes.Lily 總是穿紅色的鞋子。
❔
2everadv.
曾經
多用在問句
Have you ever been to Japan?你曾經去過日本嗎？
🚫
3neveradv.
從不；絕不
0%
Jay is never late for school.Jay 上學從不遲到。
🔂
4oftenadv.
時常
How often do you go to the movies?你多常去看電影？
🤏
5seldomadv.
很少；不常
My grandpa seldom watches TV.我爺爺很少看電視。
🌗
6sometimesadv.
有時候
I sometimes go swimming on weekends.我週末有時候會去游泳。
🚶
7usuallyadv.
通常
He usually walks to school.他通常走路上學。
💡
8actuallyadv.
實際上；事實上
He looks scary, but actually he is very kind.他看起來很兇，但其實人很好。
🔄
9againadv.
再一次
Can you say that again?你可以再說一次嗎？
➕
10alsoadv.
也
用在肯定句，放在動詞前
I like cats. I also like dogs.我喜歡貓，我也喜歡狗。
🏃
11awayadv.
遠離地；離開
go away 走開
The school is far away from my house.學校離我家很遠。
✌️
12tooadv.
也；太
肯定句句尾用 too
Your pen is blue, and mine is, too.你的筆是藍色的，我的也是。
🫣
13almostadv.
幾乎；差一點
I almost missed the train.我差點錯過火車。
😯
14evenadv.
甚至；連
He lied to everyone, even his family.他對所有人說謊，甚至是他的家人。
🏁
15finallyadv.
最後；終於
Finally, I passed the exam.我終於通過考試了。
⏱️
16justadv.
正好；僅僅；剛剛
I just finished my homework.我剛寫完功課。
📉
17leastadv.
最少；最小
at least 至少
You should sleep at least eight hours.你應該至少睡八個小時。
🤷
18maybeadv.
也許
放句首；may be 是「可能是」
Maybe it will rain tomorrow.也許明天會下雨。
🤔
19perhapsadv.
也許
比 maybe 正式
Perhaps she is on her way.也許她已經在路上了。
📊
20probablyadv.
大概；很可能
比 maybe 更確定
He will probably come tonight.他今晚很可能會來。
💯
21reallyadv.
真正地；非常
I really like this song.我真的很喜歡這首歌。
😍
22soadv.
如此地；非常
也可當連接詞：所以
Snow White is so kind!白雪公主真善良！
⏳
23stilladv.
仍然
Are you still hungry?你還餓嗎？
➡️
24thenadv.
然後；當時
He did his homework, and then he went to bed.他寫完功課，然後去睡覺。
👨‍👩‍👧
25togetheradv.
一起
Let's go shopping together.我們一起去逛街吧。
✌️
26twiceadv.
兩次；兩倍
once 一次、three times 三次
I visit my grandparents twice a month.我一個月去看爺爺奶奶兩次。
‼️
27veryadv.
非常
The movie was very funny.這部電影非常好笑。
📏
28quiteadv.
相當
He is quite tall.他相當高。
⌛
29yetadv.
尚未；已經（問句）
用在否定句、問句
I haven't finished my homework yet.我還沒寫完功課。
✈️
30abroadadv.
在國外；到國外
study abroad 出國留學
She wants to study abroad.她想出國留學。
⏩
31aheadadv.
在前方；提前
go ahead 請便、繼續
The bus stop is just ahead.公車站就在前面。
📍
32somewhereadv.
某處
My keys must be somewhere in this room.我的鑰匙一定在這個房間的某個地方。
🙅
33eitheradv.
也（否定句）
否定句句尾用 either
I don't like fish. My sister doesn't, either.我不喜歡魚，我妹妹也不喜歡。
❌
34noadv.
不；沒有
No, I'm not tired.不，我不累。
🚫
35notadv.
不
Snoopy is a dog, not a cat.史努比是狗，不是貓。
✅
36yesadv.
是的
Yes, I love English.是的，我愛英文。
FOCUS

頻率副詞

副詞頻率例句
always100%I always get up at six.
usually80%He usually walks to school.
often60%We often play basketball.
sometimes40%She sometimes cooks dinner.
seldom10%Grandpa seldom watches TV.
never0%Jay is never late.
位置：be 動詞、助動詞後面，一般動詞前面。問頻率用 How often…?
FOCUS

「也」的三種說法

副詞用在例句
also肯定句，放動詞前I also like dogs.
too肯定句，放句尾I like dogs, too.
either否定句，放句尾I don't like fish, either.
FOCUS

也許、大概

副詞確定程度例句
maybe一般Maybe it will rain.
perhaps一般（較正式）Perhaps he is busy.
probably比較確定He will probably come.
maybe（一個字，副詞）和 may be（助動詞＋be）不一樣：He may be a lawyer. ＝ Maybe he is a lawyer.
