健康

Health
15 個單字聽・唸・用

本單元單字一覽

身體狀況和生病時會用到的字，共 15 個。注意 health／healthy 的詞性，以及 have a cold、have a fever 的說法。

comfortablehealthysickstrongtiredweakwellcoldheadachefeversoredeathhealthlifemedicine
每個字都有 🔊，先聽發音、跟著唸，再看例句。

單字 1–4

comfortable
舒服的adj.
This sofa is soft and comfortable.
這張沙發又軟又舒服。
healthy
健康的adj.
Fruit and vegetables keep you healthy.
蔬菜水果讓你保持健康。
sick
生病的adj.
Tom is sick, so he stays home today.
湯姆生病了，所以今天待在家。
strong
強壯的；強烈的adj.
My dad is strong. He can carry me.
我爸爸很強壯，他抱得動我。

單字 5–8

tired
疲累的；厭煩的adj.
I am tired after the long walk.
走了很長的路，我好累。
weak
虛弱的；弱的adj.
The baby bird is too weak to fly.
這隻雛鳥太虛弱了，飛不起來。
well
健康的；很好地adj. / adv.
She sings very well.
她唱歌唱得很好。
cold
寒冷的；感冒adj. / n.
I have a cold. I need some rest.
我感冒了，需要休息一下。

單字 9–12

headache
頭痛n.
I have a headache. I need to lie down.
我頭痛，需要躺一下。
fever
發燒n.
She has a high fever.
她發高燒。
sore
疼痛的；痠痛的adj.
My eyes are sore after playing games.
打完電動後我的眼睛好痛。
death
死亡n.
The movie is about life and death.
這部電影講的是生與死。

單字 13–15

health
健康n.
Exercise is good for your health.
運動有益健康。
life
生命；人生；生活n.
Life is short. Enjoy every day.
人生苦短，好好享受每一天。
medicine
藥；醫學n.
Take this medicine three times a day.
這個藥一天吃三次。

形容詞和名詞

形容詞名詞例句
healthy 健康的health 健康Health is important.
sick 生病的sickness 疾病He is sick today.
dead 死的death 死亡The fish is dead.
strong 強壯的strength 力氣She is very strong.
weak 虛弱的weakness 弱點I feel weak.
be 動詞後面接形容詞：I am healthy.；介系詞、所有格後面接名詞：good for your health。字尾 -able「能…的」：comfort → comfortable。

生病了怎麼說？

英文中文
I have a cold.我感冒了。
I have a fever.我發燒了。
I have a headache.我頭痛。
My throat is sore.我喉嚨痛。
I don't feel well.我身體不舒服。
Take some medicine and get some rest.吃點藥，休息一下。
have a ＋ 病症（a cold、a fever、a headache）；feel ＋ 形容詞（feel tired、feel sick）。well 當形容詞是「健康的」，所以 I don't feel well. ＝ 我身體不舒服。
