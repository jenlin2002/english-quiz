Wh-words．疑問詞

問「誰、什麼、哪裡、什麼時候、為什麼、怎樣」的 9 個字。看到疑問詞，就要想好回答要給什麼資訊。
📊 投影片📝 單元測驗（計點數）
共 9 個單字．點 🔊 聽發音
🤔
1howadv.
如何；怎樣；多麼
how old 幾歲、how many 多少
How do you go to school?你怎麼去上學？
❓
2whatpron./adj.
什麼
What is your name?你叫什麼名字？
🔴
3whichpron./adj.
哪一個
從幾個裡面選
Which color do you like, red or blue?你喜歡哪個顏色，紅色還是藍色？
👤
4whopron.
誰
Who is that girl?那個女孩是誰？
🎒
5whosepron./adj.
誰的
不要和 who's（＝who is）搞混
Whose bag is this?這是誰的包包？
📅
6whenadv./conj.
什麼時候；當…的時候
When is your birthday?你的生日是什麼時候？
📍
7whereadv.
哪裡
Where do you live?你住在哪裡？
⚖️
8whetherconj.
是否
常說 whether… or not
I don't know whether he will come.我不知道他是否會來。
⏰
9whyadv.
為什麼
回答用 Because…
Why are you late?你為什麼遲到？
FOCUS

問什麼、答什麼

疑問詞問例句與回答
who人Who is he? — He is my uncle.
whose誰的東西Whose pen is this? — It's Sam's.
what東西、事情What are you doing? — I'm cooking.
which從幾個中選一個Which one is yours? — The red one.
when時間When will you come? — Tomorrow.
where地點Where is he from? — England.
why原因Why are you late? — Because I got up late.
how方法、狀況How is the weather? — It's sunny.
FOCUS

how 家族

說法問例句
how old年紀How old is your sister?
how many數量（可數）How many books do you have?
how much價錢；量（不可數）How much is this shirt?
how tall身高How tall are you?
how long多久；多長How long is the movie?
how often多常How often do you play tennis?
FOCUS

疑問詞放在句子中間

直接問放進句子裡
Where does he live?I don't know where he lives.
Will it rain?I don't know whether it will rain.
放進句子裡時，順序改回「主詞＋動詞」；沒有疑問詞的問句用 whether（是否）。
