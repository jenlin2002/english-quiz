UNIT 23 · VOCABULARY

職業

Occupations
30 個單字聽・唸・用

本單元單字一覽

30 個和工作、職業有關的字。很多職業是「動作＋-er／-or」（drive → driver、act → actor），也有 -man 結尾、複數變 -men 的字。

actoractressbossbusinessmanclerkcookdentistdoctordriverengineerfarmerfishermanhousewifelawyermailmannursepolice officerreportersalesmansecretaryshopkeepersingersoldierwaiterwaitressworkerwriterbusinessjobwork
每個字都有 🔊，先聽發音、跟著唸，再看例句。

單字 1–4

actor
演員；男演員n.
The actor practiced his lines every night.
那位演員每天晚上練台詞。
actress
女演員n.
She became a famous actress at the age of twenty.
她二十歲就成了有名的女演員。
boss
老闆；主人n.
My boss lets us go home early on Fridays.
我老闆星期五讓我們早點回家。
businessman
生意人；企業家n.
The businessman flies to Japan twice a month.
那位生意人一個月飛日本兩次。

單字 5–8

clerk
職員；店員n.
The clerk at the counter helped me find my size.
櫃台店員幫我找到我的尺寸。
cook
廚師n.
The cook in this restaurant makes great fried rice.
這家餐廳的廚師炒飯做得很棒。
dentist
牙醫n.
Visit the dentist twice a year to check your teeth.
一年要看兩次牙醫檢查牙齒。
doctor
醫生；博士n.
The doctor told me to drink more water.
醫生叫我多喝水。

單字 9–12

driver
司機；駕駛n.
The bus driver said hello to every student.
公車司機和每個學生打招呼。
engineer
工程師；技師n.
The engineer is fixing the computer system.
工程師正在修電腦系統。
farmer
農夫；農民n.
The farmer grows rice and corn.
那位農夫種稻米和玉米。
fisherman
漁夫；漁民n.
The fisherman goes out to sea before sunrise.
漁夫在日出前出海。

單字 13–16

housewife
家庭主婦n.
My aunt is a housewife and takes care of three kids.
我阿姨是家庭主婦，照顧三個孩子。
lawyer
律師n.
You should talk to a lawyer before you sign it.
你簽名之前應該先問律師。
mailman
郵差n.
The mailman brings letters every morning.
郵差每天早上送信來。
nurse
護士n.
The nurse took my temperature.
護士幫我量了體溫。

單字 17–20

police officer
警官；警員n.
A police officer showed us the way to the station.
一位警察告訴我們去車站的路。
reporter
記者n.
The reporter asked the singer some questions.
記者問了歌手幾個問題。
salesman
推銷員；業務員n.
The salesman showed us three kinds of phones.
業務員給我們看了三種手機。
secretary
秘書n.
The secretary answers the phone for the boss.
秘書幫老闆接電話。

單字 21–24

shopkeeper
店主；老闆n.
The shopkeeper knows every kid on this street.
那位店老闆認識這條街上每個小孩。
singer
歌手n.
She wants to be a singer when she grows up.
她長大想當歌手。
soldier
士兵；軍人n.
The soldiers helped people after the typhoon.
颱風過後軍人幫忙災民。
waiter
（男）服務生n.
The waiter brought us the menu.
服務生拿菜單給我們。

單字 25–28

waitress
（女）服務生n.
The waitress asked what we wanted to drink.
女服務生問我們要喝什麼。
worker
工人n.
The workers are building a new bridge.
工人們正在蓋一座新橋。
writer
作家n.
J.K. Rowling is a famous writer.
J.K. 羅琳是一位有名的作家。
business
生意；企業；公司n.
My uncle runs a small business in Tainan.
我叔叔在臺南經營一家小公司。

單字 29–30

job
工作n.
My sister found a part-time job at a bookstore.
我姊姊在書店找到一份兼職工作。
work
工作；作品n./v.
I have a lot of work to do today.
我今天有很多工作要做。

職業字怎麼來的

規則例子
動詞＋-erdrive → driver、sing → singer、report → reporter、write → writer、work → worker
名詞＋-erfarm → farmer、law → lawyer
動詞＋-oract → actor
…＋-man（複數 -men）businessman、fisherman、mailman、salesman
女性：-essactor → actress、waiter → waitress
注意：cook 本身就是「廚師」，cooker 是「炊具、電鍋」。

特別的複數

單數複數
businessmanbusinessmen
fishermanfishermen
housewifehousewives
secretarysecretaries
actress / waitress / bossactresses / waitresses / bosses
-man → -men；-fe → -ves；子音＋y → -ies；-ss → -sses。

問職業、job 和 work

說法中文
What does your father do? — He is an engineer.你爸爸做什麼工作？——他是工程師。
What do you want to be? — I want to be a doctor.你想當什麼？——我想當醫生。
She has a job. / She has two jobs.她有一份工作。／她有兩份工作。（job 可數）
I have a lot of work.我有很多工作。（work 不可數）
說職業要加 a／an：He is a nurse. / She is an actress.
