連接詞

Conjunctions
12 個單字聽・唸・用

本單元單字一覽

把兩個字或兩個句子接起來的字，共 12 個。注意：because 和 so、though 和 but 不能一起用。

andasbecausebuthoweveriforsincethanthatthoughwhile
每個字都有 🔊，先聽發音、跟著唸，再看例句。

單字 1–4

➕
and
和；而且conj.
I like apples and bananas.
我喜歡蘋果和香蕉。
🌦️
as
當…時；因為；如同conj./prep.
As I was walking home, it started to rain.
我走路回家的時候，開始下雨了。
🤒
because
因為conj.
I stayed home because I was sick.
我待在家，因為我生病了。
↩️
but
但是conj.
The car is nice, but it's too expensive.
這台車很好，但是太貴了。

單字 5–8

🔀
however
然而adv.
It was raining. However, we still went out.
當時在下雨。然而我們還是出門了。
☔
if
如果；是否conj.
If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home.
如果明天下雨，我們就待在家。
🔁
or
或者；否則conj.
Do you want tea or coffee?
你要茶還是咖啡？
📆
since
自從；既然conj.
Since you are here, let's start.
既然你來了，我們就開始吧。

單字 9–12

📏
than
比conj.
She is younger than I am.
她比我年輕。
💭
that
（引導名詞子句）conj.
I think that he is right.
我認為他是對的。
🏃
though
雖然；不過conj./adv.
Though he is old, he still runs every day.
雖然他年紀大了，他還是每天跑步。
🚿
while
當…的時候conj.
Mom called while I was taking a shower.
我在洗澡的時候媽媽打電話來。

對等連接詞

連接詞關係例句
and加上He is tall and strong.
but相反He is tall but weak.
or選擇；否則Hurry up, or you'll be late.
so結果I was tired, so I went to bed.

不能一起用的兩對

✗ 錯誤✓ 正確
Because it rained, so we stayed home.Because it rained, we stayed home. ／ It rained, so we stayed home.
Though he is rich, but he is not happy.Though he is rich, he is not happy. ／ He is rich, but he is not happy.
because 和 so 選一個；though 和 but 選一個。

表示時間、條件的連接詞

連接詞意思例句
when／as／while當…的時候While I was reading, he came in.
since自從；既然I have known her since I was six.
if如果If you are free, call me.
if、when 表示未來時，子句用現在式：If it rains tomorrow, …（不是 will rain）。
