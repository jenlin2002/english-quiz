Conjunctions．連接詞

把兩個字或兩個句子接起來的字，共 12 個。注意：because 和 so、though 和 but 不能一起用。
📊 投影片📝 單元測驗（計點數）
共 12 個單字．點 🔊 聽發音
➕
1andconj.
和；而且
I like apples and bananas.我喜歡蘋果和香蕉。
🌦️
2asconj./prep.
當…時；因為；如同
as… as… 和…一樣
As I was walking home, it started to rain.我走路回家的時候，開始下雨了。
🤒
3becauseconj.
因為
不和 so 一起用
I stayed home because I was sick.我待在家，因為我生病了。
↩️
4butconj.
但是
The car is nice, but it's too expensive.這台車很好，但是太貴了。
🔀
5howeveradv.
然而
前後常加逗點
It was raining. However, we still went out.當時在下雨。然而我們還是出門了。
☔
6ifconj.
如果；是否
If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home.如果明天下雨，我們就待在家。
🔁
7orconj.
或者；否則
Do you want tea or coffee?你要茶還是咖啡？
📆
8sinceconj.
自從；既然
Since you are here, let's start.既然你來了，我們就開始吧。
📏
9thanconj.
比
比較級＋than
She is younger than I am.她比我年輕。
💭
10thatconj.
（引導名詞子句）
常可以省略
I think that he is right.我認為他是對的。
🏃
11thoughconj./adv.
雖然；不過
＝although，不和 but 一起用
Though he is old, he still runs every day.雖然他年紀大了，他還是每天跑步。
🚿
12whileconj.
當…的時候
常接進行式
Mom called while I was taking a shower.我在洗澡的時候媽媽打電話來。
FOCUS

對等連接詞

連接詞關係例句
and加上He is tall and strong.
but相反He is tall but weak.
or選擇；否則Hurry up, or you'll be late.
so結果I was tired, so I went to bed.
FOCUS

不能一起用的兩對

✗ 錯誤✓ 正確
Because it rained, so we stayed home.Because it rained, we stayed home. ／ It rained, so we stayed home.
Though he is rich, but he is not happy.Though he is rich, he is not happy. ／ He is rich, but he is not happy.
because 和 so 選一個；though 和 but 選一個。
FOCUS

表示時間、條件的連接詞

連接詞意思例句
when／as／while當…的時候While I was reading, he came in.
since自從；既然I have known her since I was six.
if如果If you are free, call me.
if、when 表示未來時，子句用現在式：If it rains tomorrow, …（不是 will rain）。
