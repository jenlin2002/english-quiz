介系詞

Prepositions
38 個單字聽・唸・用

本單元單字一覽

表示位置、方向、時間的小字，共 38 個。介系詞後面接名詞或受格代名詞（with me、for him）。

aboutaboveacrossafteralongaroundatbeforebehindbelowbesidebetweenbydownduringexceptforfromininsideintolikenearofoffonoutoutsideoversincethanthroughtounderuntilupwithwithout
每個字都有 🔊，先聽發音、跟著唸，再看例句。

單字 1–4

📖
about
關於；大約prep.
This is a book about dinosaurs.
這是一本關於恐龍的書。
⬆️
above
在…上方prep.
The birds are flying above the trees.
鳥在樹的上方飛。
🚸
across
橫越；在…對面prep.
We walked across the street.
我們走過馬路。
⏭️
after
在…之後prep.
Let's play basketball after school.
放學後我們去打籃球吧。

單字 5–8

🛤️
along
沿著prep.
We rode our bikes along the river.
我們沿著河騎腳踏車。
🔄
around
在…周圍；到處；大約prep.
We sat around the table.
我們圍著桌子坐。
📍
at
在（地點、時間點）prep.
The movie starts at seven.
電影七點開始。
⏮️
before
在…之前prep.
Wash your hands before dinner.
晚餐前要洗手。

單字 9–12

🙈
behind
在…後面prep.
The cat is hiding behind the sofa.
貓躲在沙發後面。
⬇️
below
在…下方；低於prep.
The temperature is below zero.
氣溫在零度以下。
👫
beside
在…旁邊prep.
Come and sit beside me.
過來坐我旁邊。
↔️
between
在（兩者）之間prep.
The bank is between the post office and the park.
銀行在郵局和公園之間。

單字 13–16

🖌️
by
被；由；在…旁邊；搭乘prep.
This picture was painted by my sister.
這幅畫是我姊姊畫的。
⤵️
down
沿著…往下prep.
The ball rolled down the hill.
球滾下山坡。
⏳
during
在…期間prep.
Don't talk during the test.
考試期間不要說話。
🚫
except
除了…之外prep.
I go to school every day except Sunday.
除了星期天，我每天都上學。

單字 17–20

🎁
for
為了；給；（時間）長達prep.
This gift is for you.
這個禮物是給你的。
🛫
from
從；來自prep.
She comes from Japan.
她來自日本。
📦
in
在…裡面；在（月、年、城市）prep.
My birthday is in July.
我的生日在七月。
🏠
inside
在…裡面prep.
It's raining. Let's stay inside the house.
在下雨，我們待在屋子裡吧。

單字 21–24

🤽
into
進入prep.
The boy jumped into the pool.
男孩跳進游泳池。
🐦
like
像prep.
She sings like a bird.
她唱歌像小鳥一樣。
📌
near
在…附近prep.
My house is near the school.
我家在學校附近。
🔗
of
…的prep.
The color of the car is red.
這台車的顏色是紅色。

單字 25–28

🚌
off
離開；脫離prep.
Please get off the bus at the next stop.
請在下一站下公車。
☕
on
在…上面；在（星期、日期）prep.
There is a cup on the table.
桌上有一個杯子。
🚪
out
從…出去prep.
The cat ran out of the room.
貓跑出房間。
🌳
outside
在…外面prep.
The kids are playing outside the house.
孩子們在屋子外面玩。

單字 29–32

✈️
over
在…正上方；越過；超過prep.
The plane flew over the mountain.
飛機飛過山的上空。
📆
since
自從prep.
I have lived here since 2020.
我從 2020 年就住在這裡。
📏
than
比prep.
Tom is taller than his father.
湯姆比他爸爸高。
🚇
through
穿過；通過prep.
The train went through the tunnel.
火車穿過隧道。

單字 33–36

➡️
to
往；到prep.
I go to school by bus.
我搭公車上學。
🐕
under
在…下面prep.
The dog is sleeping under the table.
狗在桌子底下睡覺。
⌛
until
直到prep.
I waited for you until ten o'clock.
我等你等到十點。
⤴️
up
沿著…往上prep.
We climbed up the hill.
我們爬上小山。

單字 37–38

🤝
with
和…一起；用prep.
I went to the movies with my friends.
我和朋友去看電影。
🐟
without
沒有prep.
Fish can't live without water.
魚沒有水不能活。

位置

介系詞意思例
on／in／under在上面（碰到）／在裡面／在下面on the desk、in the box、under the bed
above／below在上方／下方（沒碰到）above the clouds、below the window
over正上方；越過a bridge over the river
beside／near旁邊／附近beside me、near the station
between… and…在兩者之間between Tom and Amy
behind／in front of後面／前面behind the door
across from在…對面across from the bank

時間：at、on、in

介系詞用在例
at幾點、一個時間點at 6:30、at noon、at night
on星期、日期、特定某天on Sunday、on May 5、on my birthday
in月、年、季節、早上下午in July、in 2026、in summer、in the morning
時間的長短：for three days（長達三天）、since Monday（從星期一起）、until ten（直到十點）、during the summer（在夏天期間）。

方向

介系詞意思例
into／out of進入／從…出來walk into the room
up／down往上／往下run up the stairs
across橫越（平面）swim across the river
through穿過（立體）walk through the park
along沿著walk along the beach
around繞著run around the lake
