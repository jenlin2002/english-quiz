Other adverbs
36 個單字聽・唸・用

本單元單字一覽

頻率副詞（always、usually、often、sometimes、seldom、never）和其他常用副詞，共 36 個。頻率副詞放在 be 動詞後面、一般動詞前面。

alwayseverneveroftenseldomsometimesusuallyactuallyagainalsoawaytooalmostevenfinallyjustleastmaybeperhapsprobablyreallysostillthentogethertwiceveryquiteyetabroadaheadsomewhereeithernonotyes
每個字都有 🔊，先聽發音、跟著唸，再看例句。

單字 1–4

🔁
always
總是adv.
Lily always wears red shoes.
Lily 總是穿紅色的鞋子。
❔
ever
曾經adv.
Have you ever been to Japan?
你曾經去過日本嗎？
🚫
never
從不；絕不adv.
Jay is never late for school.
Jay 上學從不遲到。
🔂
often
時常adv.
How often do you go to the movies?
你多常去看電影？

單字 5–8

🤏
seldom
很少；不常adv.
My grandpa seldom watches TV.
我爺爺很少看電視。
🌗
sometimes
有時候adv.
I sometimes go swimming on weekends.
我週末有時候會去游泳。
🚶
usually
通常adv.
He usually walks to school.
他通常走路上學。
💡
actually
實際上；事實上adv.
He looks scary, but actually he is very kind.
他看起來很兇，但其實人很好。

單字 9–12

🔄
again
再一次adv.
Can you say that again?
你可以再說一次嗎？
➕
also
也adv.
I like cats. I also like dogs.
我喜歡貓，我也喜歡狗。
🏃
away
遠離地；離開adv.
The school is far away from my house.
學校離我家很遠。
✌️
too
也；太adv.
Your pen is blue, and mine is, too.
你的筆是藍色的，我的也是。

單字 13–16

🫣
almost
幾乎；差一點adv.
I almost missed the train.
我差點錯過火車。
😯
even
甚至；連adv.
He lied to everyone, even his family.
他對所有人說謊，甚至是他的家人。
🏁
finally
最後；終於adv.
Finally, I passed the exam.
我終於通過考試了。
⏱️
just
正好；僅僅；剛剛adv.
I just finished my homework.
我剛寫完功課。

單字 17–20

📉
least
最少；最小adv.
You should sleep at least eight hours.
你應該至少睡八個小時。
🤷
maybe
也許adv.
Maybe it will rain tomorrow.
也許明天會下雨。
🤔
perhaps
也許adv.
Perhaps she is on her way.
也許她已經在路上了。
📊
probably
大概；很可能adv.
He will probably come tonight.
他今晚很可能會來。

單字 21–24

💯
really
真正地；非常adv.
I really like this song.
我真的很喜歡這首歌。
😍
so
如此地；非常adv.
Snow White is so kind!
白雪公主真善良！
⏳
still
仍然adv.
Are you still hungry?
你還餓嗎？
➡️
then
然後；當時adv.
He did his homework, and then he went to bed.
他寫完功課，然後去睡覺。

單字 25–28

👨‍👩‍👧
together
一起adv.
Let's go shopping together.
我們一起去逛街吧。
✌️
twice
兩次；兩倍adv.
I visit my grandparents twice a month.
我一個月去看爺爺奶奶兩次。
‼️
very
非常adv.
The movie was very funny.
這部電影非常好笑。
📏
quite
相當adv.
He is quite tall.
他相當高。

單字 29–32

⌛
yet
尚未；已經（問句）adv.
I haven't finished my homework yet.
我還沒寫完功課。
✈️
abroad
在國外；到國外adv.
She wants to study abroad.
她想出國留學。
⏩
ahead
在前方；提前adv.
The bus stop is just ahead.
公車站就在前面。
📍
somewhere
某處adv.
My keys must be somewhere in this room.
我的鑰匙一定在這個房間的某個地方。

單字 33–36

🙅
either
也（否定句）adv.
I don't like fish. My sister doesn't, either.
我不喜歡魚，我妹妹也不喜歡。
❌
no
不；沒有adv.
No, I'm not tired.
不，我不累。
🚫
not
不adv.
Snoopy is a dog, not a cat.
史努比是狗，不是貓。
✅
yes
是的adv.
Yes, I love English.
是的，我愛英文。

頻率副詞

副詞頻率例句
always100%I always get up at six.
usually80%He usually walks to school.
often60%We often play basketball.
sometimes40%She sometimes cooks dinner.
seldom10%Grandpa seldom watches TV.
never0%Jay is never late.
位置：be 動詞、助動詞後面，一般動詞前面。問頻率用 How often…?

「也」的三種說法

副詞用在例句
also肯定句，放動詞前I also like dogs.
too肯定句，放句尾I like dogs, too.
either否定句，放句尾I don't like fish, either.

也許、大概

副詞確定程度例句
maybe一般Maybe it will rain.
perhaps一般（較正式）Perhaps he is busy.
probably比較確定He will probably come.
maybe（一個字，副詞）和 may be（助動詞＋be）不一樣：He may be a lawyer. ＝ Maybe he is a lawyer.
