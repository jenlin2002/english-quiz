FOCUS

感官動詞＋形容詞

動詞意思例句
look看起來You look tired.
sound聽起來That sounds great.
smell聞起來The soup smells good.
taste嚐起來The cake tastes sweet.
feel感覺；摸起來I feel happy.
後面接形容詞，不接副詞：It tastes good.（不是 well）；接名詞要加 like：She looks like her mother.
FOCUS

看和聽

動詞意思
look at注意看（靜態）
watch觀看（動態：電視、比賽）
see看見（自然看到）
listen to注意聽
hear聽見（自然聽到）
FOCUS

後面接 to V 還是 V-ing

接法動詞例句
V-ingfinish、mindI finished reading the book.
to Vwant、hope、needI want to go home.
兩種都可以like、love、hateI like swimming. ＝ I like to swim.
意思不同forget／rememberforget to V 忘了要做；forget V-ing 忘了做過
