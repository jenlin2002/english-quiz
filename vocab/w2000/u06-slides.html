UNIT 6 · VOCABULARY

家庭

Family
20 個單字聽・唸・用

本單元單字一覽

家人、親戚的稱呼，以及出生、長大、結婚的說法，共 20 個。注意男女配對，以及 be born、grow up 的用法。

auntbrothercousindaughterfamilyfathergrandfathergrandmotherhusbandmotherparentrelativesistersonunclewifeborngrowlivemarried
每個字都有 🔊，先聽發音、跟著唸，再看例句。

單字 1–4

aunt
姑姑；阿姨；伯母n.
My aunt lives in Kaohsiung.
我阿姨住在高雄。
brother
兄弟n.
My brother is two years older than me.
我哥哥比我大兩歲。
cousin
堂（表）兄弟姊妹n.
My cousin and I go fishing on weekends.
我和表哥週末會去釣魚。
daughter
女兒n.
Mr. Lin has two daughters.
林先生有兩個女兒。

單字 5–8

family
家庭；家人n.
There are four people in my family.
我家有四個人。
father
父親n.
My father cooks breakfast every morning.
我爸爸每天早上做早餐。
grandfather
祖父；外公n.
My grandfather tells me old stories.
爺爺跟我說以前的故事。
grandmother
祖母；外婆n.
Grandma makes the best dumplings.
奶奶包的水餃最好吃。

單字 9–12

husband
丈夫n.
Her husband often washes the dishes.
她先生常常洗碗。
mother
母親n.
My mother reads with me every night.
媽媽每天晚上陪我看書。
parent
父親或母親n.
My parents take good care of me.
我爸媽把我照顧得很好。
relative
親戚n.
We visit our relatives at Chinese New Year.
過年時我們會去拜訪親戚。

單字 13–16

sister
姊妹n.
My sister and I share a room.
我和妹妹睡同一間房間。
son
兒子n.
Their son is in junior high school.
他們的兒子在念國中。
uncle
叔叔；舅舅；伯父n.
My uncle teaches me to play the guitar.
叔叔教我彈吉他。
wife
妻子n.
He and his wife ride bikes on weekends.
他和太太週末會一起騎腳踏車。

單字 17–20

born
出生v.
I was born in Taipei.
我在台北出生。
grow
長大；成長；種植v.
Plants grow fast in summer.
植物在夏天長得很快。
live
住；生活v.
We live in a small apartment.
我們住在一間小公寓。
married
已婚的adj.
My aunt got married last year.
我阿姨去年結婚了。

家人的稱呼（男／女）

男女合稱
father 父親mother 母親parents 父母
son 兒子daughter 女兒children 孩子
brother 兄弟sister 姊妹siblings 兄弟姊妹
husband 丈夫wife 妻子couple 夫妻
grandfather 祖父grandmother 祖母grandparents 祖父母
uncle 叔叔aunt 阿姨relatives 親戚
cousin 不分男女，堂兄弟姊妹、表兄弟姊妹都叫 cousin。英文不分父系或母系：爸爸的兄弟、媽媽的兄弟都是 uncle。

出生、長大、結婚怎麼說？

英文中文
I was born in 2012.我在 2012 年出生。
I was born in Taipei.我在台北出生。
I want to be a doctor when I grow up.我長大想當醫生。
I live with my grandparents.我和祖父母一起住。
My aunt got married last year.我阿姨去年結婚了。
Is your brother married?你哥哥結婚了嗎？
「出生」一定說 be born：I was born…（不是 I born…）。grow 的變化 grow-grew-grown；grow up 長大。
