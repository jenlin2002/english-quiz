WRITING · UNIT 2

My Pet 我的寵物

介紹你的寵物；沒有養寵物的話，就寫你想養的寵物。
① 說說看② 字詞庫③ 句型練習④ 造句練習⑤ 寫短文
STEP 1 · LISTEN & SPEAK

① 先聽，再說說看

先聽一段對話，再回答下面的問題：按 🎤 用說的（會變成文字），也可以直接打字。說出來的想法，等一下寫短文用得到。

Amy Is that your dog in the photo?
Ben Yes! His name is Lucky. He is three years old.
Amy He's so cute! What does he like to do?
Ben He loves to run in the park and catch balls.
Amy Who takes care of him?
Ben I feed him every morning, and my brother walks him after dinner.
Amy I want a pet, too. Maybe a cat.
Ben Cats are great! They are quiet and clean.
  1. Do you have a pet? What is it?
    你有養寵物嗎？是什麼？
    💡 例：Yes, I have a dog. / No, but I want a cat.
  2. What does it look like?
    牠長什麼樣子？
    💡 例：It is small and white. It has big eyes.
  3. What does it like to do?
    牠喜歡做什麼？
    💡 例：It likes to sleep. / It likes to play with a ball.
  4. How do you take care of it?
    你怎麼照顧牠？
    💡 例：I feed it every day. / I walk it after school.
STEP 2 · WORD BANK

② 這個主題的字詞庫

寫短文時至少要用到幾個這裡的字詞（★ 2 個、★★ 3 個）。按 ☆ 可以存成字卡。

pet 寵物
Do you have a pet at home?
你家有養寵物嗎？
cute 可愛的
The little rabbit is so cute.
那隻小兔子好可愛。
fluffy 毛茸茸的
My cat has fluffy white fur.
我的貓有毛茸茸的白毛。
tail 尾巴
The dog is wagging its tail.
那隻狗在搖尾巴。
feed 餵
I feed my fish twice a day.
我一天餵魚兩次。
walk the dog 遛狗
Dad walks the dog after dinner.
爸爸晚餐後去遛狗。
take care of 照顧
I take care of my hamster every day.
我每天照顧我的倉鼠。
play with 和…玩
My cat likes to play with a ball.
我的貓喜歡玩球。
smart 聰明的
Parrots are smart birds.
鸚鵡是很聰明的鳥。
friendly 友善的
Our dog is friendly to everyone.
我們的狗對每個人都很友善。
noisy 吵鬧的
The puppy is noisy at night.
小狗晚上很吵。
give … a bath 幫…洗澡
We give our dog a bath every Sunday.
我們每個星期天幫狗洗澡。
vet 獸醫
We took our cat to the vet.
我們帶貓去看獸醫。
best friend 最好的朋友
My dog is my best friend.
我的狗是我最好的朋友。
STEP 3 · SENTENCE FRAMES

③ 句型練習：填空造句

用自己的情況把句子填完整（可以按 🎤 用說的）。想不出來就按「看例句」。這些句子可以直接放進你的短文裡。

  1. I have a . Its name is .
    我有一隻……，牠的名字是……（沒有寵物就寫：I want a ____.）
    I have a cat. Its name is Mimi.
  2. It is and .
    牠很……又很……
    It is small and fluffy.
  3. It has .
    牠有……（身體的特徵）
    It has big eyes and a short tail.
  4. It likes to .
    牠喜歡……
    It likes to sleep on my bed.
  5. Every day, I .
    每天我會……（怎麼照顧牠）
    Every day, I feed it and play with it.
  6. My pet is like my because .
    我的寵物像我的……，因為……
    My pet is like my best friend because it always makes me happy.
STEP 4 · GEPT SENTENCE WRITING

④ 造句練習（英檢題型）

全民英檢初級寫作第一部分的三種題型：句子合併、句子改寫、重組句子，共 15 題。要自己寫出完整的句子，送出後對答案、錯的要訂正，會計點數。
