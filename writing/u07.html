WRITING · UNIT 7

Going Shopping 去買東西

寫一次買東西的經驗：去哪裡、跟誰、想買什麼、價錢、最後買了沒有、心情如何。
① 說說看② 字詞庫③ 句型練習④ 造句練習⑤ 寫短文
STEP 1 · LISTEN & SPEAK

① 先聽，再說說看

先聽一段對話，再回答下面的問題：按 🎤 用說的（會變成文字），也可以直接打字。說出來的想法，等一下寫短文用得到。

Amy Excuse me. How much is this T-shirt?
Ben It's three hundred dollars. It's on sale today.
Amy Do you have it in a smaller size?
Ben Yes, here you are. The fitting room is over there.
Amy Thanks. Hmm, it fits well. Do you have it in blue?
Ben Sorry, we only have white and black.
Amy OK, I'll take the black one. Can I pay by card?
Ben Sure. Here's your receipt. Have a nice day!
  1. Where do you usually go shopping?
    你通常去哪裡買東西？
    💡 例：I usually go to the department store.
  2. What did you buy last time?
    你上次買了什麼？
    💡 例：I bought a pair of shoes.
  3. How much was it?
    多少錢？
    💡 例：It was eight hundred dollars.
  4. Do you like shopping? Why?
    你喜歡逛街嗎？為什麼？
    💡 例：Yes, because I like to look at new things.
STEP 2 · WORD BANK

② 這個主題的字詞庫

寫短文時至少要用到幾個這裡的字詞（★ 2 個、★★ 3 個）。按 ☆ 可以存成字卡。

go shopping 去購物
We go shopping on Saturdays.
我們星期六去購物。
How much 多少錢
How much is this cap?
這頂帽子多少錢？
cost 花費
The shoes cost one thousand dollars.
這雙鞋要一千元。
price 價格
The price is too high.
價格太高了。
cheap 便宜的
This bag is cheap.
這個包包很便宜。
expensive 貴的
That phone is too expensive.
那支手機太貴了。
on sale 特價
Everything is on sale today.
今天全部都在特價。
size 尺寸
Do you have a larger size?
有大一點的尺寸嗎？
try on 試穿
Can I try on these jeans?
我可以試穿這條牛仔褲嗎？
fit 合身
The jacket fits me well.
這件外套很合身。
pay 付錢
I paid in cash.
我付現金。
buy 買
I bought a gift for my mom.
我買了禮物給媽媽。
spend 花（錢）
I spent five hundred dollars.
我花了五百元。
department store 百貨公司
The department store is crowded.
百貨公司人很多。
STEP 3 · SENTENCE FRAMES

③ 句型練習：填空造句

用自己的情況把句子填完整（可以按 🎤 用說的）。想不出來就按「看例句」。這些句子可以直接放進你的短文裡。

  1. Last , I went shopping at .
    上個……我去……買東西
    Last Sunday, I went shopping at the mall.
  2. I went with .
    我跟……一起去
    I went with my mom and sister.
  3. I wanted to buy .
    我想買……
    I wanted to buy a new pair of sneakers.
  4. It cost dollars.
    它要……元
    It cost nine hundred dollars.
  5. It was , so I .
    它很……，所以我……
    It was on sale, so I bought two.
  6. I spent in total.
    我總共花了……
    I spent one thousand two hundred dollars in total.
STEP 4 · GEPT SENTENCE WRITING

④ 造句練習（英檢題型）

全民英檢初級寫作第一部分的三種題型：句子合併、句子改寫、重組句子，共 15 題。要自己寫出完整的句子，送出後對答案、錯的要訂正，會計點數。
