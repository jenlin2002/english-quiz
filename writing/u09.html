WRITING · UNIT 9

Holidays in Taiwan 臺灣的節日

向外國朋友介紹一個臺灣的節日：什麼時候、大家做什麼、吃什麼、你家怎麼過。
① 說說看② 字詞庫③ 句型練習④ 造句練習⑤ 寫短文
STEP 1 · LISTEN & SPEAK

① 先聽，再說說看

先聽一段對話，再回答下面的問題：按 🎤 用說的（會變成文字），也可以直接打字。說出來的想法，等一下寫短文用得到。

Amy What's the most important holiday in Taiwan?
Ben Chinese New Year. It's usually in January or February.
Amy What do people do?
Ben Families get together and eat a big dinner on New Year's Eve.
Amy Do children get presents?
Ben We get red envelopes with money inside. That's my favorite part!
Amy What other holidays do you have?
Ben The Moon Festival. We eat mooncakes and have barbecues with our neighbors.
  1. What is your favorite holiday?
    你最喜歡哪個節日？
    💡 例：My favorite holiday is Chinese New Year.
  2. When is it?
    是什麼時候？
    💡 例：It is in January or February.
  3. What do people eat?
    大家吃什麼？
    💡 例：People eat rice dumplings. / People eat mooncakes.
  4. What does your family do?
    你家做什麼？
    💡 例：We visit my grandparents and play cards.
STEP 2 · WORD BANK

② 這個主題的字詞庫

寫短文時至少要用到幾個這裡的字詞（★ 2 個、★★ 3 個）。按 ☆ 可以存成字卡。

holiday 節日；假日
Chinese New Year is a long holiday.
農曆新年是很長的假期。
festival 節慶
The Lantern Festival is very beautiful.
元宵節很漂亮。
Chinese New Year 農曆新年
We clean the house before Chinese New Year.
過年前我們大掃除。
New Year's Eve 除夕
We eat a big dinner on New Year's Eve.
除夕我們吃年夜飯。
red envelope 紅包
Grandma gave me a red envelope.
奶奶給我一個紅包。
lantern 燈籠
Children carry lanterns at night.
孩子們晚上提燈籠。
mooncake 月餅
Mooncakes are sweet and heavy.
月餅又甜又紮實。
rice dumpling 粽子
We eat rice dumplings at the Dragon Boat Festival.
端午節我們吃粽子。
dragon boat race 龍舟賽
We watched a dragon boat race.
我們看了龍舟賽。
get together 團聚
Families get together for dinner.
家人團聚吃晚餐。
fireworks 煙火
The fireworks were amazing.
煙火好壯觀。
tradition 傳統
It is a Taiwanese tradition.
這是臺灣的傳統。
wish 祝福；願望
We wish everyone good luck.
我們祝大家好運。
barbecue 烤肉
We have a barbecue at the Moon Festival.
中秋節我們烤肉。
STEP 3 · SENTENCE FRAMES

③ 句型練習：填空造句

用自己的情況把句子填完整（可以按 🎤 用說的）。想不出來就按「看例句」。這些句子可以直接放進你的短文裡。

  1. My favorite holiday is .
    我最喜歡的節日是……
    My favorite holiday is the Moon Festival.
  2. It is in .
    它在……（月份）
    It is in September or October.
  3. On this holiday, people .
    這個節日大家會……
    On this holiday, people look at the moon.
  4. We eat because .
    我們吃……，因為……
    We eat rice dumplings because it is a tradition.
  5. My family usually .
    我家通常……
    My family usually has a barbecue with our neighbors.
  6. I like this holiday because .
    我喜歡這個節日，因為……
    I like this holiday because I can see all my cousins.
