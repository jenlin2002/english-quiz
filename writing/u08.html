WRITING · UNIT 8

Asking the Way 問路

學會問路和報路：寫一段「從你家（或學校）到某個地方怎麼走」的說明，或寫一次問路的經驗。
① 說說看② 字詞庫③ 句型練習④ 造句練習⑤ 寫短文
STEP 1 · LISTEN & SPEAK

① 先聽，再說說看

先聽一段對話，再回答下面的問題：按 🎤 用說的（會變成文字），也可以直接打字。說出來的想法，等一下寫短文用得到。

Amy Excuse me. Could you tell me how to get to the library?
Ben Sure. Go straight down this street for two blocks.
Amy Two blocks. And then?
Ben Turn left at the traffic light. The library is on your right.
Amy Is it far from here?
Ben No, it's only about five minutes on foot.
Amy Is it next to the post office?
Ben Yes, it's between the post office and a small park. You can't miss it.
  1. How do you get to school?
    你怎麼去學校？
    💡 例：I walk to school. / I take the bus.
  2. What is near your home?
    你家附近有什麼？
    💡 例：There is a park and a convenience store.
  3. How do you get from your home to the nearest store?
    從你家到最近的店怎麼走？
    💡 例：Go out and turn right. It's on the corner.
  4. Did you ever get lost? What did you do?
    你迷過路嗎？怎麼辦？
    💡 例：Yes. I asked a police officer for help.
STEP 2 · WORD BANK

② 這個主題的字詞庫

寫短文時至少要用到幾個這裡的字詞（★ 2 個、★★ 3 個）。按 ☆ 可以存成字卡。

go straight 直走
Go straight for two blocks.
直走兩個路口。
turn left / right 左轉／右轉
Turn right at the bank.
在銀行右轉。
block 街區；路口
The park is three blocks away.
公園在三個路口外。
corner 轉角
There is a bakery on the corner.
轉角有一家麵包店。
traffic light 紅綠燈
Stop at the traffic light.
在紅綠燈停下來。
cross 穿越
Cross the street carefully.
小心過馬路。
next to 在…旁邊
The bank is next to the hospital.
銀行在醫院旁邊。
between … and … 在…和…之間
The shop is between the bank and the school.
店在銀行和學校中間。
across from 在…對面
The MRT station is across from the mall.
捷運站在購物中心對面。
on foot 走路
It takes ten minutes on foot.
走路要十分鐘。
far from 離…遠
My school isn't far from my home.
我的學校離我家不遠。
get lost 迷路
I got lost in the city.
我在城市裡迷路了。
map 地圖
Let's check the map on my phone.
我們看一下手機上的地圖。
How do I get to …? …怎麼去？
How do I get to the train station?
火車站怎麼去？
STEP 3 · SENTENCE FRAMES

③ 句型練習：填空造句

用自己的情況把句子填完整（可以按 🎤 用說的）。想不出來就按「看例句」。這些句子可以直接放進你的短文裡。

  1. Excuse me. How do I get to ?
    不好意思，……怎麼去？
    Excuse me. How do I get to the night market?
  2. Go straight for blocks.
    直走……個路口
    Go straight for two blocks.
  3. Turn at .
    在……左／右轉
    Turn left at the convenience store.
  4. It is next to / across from .
    它在……旁邊／對面
    It is across from the bank.
  5. It takes about minutes on foot.
    走路大約……分鐘
    It takes about ten minutes on foot.
  6. If you get lost, you can .
    如果迷路，你可以……
    If you get lost, you can ask a police officer.
STEP 4 · GEPT SENTENCE WRITING

④ 造句練習（英檢題型）
