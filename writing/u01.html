WRITING · UNIT 1

My Weekend 我的週末

週末做了什麼？先聽、先說，再一步一步寫成一篇短文。
① 說說看② 字詞庫③ 句型練習④ 造句練習⑤ 寫短文
STEP 1 · LISTEN & SPEAK

① 先聽，再說說看

先聽一段對話，再回答下面的問題：按 🎤 用說的（會變成文字），也可以直接打字。說出來的想法，等一下寫短文用得到。

Amy Hi, Ben! How was your weekend?
Ben It was great! I went hiking with my dad on Saturday.
Amy Wow! Where did you go?
Ben We went to Yangmingshan. The view was beautiful.
Amy That sounds fun. What did you do on Sunday?
Ben I stayed home and played video games with my sister. How about you?
Amy I helped my mom clean the house, and then we ate hot pot for dinner.
Ben Yum! That sounds like a nice weekend.
  1. What do you usually do on Saturday morning?
    你星期六早上通常做什麼？
    💡 例：I usually sleep late. / I usually play basketball.
  2. Who do you usually spend your weekend with?
    你週末通常和誰一起過？
    💡 例：I spend it with my family. / I spend it with my friends.
  3. What did you do last Sunday?
    你上星期天做了什麼？
    💡 例：I went to the park. / I watched a movie at home.
  4. How did you feel? Why?
    你覺得怎麼樣？為什麼？
    💡 例：I felt happy because I didn't have to go to school.
STEP 2 · WORD BANK

② 這個主題的字詞庫

寫短文時至少要用到幾個這裡的字詞（★ 2 個、★★ 3 個）。按 ☆ 可以存成字卡。

weekend 週末
What are you going to do this weekend?
你這個週末要做什麼？
usually 通常
I usually get up at nine on Sundays.
我星期天通常九點起床。
sleep late 睡晚一點
I like to sleep late on Saturdays.
我星期六喜歡睡晚一點。
go hiking 去健行
We went hiking in the mountains.
我們去山上健行。
go shopping 去購物
Mom and I went shopping at the mall.
我和媽媽去購物中心買東西。
visit my grandparents 拜訪祖父母
I visit my grandparents every Sunday.
我每個星期天都去看祖父母。
play video games 打電動
My brother played video games all afternoon.
我哥哥整個下午都在打電動。
watch a movie 看電影
We watched a funny movie at home.
我們在家看了一部好笑的電影。
clean my room 打掃房間
I cleaned my room on Saturday morning.
我星期六早上打掃了房間。
do my homework 寫功課
I did my homework before dinner.
我在晚餐前寫完了功課。
take a walk 散步
After dinner, we took a walk in the park.
晚餐後我們在公園散步。
eat out 外食；上館子
We ate out at a noodle shop.
我們去一家麵店吃飯。
relaxing 令人放鬆的
It was a relaxing weekend.
那是一個很放鬆的週末。
tired 累的
I was tired after the long hike.
走了那麼遠的路，我很累。
STEP 3 · SENTENCE FRAMES

③ 句型練習：填空造句

用自己的情況把句子填完整（可以按 🎤 用說的）。想不出來就按「看例句」。這些句子可以直接放進你的短文裡。

  1. On Saturday morning, I usually .
    星期六早上，我通常……
    On Saturday morning, I usually sleep late.
  2. I like weekends because .
    我喜歡週末，因為……
    I like weekends because I don't have to go to school.
  3. Last Sunday, I with .
    上星期天，我和……一起……
    Last Sunday, I went shopping with my mom.
  4. First, I . Then, I .
    首先我……，接著我……
    First, I did my homework. Then, I watched a movie.
  5. It was , but I felt .
    那天很……，但我覺得……
    It was a busy day, but I felt happy.
  6. Next weekend, I want to .
    下個週末，我想要……
    Next weekend, I want to visit my grandparents.
STEP 4 · GEPT SENTENCE WRITING

④ 造句練習（英檢題型）

全民英檢初級寫作第一部分的三種題型：句子合併、句子改寫、重組句子，共 15 題。要自己寫出完整的句子，送出後對答案、錯的要訂正，會計點數。
