WRITING · UNIT 13

Saying Sorry 寫道歉卡片

寫一張道歉卡片：說明你做錯了什麼、為什麼會這樣、對方的感受、你會怎麼補救、以後會怎麼做。
① 說說看② 字詞庫③ 句型練習④ 造句練習⑤ 寫短文
STEP 1 · LISTEN & SPEAK

① 先聽，再說說看

先聽一段對話，再回答下面的問題：按 🎤 用說的（會變成文字），也可以直接打字。說出來的想法，等一下寫短文用得到。

Amy Ben, you look upset. What's wrong?
Ben I borrowed my friend Leo's comic book, and I spilled juice on it.
Amy Oh no. Did you tell him?
Ben Not yet. I feel terrible. I don't know what to say.
Amy Why don't you write him a card? Say sorry and explain what happened.
Ben Good idea. I can also buy him a new one with my pocket money.
Amy That's a great way to fix it. He'll understand.
Ben Thanks, Amy. I'll be more careful next time.
  1. When did you last say sorry to someone?
    你上次跟人道歉是什麼時候？
    💡 例：Last week, I said sorry to my sister.
  2. What happened?
    發生了什麼事？
    💡 例：I broke her pencil case.
  3. How did the other person feel?
    對方覺得怎樣？
    💡 例：She was angry and sad.
  4. What did you do to make it better?
    你怎麼補救？
    💡 例：I bought her a new one. / I helped her clean up.
STEP 2 · WORD BANK

② 這個主題的字詞庫

寫短文時至少要用到幾個這裡的字詞（★ 2 個、★★ 3 個）。按 ☆ 可以存成字卡。

I'm sorry 對不起
I'm sorry for being late.
對不起我遲到了。
mistake 錯誤
It was my mistake.
那是我的錯。
break 打破；弄壞
I broke your cup by accident.
我不小心打破了你的杯子。
forget 忘記
I forgot your birthday.
我忘了你的生日。
by accident 不小心
I stepped on your foot by accident.
我不小心踩到你的腳。
upset 難過的；生氣的
You were upset, and I understand.
你很難過，我能理解。
feel terrible 覺得很糟
I feel terrible about it.
我對這件事覺得很過意不去。
should have 早該…
I should have told you the truth.
我早該告訴你實話。
promise 保證
I promise it won't happen again.
我保證不會再發生。
careful 小心的
I will be more careful.
我會更小心。
make it up to you 補償你
Let me make it up to you.
讓我補償你。
forgive 原諒
I hope you can forgive me.
希望你能原諒我。
friendship 友誼
Our friendship is important to me.
我們的友誼對我很重要。
next time 下一次
Next time, I will ask first.
下次我會先問。
STEP 3 · SENTENCE FRAMES

③ 句型練習：填空造句

用自己的情況把句子填完整（可以按 🎤 用說的）。想不出來就按「看例句」。這些句子可以直接放進你的短文裡。

  1. Dear ,
    親愛的……
    Dear Leo,
  2. I'm really sorry for .
    我真的很抱歉……
    I'm really sorry for spilling juice on your comic book.
  3. I didn't mean to .
    我不是故意要……
    I didn't mean to make you upset.
  4. I should have .
    我早該……
    I should have been more careful.
  5. To make it up to you, I will .
    為了補償你，我會……
    To make it up to you, I will buy you a new one.
  6. I promise I will next time.
    我保證下次會……
    I promise I will be more careful next time.
