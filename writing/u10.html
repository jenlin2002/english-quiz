WRITING · UNIT 10

A Trip 一次旅行

寫一次難忘的旅行：去哪裡、跟誰、怎麼去、看到什麼、吃了什麼、發生什麼特別的事。
① 說說看② 字詞庫③ 句型練習④ 造句練習⑤ 寫短文
STEP 1 · LISTEN & SPEAK

① 先聽，再說說看

先聽一段對話，再回答下面的問題：按 🎤 用說的（會變成文字），也可以直接打字。說出來的想法，等一下寫短文用得到。

Amy Where did you go during summer vacation?
Ben I went to Hualien with my family. We took the train.
Amy How long did you stay there?
Ben Three days. We stayed at a hotel near the beach.
Amy What did you do?
Ben We visited Taroko Gorge and rode bikes along the coast.
Amy Sounds amazing! Did anything special happen?
Ben Yes! We saw dolphins on a boat trip. I took lots of pictures.
  1. Where did you go on your last trip?
    你上次旅行去哪裡？
    💡 例：I went to Kenting.
  2. Who did you go with, and how did you get there?
    跟誰去？怎麼去？
    💡 例：I went with my family by car.
  3. What did you see or do there?
    在那裡看到什麼、做什麼？
    💡 例：We swam in the sea and visited a museum.
  4. What was the best part?
    最棒的是什麼？
    💡 例：The best part was the night market.
STEP 2 · WORD BANK

② 這個主題的字詞庫

寫短文時至少要用到幾個這裡的字詞（★ 2 個、★★ 3 個）。按 ☆ 可以存成字卡。

trip 旅行
We took a trip to Tainan.
我們去台南旅行。
take the train 搭火車
We took the train to Taitung.
我們搭火車去台東。
stay at 住在
We stayed at a hotel by the sea.
我們住在海邊的飯店。
visit 參觀；拜訪
We visited an old temple.
我們參觀了一座古廟。
scenery 風景
The scenery was beautiful.
風景很美。
take pictures 拍照
I took pictures of the mountains.
我拍了山的照片。
souvenir 紀念品
I bought a souvenir for my friend.
我買了紀念品給朋友。
local food 當地美食
We tried some local food.
我們吃了一些當地美食。
famous 有名的
This beach is famous for its sunset.
這個海灘以夕陽聞名。
excited 興奮的
I was excited before the trip.
旅行前我很興奮。
tired 累的
We were tired but happy.
我們很累但很開心。
unforgettable 難忘的
It was an unforgettable trip.
那是一次難忘的旅行。
first … then … finally 首先…然後…最後
First, we went to the beach. Finally, we went home.
首先我們去海邊，最後我們回家。
weather 天氣
The weather was sunny every day.
每天都是晴天。
STEP 3 · SENTENCE FRAMES

③ 句型練習：填空造句

用自己的情況把句子填完整（可以按 🎤 用說的）。想不出來就按「看例句」。這些句子可以直接放進你的短文裡。

  1. Last , I went to with .
    上個……我跟……去了……
    Last summer, I went to Hualien with my family.
  2. We got there by .
    我們搭……去
    We got there by train.
  3. We stayed at for days.
    我們住在……，住了……天
    We stayed at a hotel for three days.
  4. First, we . Then, we .
    首先我們……，然後我們……
    First, we visited a temple. Then, we ate local food.
  5. The best part was .
    最棒的是……
    The best part was the boat trip.
  6. I hope I can again.
    我希望能再……
    I hope I can go there again next year.
STEP 4 · GEPT SENTENCE WRITING

④ 造句練習（英檢題型）
