WRITING · UNIT 3

My Family 我的家人

介紹你的家人：有誰、做什麼工作、長什麼樣子、你們一起做什麼。
① 說說看② 字詞庫③ 句型練習④ 造句練習⑤ 寫短文
STEP 1 · LISTEN & SPEAK

① 先聽，再說說看

先聽一段對話，再回答下面的問題：按 🎤 用說的（會變成文字），也可以直接打字。說出來的想法，等一下寫短文用得到。

Amy Is this a picture of your family?
Ben Yes. There are five people in my family.
Amy Who is the tall man?
Ben That's my dad. He is a bus driver. He is funny and kind.
Amy And the woman next to him?
Ben That's my mom. She works at a bank. She is a great cook, too.
Amy What do you do together on weekends?
Ben We usually go hiking or visit my grandparents in Tainan.
  1. How many people are there in your family?
    你家有幾個人？
    💡 例：There are four people in my family.
  2. Who are they?
    他們是誰？
    💡 例：My dad, my mom, my sister, and me.
  3. What does your dad or mom do?
    你爸爸或媽媽做什麼工作？
    💡 例：My mom is a nurse. / My dad works in an office.
  4. What do you do together?
    你們一起做什麼？
    💡 例：We often eat dinner together and watch TV.
STEP 2 · WORD BANK

② 這個主題的字詞庫

寫短文時至少要用到幾個這裡的字詞（★ 2 個、★★ 3 個）。按 ☆ 可以存成字卡。

family 家人；家庭
I love my family very much.
我很愛我的家人。
parents 父母
My parents both work in Taipei.
我爸媽都在台北工作。
grandparents 祖父母
I visit my grandparents every month.
我每個月去看祖父母。
older brother 哥哥
My older brother is in high school.
我哥哥讀高中。
only child 獨生子／女
I am an only child.
我是獨生子。
kind 親切的
My grandma is kind to everyone.
我奶奶對每個人都很親切。
funny 好笑的；風趣的
My uncle is very funny.
我叔叔很風趣。
hard-working 努力的
My mom is hard-working.
我媽媽工作很努力。
look like 長得像
I look like my dad.
我長得像我爸爸。
be good at 擅長
My sister is good at drawing.
我姊姊很會畫畫。
work as 擔任…工作
My aunt works as a teacher.
我阿姨是當老師。
spend time with 和…共度時光
I like to spend time with my family.
我喜歡和家人在一起。
take care of 照顧
My grandpa takes care of me after school.
放學後我爺爺照顧我。
get along with 和…處得好
I get along well with my sister.
我和我妹妹處得很好。
STEP 3 · SENTENCE FRAMES

③ 句型練習：填空造句

用自己的情況把句子填完整（可以按 🎤 用說的）。想不出來就按「看例句」。這些句子可以直接放進你的短文裡。

  1. There are people in my family.
    我家有……個人
    There are four people in my family.
  2. My is a .
    我的……是……（職業）
    My mom is a nurse.
  3. He / She is and .
    他／她很……又很……（個性）
    She is kind and hard-working.
  4. I look like my .
    我長得像我的……
    I look like my dad.
  5. My is good at .
    我的……很會……
    My sister is good at singing.
  6. On weekends, we often .
    週末我們常常……
    On weekends, we often go to the night market.
STEP 4 · GEPT SENTENCE WRITING

④ 造句練習（英檢題型）

全民英檢初級寫作第一部分的三種題型：句子合併、句子改寫、重組句子，共 15 題。要自己寫出完整的句子，送出後對答案、錯的要訂正，會計點數。
