My Favorite Food 我最喜歡的食物

介紹你最喜歡的食物：它是什麼、吃起來怎樣、在哪裡吃、跟誰吃、為什麼喜歡。
① 說說看② 字詞庫③ 句型練習④ 造句練習⑤ 寫短文
STEP 1 · LISTEN & SPEAK

① 先聽，再說說看

先聽一段對話，再回答下面的問題：按 🎤 用說的（會變成文字），也可以直接打字。說出來的想法，等一下寫短文用得到。

Amy What's your favorite food?
Ben Beef noodles! My grandpa makes the best beef noodles.
Amy What does it taste like?
Ben The soup is rich and a little spicy, and the beef is soft.
Amy Sounds delicious. How often do you eat it?
Ben About once a week. What about you?
Amy I love mango shaved ice. It's sweet and cold.
Ben That's perfect for hot summer days!
  1. What is your favorite food?
    你最喜歡的食物是什麼？
    💡 例：My favorite food is fried rice.
  2. What does it taste like?
    吃起來怎麼樣？
    💡 例：It is salty and a little sweet.
  3. Where and with whom do you eat it?
    在哪裡、跟誰吃？
    💡 例：I eat it at home with my family.
  4. Why do you like it?
    為什麼喜歡？
    💡 例：Because it is cheap and delicious.
STEP 2 · WORD BANK

② 這個主題的字詞庫

寫短文時至少要用到幾個這裡的字詞（★ 2 個、★★ 3 個）。按 ☆ 可以存成字卡。

favorite 最喜歡的
Dumplings are my favorite food.
餃子是我最喜歡的食物。
delicious 美味的
This cake is delicious.
這個蛋糕很好吃。
taste 嚐起來
The soup tastes a little salty.
這湯嚐起來有點鹹。
sweet 甜的
Bubble tea is too sweet for me.
珍奶對我來說太甜了。
spicy 辣的
I can't eat spicy food.
我不能吃辣。
sour 酸的
Lemons are sour.
檸檬是酸的。
salty 鹹的
The fries are salty.
薯條很鹹。
smell 聞起來
The bread smells good.
麵包聞起來很香。
night market 夜市
We eat fried chicken at the night market.
我們在夜市吃炸雞。
restaurant 餐廳
There is a good noodle restaurant near my home.
我家附近有一家好吃的麵店。
cook 煮；廚師
My dad cooks dinner on Sundays.
我爸爸星期天煮晚餐。
healthy 健康的
Vegetables are healthy.
蔬菜很健康。
be full 吃飽的
I can't eat any more. I'm full.
我吃不下了，我飽了。
once a week 一週一次
I eat hot pot once a week.
我一週吃一次火鍋。
STEP 3 · SENTENCE FRAMES

③ 句型練習：填空造句

用自己的情況把句子填完整（可以按 🎤 用說的）。想不出來就按「看例句」。這些句子可以直接放進你的短文裡。

  1. My favorite food is .
    我最喜歡的食物是……
    My favorite food is beef noodles.
  2. It tastes and .
    它吃起來……又……
    It tastes sweet and sour.
  3. I usually eat it at with .
    我通常在……跟……一起吃
    I usually eat it at the night market with my friends.
  4. I eat it about .
    我大約多久吃一次
    I eat it about once a week.
  5. I like it because .
    我喜歡它是因為……
    I like it because it reminds me of my grandma.
  6. If you come to Taiwan, you should try .
    如果你來台灣，你應該試試……
    If you come to Taiwan, you should try stinky tofu.
STEP 4 · GEPT SENTENCE WRITING

④ 造句練習（英檢題型）
