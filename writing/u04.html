WRITING · UNIT 4

A Day at School 學校的一天

照時間順序寫你在學校的一天：幾點到校、上什麼課、午餐和下課做什麼、放學後去哪。
① 說說看② 字詞庫③ 句型練習④ 造句練習⑤ 寫短文
STEP 1 · LISTEN & SPEAK

① 先聽，再說說看

先聽一段對話，再回答下面的問題：按 🎤 用說的（會變成文字），也可以直接打字。說出來的想法，等一下寫短文用得到。

Amy What time do you get to school every day?
Ben At seven thirty. My first class starts at eight.
Amy What's your favorite class?
Ben P.E.! We play basketball on Tuesdays. What about you?
Amy I like art. I'm not good at math, though.
Ben What do you do at lunchtime?
Amy I eat lunch with my classmates, and then I take a short nap.
Ben Me too. After school, I go to the library to do my homework.
  1. What time do you get to school?
    你幾點到學校？
    💡 例：I get to school at seven forty.
  2. What is your favorite subject? Why?
    你最喜歡哪一科？為什麼？
    💡 例：I like English because it is fun.
  3. What do you do at lunchtime?
    你午餐時間做什麼？
    💡 例：I eat lunch and then take a nap.
  4. What do you do after school?
    你放學後做什麼？
    💡 例：I go to cram school. / I play basketball.
STEP 2 · WORD BANK

② 這個主題的字詞庫

寫短文時至少要用到幾個這裡的字詞（★ 2 個、★★ 3 個）。按 ☆ 可以存成字卡。

get to school 到學校
I get to school at seven thirty.
我七點半到學校。
class 課；班級
We have seven classes a day.
我們一天有七堂課。
subject 科目
Science is my favorite subject.
自然是我最喜歡的科目。
classmate 同學
My classmates are friendly.
我的同學都很友善。
break 下課時間
We play ball during the break.
我們下課時打球。
lunchtime 午餐時間
At lunchtime, I eat with my friends.
午餐時間我和朋友一起吃飯。
take a nap 午睡
We take a nap after lunch.
我們午餐後午睡。
P.E. class 體育課
We run a lot in P.E. class.
體育課我們跑很多。
homework 功課
I do my homework after dinner.
我晚餐後寫功課。
quiz 小考
We have an English quiz today.
我們今天有英文小考。
clean the classroom 打掃教室
We clean the classroom at four.
我們四點打掃教室。
club 社團
I am in the science club.
我參加自然科學社。
first … then … 先……然後……
First, I wash my hands. Then, I eat lunch.
我先洗手，然後吃午餐。
after school 放學後
I go to cram school after school.
我放學後去補習班。
STEP 3 · SENTENCE FRAMES

③ 句型練習：填空造句

用自己的情況把句子填完整（可以按 🎤 用說的）。想不出來就按「看例句」。這些句子可以直接放進你的短文裡。

  1. I get to school at .
    我……點到學校
    I get to school at seven thirty.
  2. My first class is .
    我第一堂課是……
    My first class is math.
  3. My favorite subject is because .
    我最喜歡的科目是……，因為……
    My favorite subject is science because I like doing experiments.
  4. During the break, I .
    下課時我……
    During the break, I chat with my friends.
  5. After lunch, we .
    午餐後我們……
    After lunch, we take a nap.
  6. After school, I .
    放學後我……
    After school, I go to the library.
STEP 4 · GEPT SENTENCE WRITING
