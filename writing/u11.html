WRITING · UNIT 11

My Dream 我的夢想

寫你的夢想：長大想做什麼工作、想去哪裡、想完成什麼事，為什麼，以及你現在怎麼努力。
① 說說看② 字詞庫③ 句型練習④ 造句練習⑤ 寫短文
STEP 1 · LISTEN & SPEAK

① 先聽，再說說看

先聽一段對話，再回答下面的問題：按 🎤 用說的（會變成文字），也可以直接打字。說出來的想法，等一下寫短文用得到。

Amy What do you want to be in the future?
Ben I want to be a game designer.
Amy Why?
Ben Because I love games, and I want to make people happy.
Amy What are you doing now to reach your dream?
Ben I'm learning to code, and I draw characters in my free time.
Amy That's cool. My dream is to be a doctor and help sick children.
Ben You're good at science, so I'm sure you can do it!
  1. What do you want to be in the future?
    你將來想做什麼？
    💡 例：I want to be a teacher.
  2. Why do you want to do that?
    為什麼？
    💡 例：Because I like to help others.
  3. What do you need to learn?
    你需要學什麼？
    💡 例：I need to learn English and science.
  4. What are you doing now for your dream?
    你現在為夢想做什麼？
    💡 例：I study hard and read a lot of books.
STEP 2 · WORD BANK

② 這個主題的字詞庫

寫短文時至少要用到幾個這裡的字詞（★ 2 個、★★ 3 個）。按 ☆ 可以存成字卡。

dream 夢想
My dream is to travel around the world.
我的夢想是環遊世界。
future 未來
I want to be a pilot in the future.
我將來想當飛行員。
want to be 想成為
She wants to be a singer.
她想當歌手。
hope 希望
I hope to live by the sea.
我希望住在海邊。
job 工作
Being a nurse is a hard job.
當護理師是很辛苦的工作。
help people 幫助人
Doctors help people every day.
醫生每天幫助人。
practice 練習
I practice the piano every day.
我每天練鋼琴。
study hard 用功讀書
I need to study hard.
我需要用功讀書。
skill 技能
Drawing is an important skill for designers.
畫圖是設計師很重要的技能。
reach my dream 實現夢想
I will work hard to reach my dream.
我會努力實現我的夢想。
interested in 對…有興趣
I am interested in animals.
我對動物有興趣。
someday 有一天
Someday I will open my own café.
有一天我會開自己的咖啡店。
give up 放棄
Never give up on your dream.
永遠不要放棄你的夢想。
because 因為
I want to be a vet because I love animals.
我想當獸醫，因為我愛動物。
STEP 3 · SENTENCE FRAMES

③ 句型練習：填空造句

用自己的情況把句子填完整（可以按 🎤 用說的）。想不出來就按「看例句」。這些句子可以直接放進你的短文裡。

  1. My dream is to .
    我的夢想是……
    My dream is to be a vet.
  2. I want to be a because .
    我想當……，因為……
    I want to be a chef because I love cooking.
  3. I am interested in .
    我對……有興趣
    I am interested in computers.
  4. To reach my dream, I need to .
    為了實現夢想，我需要……
    To reach my dream, I need to study science.
  5. Now, I every day.
    現在我每天……
    Now, I practice drawing every day.
  6. Someday, I will .
    有一天我會……
    Someday, I will open my own animal hospital.
