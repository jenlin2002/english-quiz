WRITING · UNIT 6

A Birthday Party 生日派對

寫一場你參加過或辦過的生日派對：誰的生日、在哪裡、有誰來、做了什麼、收到或送了什麼禮物。
① 說說看② 字詞庫③ 句型練習④ 造句練習⑤ 寫短文
STEP 1 · LISTEN & SPEAK

① 先聽，再說說看

先聽一段對話，再回答下面的問題：按 🎤 用說的（會變成文字），也可以直接打字。說出來的想法，等一下寫短文用得到。

Amy How was your birthday party last Saturday?
Ben It was great! We had it at a pizza restaurant.
Amy Who came?
Ben Eight of my friends and my cousins. We played games and sang songs.
Amy Did you get any nice presents?
Ben Yes! My parents gave me a new basketball, and my friends gave me some comic books.
Amy What kind of cake did you have?
Ben A chocolate cake with thirteen candles. I made a wish and blew them out.
  1. When is your birthday?
    你的生日是什麼時候？
    💡 例：My birthday is on May 3rd.
  2. How did you celebrate your last birthday?
    你上次生日怎麼慶祝？
    💡 例：I had dinner with my family.
  3. What presents did you get?
    你收到什麼禮物？
    💡 例：I got a new watch.
  4. What did you wish for?
    你許了什麼願？
    💡 例：I wished for good grades. / It's a secret!
STEP 2 · WORD BANK

② 這個主題的字詞庫

寫短文時至少要用到幾個這裡的字詞（★ 2 個、★★ 3 個）。按 ☆ 可以存成字卡。

birthday party 生日派對
I went to Amy's birthday party.
我去了 Amy 的生日派對。
celebrate 慶祝
We celebrated at home.
我們在家慶祝。
invite 邀請
She invited ten friends.
她邀請了十個朋友。
guest 客人
The guests arrived at six.
客人六點到。
present 禮物
I got a lot of presents.
我收到很多禮物。
candle 蠟燭
There were twelve candles on the cake.
蛋糕上有十二根蠟燭。
make a wish 許願
Close your eyes and make a wish.
閉上眼睛許個願。
blow out 吹熄
He blew out the candles.
他吹熄了蠟燭。
decorate 裝飾
We decorated the room with balloons.
我們用氣球裝飾房間。
balloon 氣球
There were colorful balloons everywhere.
到處都是彩色氣球。
surprise 驚喜
The party was a surprise for my mom.
這個派對是給我媽媽的驚喜。
have fun 玩得開心
Everyone had fun at the party.
大家在派對上都玩得很開心。
sing 唱歌
We sang "Happy Birthday" together.
我們一起唱生日快樂歌。
wrap 包裝
I wrapped the present in red paper.
我用紅色的紙包禮物。
STEP 3 · SENTENCE FRAMES

③ 句型練習：填空造句

用自己的情況把句子填完整（可以按 🎤 用說的）。想不出來就按「看例句」。這些句子可以直接放進你的短文裡。

  1. Last , I went to 's birthday party.
    上個……，我去了……的生日派對
    Last Sunday, I went to my cousin's birthday party.
  2. The party was at .
    派對在……舉行
    The party was at her house.
  3. We and .
    我們……還有……
    We played games and ate pizza.
  4. I gave him / her .
    我送他／她……
    I gave her a storybook.
  5. The cake was .
    蛋糕是……的
    The cake was big and full of strawberries.
  6. It was a day because .
    那是……的一天，因為……
    It was a happy day because all my friends were there.
