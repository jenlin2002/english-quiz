WRITING · UNIT 12

An Invitation Card 寫邀請卡

寫一張邀請卡：邀請朋友參加你的活動（派對、球賽、表演、烤肉），寫清楚什麼活動、時間、地點、要帶什麼、怎麼回覆。
① 說說看② 字詞庫③ 句型練習④ 造句練習⑤ 寫短文
STEP 1 · LISTEN & SPEAK

① 先聽，再說說看

先聽一段對話，再回答下面的問題：按 🎤 用說的（會變成文字），也可以直接打字。說出來的想法，等一下寫短文用得到。

Amy I'm having a barbecue party next Saturday. Would you like to come?
Ben I'd love to! What time does it start?
Amy At five in the afternoon. It's in my backyard.
Ben Should I bring anything?
Amy You can bring some drinks or snacks if you want.
Ben Sure. Can I bring my little sister?
Amy Of course! Please tell me by Wednesday, so I can buy enough food.
Ben OK. Thanks for inviting me!
  1. What event do you want to invite friends to?
    你想邀請朋友參加什麼活動？
    💡 例：My birthday party. / A basketball game.
  2. When and where is it?
    什麼時候、在哪裡？
    💡 例：It is on Sunday at three, at my home.
  3. What should your friends bring?
    朋友要帶什麼？
    💡 例：They can bring a swimsuit.
  4. How should they answer you?
    他們要怎麼回覆你？
    💡 例：Please call me or send me a message.
STEP 2 · WORD BANK

② 這個主題的字詞庫

寫短文時至少要用到幾個這裡的字詞（★ 2 個、★★ 3 個）。按 ☆ 可以存成字卡。

invite 邀請
I want to invite you to my party.
我想邀請你來我的派對。
Would you like to …? 你想要…嗎？
Would you like to come to my concert?
你想來我的音樂會嗎？
Please come to … 請來參加…
Please come to my birthday party.
請來參加我的生日派對。
date 日期
Date: Saturday, May 10
日期：5 月 10 日星期六
time 時間
Time: 2:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.
時間：下午兩點到五點
place 地點
Place: My home, 25 Park Road
地點：我家，公園路 25 號
bring 帶來
Please bring your swimsuit.
請帶泳衣。
celebrate 慶祝
Let's celebrate together!
我們一起慶祝吧！
let me know 讓我知道
Please let me know if you can come.
能不能來請讓我知道。
by (+ 日期) 在…之前
Please reply by Friday.
請在星期五前回覆。
see you there 到時見
See you there!
到時見！
fun 有趣的；樂趣
It will be a lot of fun.
一定會很好玩。
dress up 打扮
Please dress up as a movie character.
請打扮成電影角色。
Dear … 親愛的…（卡片開頭）
Dear Amy,
親愛的 Amy：
STEP 3 · SENTENCE FRAMES

③ 句型練習：填空造句

用自己的情況把句子填完整（可以按 🎤 用說的）。想不出來就按「看例句」。這些句子可以直接放進你的短文裡。

  1. Dear ,
    親愛的……（收卡的人）
    Dear Tom,
  2. I am having a on .
    我在……（日期）要辦一個……
    I am having a pool party on Sunday, July 20.
  3. Would you like to come?
    你想來嗎？（照抄這句）
    Would you like to come?
  4. It will be at from to .
    地點在……，時間從……到……
    It will be at my home from two to five p.m.
  5. Please bring .
    請帶……
    Please bring a swimsuit and a towel.
  6. Please let me know by .
    請在……前讓我知道
    Please let me know by Wednesday.
STEP 4 · GEPT SENTENCE WRITING

④ 造句練習（英檢題型）

全民英檢初級寫作第一部分的三種題型：句子合併、句子改寫、重組句子，共 15 題。要自己寫出完整的句子，送出後對答案、錯的要訂正，會計點數。
