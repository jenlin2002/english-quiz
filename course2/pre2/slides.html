五大句型

The Five Basic Sentence Patterns
S + VS + V + O

本週目標與暖身

本週目標

  • 說出五大句型：S+V、S+V+C、S+V+O、S+V+O+O、S+V+O+C
  • 分辨補語與受詞：looks happy 和 looks at me 不一樣
  • 避開陷阱：arrive at、listen to、discuss 不加 about

暖身 3 題

1He looks happy. happy 是補語還是受詞？補語（說明主詞 He）
2Mom bought me a bag. 有幾個受詞？兩個：me 和 a bag
3I listen ____ music. (to / 不加)to

在真實英文裡找句型

新聞標題、廣告、對話各一句。

新聞標題

StudentsS loveV the new cafeteriaO
→ S+V+O　學生們喜愛新餐廳

廣告

WeS giveV every customerO₁ a free giftO₂!
→ S+V+O+O　送每位客人一份禮物

對話

A: YouS lookV tiredC.
B: The testS madeV meO tiredC.
→ S+V+C 與 S+V+O+C
SS 主詞VV 動詞OO 受詞CC 補語OO₁ 間接受詞OO₂ 直接受詞

五大句型總表

句型例句重點
S + VBirds fly.動詞後面不需要別的成分
S + V + CShe is a nurse. / The soup smells good.補語說明主詞；連綴動詞 be、look、smell、taste、sound、feel、become
S + V + OI like music.受詞是動作的對象，不等於主詞
S + V + O + OHe gave me a gift.give、send、show、buy、make、teach：先給誰，再什麼
S + V + O + CThe news made me sad.補語說明受詞；make、find、call、keep
時間、地點、方式的字（修飾語）不算在句型裡，例如 The baby sleeps quietly 仍是 S + V。

前三種句型：S+V、S+V+C、S+V+O

S + V

特色：動詞自己就完整；後面可加修飾語

BirdsS flyV.
The babyS sleepsV quietly.
WeS arrivedV at six.

S + V + C

特色：連綴動詞後面接補語

SheS isV a teacherC.
The soupS smellsV goodC.
HeS becameV angryC.

S + V + O

特色：及物動詞後面直接接受詞

IS likeV musicO.
SheS readsV booksO.
TheyS builtV a houseO.

後兩種句型：S+V+O+O、S+V+O+C

S + V + O + O

先「給誰」，再「什麼」

改寫：give 用 to，buy、make 用 for

HeS gaveV meO₁ a giftO₂.
→ = He gave a gift to me.
MomS boughtV meO₁ a bagO₂.
→ = Mom bought a bag for me.

S + V + O + C

補語說明「受詞」

常用動詞：make、find、call、keep

The newsS madeV meO sadC.
IS foundV the bookO interestingC.
WeS callV the dogO LuckyC.

對比頁：補語 vs 受詞，及物 vs 不及物

問自己：後面那個字，是在說明主詞（補語），還是動作的對象（受詞）？

補語　說明主詞

He looks happyC.
→ He = happy　他看起來開心
She is a nurseC.
→ She = a nurse

受詞　動作的對象

He looks at meO.
→ He ≠ me　他看著我
I like musicO.
→ I ≠ music

不及物動詞：要加介系詞

arrive at the station　listen to music
→ 到達車站；聽音樂
look at the board　wait for the bus
→ 看黑板；等公車

及物動詞：不要多加字

discuss the plan（×discuss about）
→ 討論計畫
enter the room（×enter into）
→ 進入房間

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
She looks happily. → She looks happy.
look 是連綴動詞，後面接形容詞當補語，不接副詞。
2
We discussed about the plan. → We discussed the plan.
discuss 是及物動詞，直接接受詞，不加 about。
3
I listen music. → I listen to music.
listen 是不及物動詞，要加 to 才能接受詞。
4
He gave to me a book. → He gave me a book.
兩個受詞緊接在動詞後面時不用 to；用 to 時，「什麼」要放前面。
5
The news made me sadly. → The news made me sad.
受詞補語說明受詞的狀態，用形容詞。

快問快答：這是哪一種句型？

1Birds fly.S + V
2She is a nurse.S + V + C
3I like music.S + V + O
4He gave me a gift.S + V + O + O
5The song made her happy.S + V + O + C
6The soup smells good.S + V + C
7We arrived at six.S + V（at six 是修飾語）
8They call the dog Lucky.S + V + O + C

挑錯練習與句型轉換

挑錯：每句有一個錯

1She looks beautifully.beautifully → beautiful
2We discussed about the plan.刪掉 about
3He gave to me a book.He gave me a book. 或 He gave a book to me.

轉換

1He gave a gift to me.（改成 S+V+O+O）He gave me a gift.
2I bought my mom a bag.（改用 for）I bought a bag for my mom.
3The gift is nice. It makes him happy.（S+V+O+C）The gift made him happy.

在閱讀與聽力裡找到它

His motherS madeV himO₁ a big chocolate cakeO₂. The cakeS tastedV deliciousC. TomS wasV tiredC, but heS wasV very happyC.

閱讀怎麼用

  • 先找動詞，再看動詞後面接什麼，就知道句型
  • 動詞是 look、taste、feel 時，後面的形容詞說明主詞

聽力怎麼用

  • 聽到 gave me、bought me，後面接的是「什麼東西」
  • 聽到 made me，後面常接感受：sad、happy、tired

口說句型跟讀

先按 🔊 聽，再跟著大聲念，最後換成自己的詞。

The babyS sleepsV quietly.
→ S+V
The soupS tastesV deliciousC.
→ S+V+C
IS likeV EnglishO.
→ S+V+O
My momS boughtV meO₁ a new bagO₂.
→ S+V+O+O
The newsS madeV usO happyC.
→ S+V+O+C

重點整理與測驗說明

句型怎麼認
S + V動詞後面沒有別的成分
S + V + C連綴動詞後接形容詞或名詞，說明主詞
S + V + O動詞後面接受詞
S + V + O + Ogive、send、buy：先給誰、再什麼
S + V + O + Cmake、find、call：補語說明受詞
口訣：連綴動詞接形容詞；不及物動詞加介系詞；及物動詞直接接受詞；兩個受詞中間不加 to。
