本週目標與暖身

本週目標

  • 用 that、if、whether、疑問詞引導名詞子句，當主詞、受詞、補語
  • 轉述 yes/no 問句和 wh- 問句，轉述命令和請求
  • 用附加問句和感嘆句
  • 認得 the fact that 這類同位語

暖身 3 題

1She asked me ____ I liked coffee. (if / that)if
2The teacher told us ____ quiet. (be / to be)to be
3You haven't finished, ____? (have you / haven't you)have you

回收：第一期的間接引語與第 12 週

第一期學過把直接引語改成間接引語（時態往後退一格）；這週補上問句、命令與名詞子句。

第一期回顧

陳述句：said that + 主詞 + 動詞（時態倒退）

例：will → would、am → was

“I am tired,” he said.
→ 直接引語
He said that he was tired.
→ 他說他很累（間接引語）

第 12 週回收

What I need is a rest.
→ what 子句當主詞（名詞子句）
The news that he won surprised us.
→ 同位語 that 子句

that 子句：當主詞、受詞、補語

名詞子句 = 當名詞用的一個句子

主詞：That he is honest is clear.

受詞：I know that he is honest.

補語：The problem is that we have no time.

That he is honest is clear.
→ 他很誠實是很清楚的
I think that she is right.
→ 我認為她是對的（受詞，that 可省略）

the fact that

that 子句說明前面名詞的內容（同位語）

常見名詞：fact、news、idea、belief、hope

I was surprised at the fact that he refused.
→ 他拒絕了這個事實讓我很驚訝
The fact that she lied made me angry.
→ 她說謊這件事讓我很生氣

if 與 whether 子句：是不是…

if / whether

引導 yes/no 問句改成的子句

意思：是不是…、會不會…

or not：只能和 whether 連用

I don't know if he will come.
→ 我不知道他會不會來
Whether he will come or not doesn't matter.
→ 他來不來都沒關係

只能用 whether 的情況

1.放在句首當主詞

2.放在介系詞後面

3.後面直接接 or not 或 to V

I'm not sure about whether he will agree.
→ 介系詞後面只用 whether
I can't decide whether to go.
→ whether + to V

疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞：間接問句

規則

問句放進句子裡，要改成「直述句」的語序

不倒裝：疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞

不加助動詞：do、does、did 都要拿掉

Do you know where does he live?
→ × 不能倒裝
Do you know where he lives?
→ 你知道他住哪裡嗎？

更多例句

Please tell me what time the train leaves.
→ 請告訴我火車幾點開
I wonder who she is.
→ 我想知道她是誰
I don't remember how I got here.
→ 我不記得我怎麼到這裡的

轉述疑問句：ask if / whether、ask + 疑問詞

直接問句轉述重點
“Do you like coffee?” she asked me. （直接）
She asked me if / whether I liked coffee. （轉述）
yes/no 問句用 if / whether
“Where have you been?” he asked. （直接）
He asked me where I had been. （轉述）
疑問詞留下，用直述語序
“What are you doing?” she asked. （直接）
She asked me what I was doing. （轉述）
時態往後退一格
轉述問句：不用 that，不加 do / does / did，語序改成直述句；問號改成句號，時態和人稱也要跟著改。

轉述命令與請求：tell / ask / order + 人 + to V

命令與請求

不用 that 子句，改用 to V

公式：tell / ask / order + 人 + to V

否定：not to V

“Be quiet,” the teacher said.
→ 直接
The teacher told us to be quiet.
→ 老師叫我們安靜

請求、否定與強度

“Could you help me?” she said to him.
→ 請求
She asked him to help her.
→ 她請他幫她
“Don't close the door,” she said.
→ 否定命令
She told him not to close the door.
→ 她叫他不要關門

附加問句

規則

前面肯定 → 後面否定；前面否定 → 後面肯定

助動詞：和前面句子的 be、助動詞、情態動詞一致

主詞：用代名詞

You are a student, aren't you?
→ 你是學生，對不對？
You haven't finished, have you?
→ 你還沒完成，對不對？

特殊情況

Let's …,shall we?

命令句，will you?

I am …,aren't I?

everyone / nobody,they

Let's go, shall we?
→ 我們走吧，好嗎？
Open the door, will you?
→ 把門打開，好嗎？

感嘆句：What 與 How

句型公式例句
What 開頭What + a / an + 形容詞 + 名詞 + (主詞 + 動詞)!
What a beautiful day it is! （多麼美好的一天！）
What + 複數／不可數What + 形容詞 + 複數名詞!
What beautiful flowers they are! （多麼漂亮的花！）
How 開頭How + 形容詞／副詞 + (主詞 + 動詞)!
How beautiful the day is! （這一天多美啊！）
判斷：後面有名詞用 What（What a nice day），沒有名詞只有形容詞用 How（How nice）。

對比頁：容易搞混的四組

if 與 whether

I wonder if he will come.
→ 一般情況兩者都可以
Whether he comes or not, I'll go.
→ 句首只能 whether

that 與 what 子句

That he lied is true.
→ that 沒有意思，只連接
What he said is true.
→ what 有意思，是「…的事」

tell 與 say

She told me to wait.
→ tell 後面接人
She said that she was tired.
→ say 後面直接接內容

What 與 How 感嘆句

What a nice day!
→ 有名詞
How nice!
→ 沒有名詞

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
Do you know where does he live? → Do you know where he lives?
間接問句要用直述語序，不加 does。
2
She asked me that I liked coffee. → She asked me if I liked coffee.
轉述 yes/no 問句要用 if 或 whether。
3
The teacher told us be quiet. → The teacher told us to be quiet.
轉述命令用 tell + 人 + to V。
4
You haven't finished, haven't you? → You haven't finished, have you?
前面否定，後面要用肯定的附加問句。
5
How a nice day it is! → What a nice day it is!
後面有名詞 day，要用 What。

快問快答 8 題

1____ he is honest is clear. (That / What)That
2I'm not sure about ____ he will agree. (if / whether)whether
3Please tell me what time ____. (the train leaves / does the train leave)the train leaves
4She asked me ____ I liked coffee. (if / that)if
5He asked me where ____. (I had been / had I been)I had been
6The teacher told us ____ quiet. (be / to be)to be
7She asked him ____ the door. (not to close / don't close)not to close
8____ a beautiful day it is! (What / How)What

在閱讀與聽力裡找到它

A reporter asked Amy whether she had expected to win. “That I won is still hard to believe,” Amy said. The reporter asked her what her secret was. Amy said her teacher had told her not to give up. “The fact that my mom believed in me helped a lot.”

閱讀怎麼用

  • 看到 asked 加 if、whether、疑問詞，是在轉述別人問了什麼
  • told / asked + 人 + to V，是轉述命令或請求
  • 看到 the fact that，後面的 that 子句是事實內容

聽力怎麼用

  • 聽到 asked me if，後面是 yes/no 問題
  • 聽到 told me to，後面是對方的指示
  • 聽到句尾的 ... aren't you?，表示確認，答案看事實

口說句型跟讀

先按 🔊 聽，再跟著大聲念，最後換成自己的情境。

The fact that I won surprised me.
→ the fact that
My friend asked me whether I wanted to join.
→ ask whether
My teacher asked me where I had put my homework.
→ ask + wh-
My mother told me to clean my room.
→ tell + 人 + to V
What a beautiful day it is!
→ 感嘆句

重點整理與測驗說明

重點規則
that 子句當主詞、受詞、補語；the fact that 是同位語
if / whether是不是；句首、介系詞後只用 whether
間接問句疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞，不倒裝
轉述命令tell / ask / order + 人 + (not) to V
附加問句肯定 → 否定；否定 → 肯定
口訣：問句變子句，語序要直述；yes/no 用 if，命令用 to V。
