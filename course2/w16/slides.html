Comparisons & Parallel Structure

本週目標與暖身

本週目標

  • 用 as…as、not as / so…as 做同級比較，會說倍數（twice as…as）
  • 會用 the 比較級…, the 比較級… 和 比較級 and 比較級
  • 用比較級說出最高級：than any other、no other
  • 用 both…and、either…or、neither…nor、not only…but also，動詞和誰一致
  • 平行結構：and、or、but 前後的形式要一樣

暖身 3 題

1My bag is as ____ as yours. (heavy / heavier)heavy
2The more you practice, the ____ you get. (better / best)better
3Neither Tom nor his sisters ____ here. (is / are)are

回收：第 14 週的分詞構句與連接詞

上週把兩個句子接起來；這週把兩樣東西「拿來比」或「並排放」。

第 14 週回顧

Having finished my homework, I went out.
→ 分詞構句：比主句更早發生
Despite the rain, we went to the park.
→ despite + 名詞

這週的延伸

My room is twice as big as yours.
→ 倍數 + as…as
She is not only smart but also kind.
→ 對等連接詞：前後形式一致

同級比較：as…as 與 not as / so…as

as + 原級 + as

一樣…

公式：A + be/V + as + 形容詞/副詞原級 + as + B

意思：「A 和 B 一樣…」

Tom is as tall as his father.
→ Tom 和他爸爸一樣高
She runs as fast as her brother.
→ 修飾動詞 runs，用副詞 fast

not as / so + 原級 + as

沒有那麼…

公式：A + not + as / so + 原級 + as + B

意思：「A 沒有 B 那麼…」= A 比 B 更不…

This test is not as difficult as the last one.
→ 這次考試沒有上次那麼難
I can't swim so well as you.
→ 否定句才可以用 so…as
as…as 中間一定是「原級」：as taller as、as tallest as 都是錯的。也可以說 as many books as、as much money as。

倍數與比較級的修飾語

倍數的兩種說法

twice、three times、half

公式 1：倍數 + as + 原級 + as

公式 2：倍數 + 比較級 + than（三倍以上較常用）

My room is twice as big as yours.
→ 我的房間是你的兩倍大
This bag costs three times as much as that one.
→ 這個包包的價錢是那個的三倍
The new road is three times longer than the old one.
→ 新路比舊路長三倍

強調比較級：much、far、even、a lot

「…得多」

可以用：much、far、even、a lot、still

不能用：very + 比較級

This phone is much cheaper than that one.
→ 便宜得多
Today is even hotter than yesterday.
→ 今天比昨天還要熱
She is far better at math than I am.
→ 她數學好得多

the 比較級…, the 比較級… 與 比較級 and 比較級

越…就越…

the + 比較級，兩邊都要有 the

公式：The + 比較級 + S + V, the + 比較級 + S + V

The more you practice, the better you get.
→ 你練習越多，就變得越好
The older he gets, the wiser he becomes.
→ 他年紀越大，就越有智慧
The sooner, the better.
→ 越快越好（慣用語，可省略主詞動詞）

越來越…

get / become / grow + 比較級 and 比較級

公式：比較級 + and + 比較級 / more and more + 原級

It is getting colder and colder.
→ 天氣越來越冷
English is becoming more and more important.
→ 英文變得越來越重要
Fewer and fewer people write letters now.
→ 越來越少人寫信了

用比較級說出最高級

句型意思例句
than any other + 單數名詞比其他任何…都
Mount Jade is higher than any other mountain in Taiwan. （玉山比台灣其他任何一座山都高）
No other + 單數名詞 + 比較級 + than沒有其他…比…更
No other mountain in Taiwan is higher than Mount Jade. （台灣沒有別的山比玉山高）
one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞最…的之一
She is one of the best students in our class. （她是我們班最好的學生之一）
最高級 + (that) S + have ever p.p.我…過最…的
This is the funniest movie I have ever seen. （這是我看過最好笑的電影）
than any other 後面接「單數名詞」，而且要有 other（不能和自己比）；one of the 後面接「複數名詞」。

比較的對象要一致，與不用 than 的比較

比較對象要對等

that / those / 所有格

規則：拿「東西」比「東西」，不能拿「東西」比「人」

The weather in Taipei is hotter than that in Tokyo.
→ that = the weather（單數）
The prices here are higher than those in my town.
→ those = the prices（複數）
My bike is newer than my brother's.
→ my brother's = my brother's bike

不用 than 的比較

to / than 的特別用法

superior / inferior to：比…優秀／差

prefer A to B：比起 B 更喜歡 A

would rather A than B：寧願 A 也不要 B（接原形）

This phone is superior to that one.
→ 這支手機比那支好
I prefer tea to coffee.
→ 比起咖啡我更喜歡茶
I would rather stay home than go out.
→ 我寧願待在家也不要出門

對等相關連接詞：both、either、neither、not only

句型意思動詞跟誰例句
both A and BA 和 B 都複數
Both Tom and Amy are students. （Tom 和 Amy 都是學生）
either A or B不是 A 就是 B靠近的 B
Either you or he has to go. （不是你就是他得去）
neither A nor BA 和 B 都不靠近的 B
Neither Tom nor his sisters are here. （Tom 和他姊妹都不在）
not only A but also B不但 A 而且 B靠近的 B
Not only the students but also the teacher was late. （不但學生，連老師也遲到了）
not A but B不是 A 而是 BB
He is not a doctor but a nurse. （他不是醫生，而是護理師）
口訣：both 一定用複數；either…or、neither…nor、not only…but also 都是「就近原則」，動詞看最靠近的那個主詞。

平行結構：and、or、but 前後要一樣

形式要一致

Parallel Structure

名詞 & 名詞：reading, music, and sports

V-ing & V-ing：I like reading and swimming.

to V & to V：I want to learn and (to) grow.

形容詞 & 形容詞：smart, kind, and funny

She likes reading, swimming, and dancing.
→ 三個都是 V-ing
He is smart, kind, and funny.
→ 三個都是形容詞

相關連接詞也要平行

前後對稱

規則：both、either、neither、not only 後面接的東西，要和 and、or、nor、but also 後面的形式一樣

She is not only smart but also hardworking.
→ 形容詞對形容詞
You can either walk or take the bus.
→ 原形對原形
She likes to swim and dancing.
→ to swim 和 dancing 不平行，要改成 swimming and dancing

對比頁：容易搞混的四組

as…as 與 than

He is as tall as me.
→ 一樣高，中間用原級
He is taller than me.
→ 比較高，用比較級 + than

much taller 與 very tall

He is much taller than me.
→ 比較級用 much 加強
He is very tall.
→ 原級才用 very

both…and 與 either…or

Both he and I are ready.
→ 兩個都，動詞用複數
Either he or I am wrong.
→ 其中一個，動詞看 I

prefer A to B 與 would rather A than B

I prefer walking to driving.
→ to 是介系詞，接 V-ing
I would rather walk than drive.
→ 接原形動詞

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
He is as taller as his father. → He is as tall as his father.
as…as 中間用原級。
2
This book is very better than that one. → This book is much better than that one.
比較級要用 much、far、even 加強，不能用 very。
3
Mount Jade is higher than any mountain in Taiwan. → Mount Jade is higher than any other mountain in Taiwan.
玉山本身就在台灣，要加 other，不能和自己比。
4
Neither my brother nor I are hungry. → Neither my brother nor I am hungry.
neither…nor 動詞看最靠近的主詞 I。
5
I like swimming, to read, and play games. → I like swimming, reading, and playing games.
並列的東西形式要一樣（平行結構）。

挑錯練習與句型轉換

挑錯：每句有一個錯

1The weather in Taipei is hotter than Tokyo.Tokyo → that in Tokyo
2She is not only kind but also she is smart.改成 not only kind but also smart
3I prefer tea than coffee.than → to

改寫成指定句型

1Tom is taller than any other boy in his class.（用 No other）No other boy in his class is taller than Tom.
2You eat more; you get fatter.（用 The…, the…）The more you eat, the fatter you get.
3Tom is not here. Mary is not here.（用 Neither…nor）Neither Tom nor Mary is here.

在閱讀與聽力裡找到它

Is city life better than country life? In the city, buses come much more often, and shops stay open later. However, rent in the city can be twice as high as it is in the country. The bigger a city grows, the busier its streets become. Country life is not only quiet but also healthy.

閱讀怎麼用

  • 看到 than、as…as，先找「誰和誰比」
  • the + 比較級, the + 比較級：前面是條件，後面是結果
  • not only A but also B：B 通常是作者要強調的新資訊

聽力怎麼用

  • 聽到 twice、three times，題目常考倍數或價錢
  • 聽到 not as…as，代表前者「比較不…」
  • 聽到 neither…nor，表示兩個都沒有

口說句型跟讀

先按 🔊 聽，再跟著大聲念，最後換成自己的句子。

My sister is not as tall as I am.
→ not as…as
The more I read, the more I learn.
→ the 比較級, the 比較級
Taipei 101 is taller than any other building in Taiwan.
→ 比較級表最高級
I like both basketball and swimming.
→ both…and
My best friend is not only funny but also helpful.
→ not only…but also

重點整理與測驗說明

重點規則
as…as中間用原級；否定 not as / so…as
倍數twice / three times + as…as 或 + 比較級 than
the 比較級, the 比較級越…就越…，兩邊都要 the
