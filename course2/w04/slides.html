暖身 3 題

1A new school ____ in our town now. (is built / is being built)is being built
2I ____ my hair cut yesterday. (had / made)had
3He ____ hurt when he fell off his bike. (was / got)got（意外用 get）

回收：第一期的被動語態

主動句的受詞變成被動句的主詞，動詞用 be + 過去分詞，做事的人用 by。

主動

Active
The cook makes the soup.
→ 廚師煮湯
Workers built the bridge in 1990.
→ 工人在 1990 年蓋了這座橋

被動

Passive

公式：be + 過去分詞（+ by 做事的人）

The soup is made by the cook.
→ 湯是廚師煮的
The bridge was built in 1990.
→ 這座橋是 1990 年蓋的

完成式被動：has been done、had been done

現在完成被動

has / have been + p.p.

用法：已經被做完，和現在有關

The room has been cleaned.
→ 房間已經被打掃過了
Many houses have been built here.
→ 這裡已經蓋了很多房子

過去完成被動

had been + p.p.

用法：在過去某個時間之前，已經被做完

When we arrived, the cake had been eaten.
→ 我們到的時候，蛋糕已經被吃掉了
The letter had been sent before noon.
→ 信在中午前就寄出去了

進行式被動：is being built、was being repaired

現在進行被動

am / is / are being + p.p.

用法：現在正在被做

A new school is being built in our town.
→ 我們鎮上正在蓋一所新學校
The streets are being cleaned now.
→ 街道現在正在被清掃

過去進行被動

was / were being + p.p.

用法：當時正在被做

The road was being repaired when the accident happened.
→ 事故發生時，那條路正在修
Dinner was being cooked when I got home.
→ 我到家時，晚餐正在煮

被動語態各種時態總整理

時態主動被動
現在簡單式They make toys.Toys are made.
過去簡單式They built a bridge.A bridge was built.
未來式They will send it.It will be sent.
現在完成式They have cleaned it.It has been cleaned.
過去完成式They had eaten it.It had been eaten.
現在進行式They are building it.It is being built.
過去進行式They were repairing it.It was being repaired.
助動詞You must finish it.It must be finished.

get + p.p. 與 be + p.p.

get + 過去分詞

強調變化或意外

特色：口語，常用在意外、變化的事

常見：get lost、get hurt、get married、get tired

We got lost in the big city.
→ 我們在大城市裡迷路了
He got hurt when he fell off his bike.
→ 他從腳踏車上摔下來受傷了
They got married last June.
→ 他們去年六月結婚了

be + 過去分詞

強調狀態或事實

特色：較正式，描述狀態

The door is closed.
→ 門是關著的
The window was broken by the storm.
→ 窗戶是被暴風雨弄壞的

使役動詞：have、get、make、let

句型意思例句
have + 人 + 原形請某人做
She had the mechanic fix her car. （她請修車師傅修車）
have + 物 + p.p.請人處理某物／遭遇某事
I had my hair cut. （我去剪了頭髮）
get + 人 + to V說服某人做
I got my brother to help me. （我請哥哥幫我）
make + 人 + 原形強迫某人做
My mom made me clean my room. （媽媽叫我打掃房間）
let + 人 + 原形允許某人做
The teacher let us leave early. （老師讓我們早點走）

感官動詞：V、V-ing、p.p.

see / hear / watch / feel + 受詞 + …

三種形式意思不同

原形 V：看到、聽到「整個過程」

V-ing：看到、聽到「正在進行」

p.p.：受詞「被」怎麼樣

I saw him cross the street.
→ 我看到他過馬路（從頭到尾）
I saw him crossing the street.
→ 我看到他正在過馬路
I heard my name called.
→ 我聽到有人叫我的名字（被叫）

記法

整個過程用原形，正在做用 V-ing，被別人做用過去分詞。
I heard someone singing in the next room.
→ 我聽到隔壁房間有人正在唱歌

對比頁：容易搞混的三組

have sth done 與 have sb do

I had my hair cut.
→ 請人剪我的頭髮
I had the barber cut my hair.
→ 請理髮師剪我的頭髮

see him cross 與 crossing

I saw him cross.
→ 看到整個過程
I saw him crossing.
→ 看到正在過馬路

be + p.p. 與 get + p.p.

The cup was broken.
→ 杯子是破的（狀態）
The cup got broken.
→ 杯子不小心打破了（意外）

has been 與 is being

The room has been cleaned.
→ 已經打掃過了
The room is being cleaned.
→ 現在正在打掃

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
A new school is building in our town. → A new school is being built in our town.
學校是「被」蓋的，進行式被動用 is being + p.p.。
2
I had my hair cut by myself yesterday. → I had my hair cut yesterday.
have + 物 + p.p. 是「請別人」做，不是自己做。
3
My mom made me to clean my room. → My mom made me clean my room.
make、let、have 後面用原形，不加 to。
4
I saw him to cross the street. → I saw him cross the street.
感官動詞後面接原形或 V-ing，不加 to。
5
He was got hurt. → He got hurt.
get + p.p. 本身就是被動，不再加 be。

快問快答 8 題

2When we arrived, the cake ____. (had been eaten / has been eaten)had been eaten

4We ____ lost in the city. (got / were got)got

挑錯練習與句型轉換

挑錯：每句有一個錯

1The bridge is building now.is building → is being built
2The teacher let us to leave early.刪掉 to
3He was got lost yesterday.was got → got

轉換

1They are building a school.（改被動）A school is being built.
2Someone stole my bike.（用 have）I had my bike stolen.
3My mom forced me to clean my room.（用 make）My mom made me clean my room.

在閱讀與聽力裡找到它

Chocolate is made from cocoa beans. A new factory was built near the river last year. Today, thousands of bars are being produced every hour, and many workers have their lunch served by the company.

閱讀怎麼用

  • 看到 be + p.p.，主詞是「被」做的人或物
  • 看到 being，表示正在進行
  • have + 物 + p.p.，是請別人做，不是自己做

聽力怎麼用

  • 聽到 has been 或 had been，表示已經完成
  • 聽到 is being，表示現在正在被做
  • 聽到 made me、let us，後面接原形動詞

口說句型跟讀

先按 🔊 聽，再跟著大聲念，最後換成自己的事。

Chocolate is made from cocoa beans.
→ 被動語態
A new road is being built near my home.
→ 進行式被動
I had my hair cut last week.
→ have + 物 + p.p.
My mother made me clean my room.
→ make + 人 + 原形
I got lost in the city last summer.
→ get + p.p.

重點整理與測驗說明

句型公式重點
完成式被動has / had been + p.p.已經被做完
進行式被動is / was being + p.p.正在被做
get + p.p.get / got + p.p.意外或變化
使役動詞make、let、have + 人 + 原形後面不加 to
have + 物 + p.p.had my hair cut請別人做
口訣：正在被做用 being；請人做用 have 加物加 p.p.；make、let、have 後面不加 to。
