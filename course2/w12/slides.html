本週目標與暖身

本週目標

  • 把關係子句縮減成 V-ing、p.p.、to V
  • 用 what、whatever、whoever、whichever 當名詞子句的開頭
  • 用 when、how、the reason why 當關係副詞
  • 用同位語在名詞後面補充說明

暖身 3 題

1The girl ____ on the sofa is my sister. (sitting / sat)sitting
2The books ____ here are cheap. (selling / sold)sold
3____ I need is a rest. (That / What)What

回收：第 11 週的關係代名詞

縮減的來源就是關係子句：先會完整的，再學怎麼縮短。

完整的關係子句

主詞關代：who、which、that + 動詞

受詞關代：可以省略

The girl who is sitting on the sofa is my sister.
→ 坐在沙發上的女孩是我姊姊
The books which are sold here are cheap.
→ 這裡賣的書很便宜

本週的目標

The girl sitting on the sofa is my sister.
→ 縮減後更簡潔
The books sold here are cheap.
→ 縮減後更簡潔

縮減成 V-ing：主動

規則

關係子句是主動 → 去掉關代，動詞改 V-ing

步驟：去掉 who / which / that 和 be 動詞

條件：關代必須是主詞

The man who is standing there is my uncle.
→ 完整版
The man standing there is my uncle.
→ 縮減版：站在那裡的男人是我叔叔

沒有 be 動詞時

People who live near the sea eat a lot of fish.
→ 完整版
People living near the sea eat a lot of fish.
→ 縮減版：住在海邊的人吃很多魚

縮減成 p.p.：被動

規則

關係子句是被動 → 去掉關代和 be 動詞，留下過去分詞

步驟：去掉 which / that + be 動詞

留下：過去分詞 + 其餘部分

The language which is spoken in Brazil is Portuguese.
→ 完整版
The language spoken in Brazil is Portuguese.
→ 縮減版：巴西說的語言是葡萄牙語

更多例句

The window broken by Tom has been repaired.
→ 被 Tom 打破的窗戶已修好
The books written by her are popular.
→ 她寫的書很受歡迎

縮減成 to V：序數、only、最高級

規則

先行詞有 the first、the last、the only、最高級時，可縮成 to V

意思：表示「要…的」或「第一個…的」

She was the first student to enter the room.
→ 她是第一個進入教室的學生
He is the only one to know the answer.
→ 他是唯一知道答案的人

也可表示「要做的」

I have a lot of homework to do.
→ 我有很多功課要做（= that I have to do）
She needs someone to help her.
→ 她需要有人幫她

縮減子句總整理

關係子句的意思縮減成例句
主動、進行中V-ing
The boy sitting there is Tom. （坐在那裡的男孩是 Tom）
被動、完成p.p.
The cake made by Mom is great. （媽媽做的蛋糕很棒）
序數、the only、最高級to V
He is the first man to land on the moon. （他是第一個登陸月球的人）
縮減的前提：關代是主詞。受詞關代只能省略，不能縮成 V-ing；縮減後，分詞的動作要和被修飾的名詞有「主動或被動」的關係。

what：包含先行詞的關代

what = the thing(s) that

what 本身就有先行詞，前面不需要名詞

意思：…的事物

用法：當主詞、受詞、補語

What I need is a rest.
→ 我需要的是休息（= The thing that I need）
I don't understand what he said.
→ 我不懂他說了什麼

對比 that

that 前面一定要有先行詞；what 前面不能有先行詞。
This is the thing that I need.
→ 有先行詞用 that
This is what I need.
→ 沒有先行詞用 what

whatever、whoever、whichever

字意思例句
whatever任何…的事物（= anything that）
You can eat whatever you like. （你可以吃任何你喜歡的）
whoever任何…的人（= anyone who）
Whoever comes first will get a prize. （最先來的人都會得獎）
whichever兩個或幾個之中任一個
Take whichever you like. （你喜歡哪個就拿哪個（在有限選項中））
這三個字後面接的是名詞子句，在句子裡當主詞或受詞；也可以接名詞：whatever book、whichever way。

關係副詞：when、how、the reason why

when 與 the reason why

when：先行詞是時間（year、day、time）

why：先行詞是 the reason

I'll never forget the day when we met.
→ 我永遠忘不了我們相遇的那天
That is the reason why he left.
→ 這就是他離開的原因

how 的特別規則

That's how I learned English.
→ 我就是這樣學英文的（how 單獨用）
That's the way how I learned English.
→ × the way 和 how 不能一起用

同位語：名詞後面補充說明

名詞 + 名詞（用逗號）

後面的名詞是在說明前面的名詞

特色：兩個名詞指同一個人或同一件事

Mr. Lee, a famous writer, will give a talk.
→ Lee 先生，一位著名作家，將演講
Taipei, the capital of Taiwan, is a big city.
→ 台北，台灣的首都，是大城市

名詞 + that 子句

that 子句說明前面名詞的內容

常見名詞：news、fact、idea、belief、hope

The news that our team won made us happy.
→ 我們隊贏了的消息讓我們很開心
I like the idea that we go together.
→ 我喜歡我們一起去的這個想法

對比頁：容易搞混的四組

V-ing 與 p.p.

The girl singing there is Amy.
→ 主動：她在唱
The song sung by Amy is nice.
→ 被動：歌被唱

what 與 that

What I want is a pen.
→ 沒有先行詞用 what
The pen that I want is red.
→ 有先行詞用 that

whoever 與 whomever

Whoever wins gets a prize.
→ 做主詞用 whoever
Invite whomever you like.
→ 做受詞可用 whomever（口語 whoever）

the news that 與 the news which

The news that he won surprised us.
→ 同位語，用 that
The news which I read was sad.
→ 關代，用 which 或 that

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
The girl sat on the sofa is my sister. → The girl sitting on the sofa is my sister.
主動的縮減要用 V-ing。
2
The books selling here are cheap. → The books sold here are cheap.
書是被賣的，被動用 p.p.。
3
That I need is a rest. → What I need is a rest.
沒有先行詞要用 what，不能用 that。
4
That's the way how I learned English. → That's how I learned English.
the way 和 how 只能留一個。
5
The news which he won surprised us. → The news that he won surprised us.
同位語的 that 子句，不能換成 which。

挑錯練習與句型轉換

挑錯：每句有一個錯

1The boy stand there is my cousin.stand → standing
2That she said surprised me.That → What
3That is the way how he solved it.刪掉 the way 或 how

縮減成短句

1The woman who is sitting there is my aunt.The woman sitting there is my aunt.
2The cake which was made by Mom is great.The cake made by Mom is great.
3He is the first man who landed on the moon.He is the first man to land on the moon.

在閱讀與聽力裡找到它

Tainan, the oldest city in Taiwan, is a place visited by millions of tourists. The streets filled with old temples tell stories of the past. What surprises visitors most is how friendly the people are. Whoever loves history should come.

閱讀怎麼用

  • 名詞後面直接接 V-ing 或 p.p.，是縮減的關係子句，在修飾前面的名詞
  • 句首的 What 子句是整個句子的主詞
  • 逗號夾住的名詞，是同位語，在補充說明

聽力怎麼用

  • 聽到名詞後接 -ing 或 -ed，把它想成「…的」
  • 聽到 whoever，表示「任何人」，常出現在規則、獎品
  • 聽到 the year when，後面是在說發生的事

口說句型跟讀

先按 🔊 聽，再跟著大聲念，最後換成自己的句子。

The boy standing near the window is my friend.
→ V-ing 縮減
The food sold in this shop is delicious.
→ p.p. 縮減
What I like most is the food.
→ what
Whoever finishes first can choose a prize.
→ whoever
2020 was the year when I started learning English.
→ when

重點整理與測驗說明

重點規則
縮減主動V-ing
縮減被動p.p.
序數、only、最高級to V
what= the thing(s) that，前面不接先行詞
whoever / whatever / whichever任何人、任何事、其中任一個
口訣：主動 V-ing，被動 p.p.，序數與 only 用 to V；what 前面沒有名詞。
