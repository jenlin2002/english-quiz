WEEK 6 · GRAMMAR

情態助動詞進階：推測與責任

Modals of Deduction & Regret
must have p.p.should have p.p.

本週目標與暖身

本週目標

  • 用 must、may、might、could、can't 推測現在與過去的事
  • 用 should have p.p.、needn't have p.p. 說明後悔與批評
  • 用 would rather、had better、be supposed to 表達偏好、勸告與責任

暖身 3 題

1Her coat is gone. She ____ have left already. (must / should)must
2I forgot my homework. I ____ have checked my bag. (should / must)should
3You ____ take an umbrella. It may rain. (had better / would rather)had better

回收：第一期的現在推測

第一期學過：must be 是「一定是」，can't be 是「不可能是」。這週往前走兩步：加上可能性的強弱，再把推測用在過去。

must be

一定是
He must be tired. He worked all night.
→ 他一定很累，他工作了整晚

can't be

不可能是
That can't be true.
→ 那不可能是真的

推測的強弱：這件事有多確定？

must be　幾乎確定→may / might / could be　有可能→can't be　幾乎不可能

很確定

She must be at home. Her light is on.
→ 她一定在家，她的燈是亮的

有可能

He might be sick. He looks pale.
→ 他可能生病了，他臉色蒼白

不可能

It can't be Tom. He is in Japan.
→ 不可能是 Tom，他在日本

推測過去的事：modal + have p.p.

公式

must have p.p.：過去一定做了／發生了

may / might / could have p.p.：過去可能做了

can't / couldn't have p.p.：過去不可能做

The street is wet. It must have rained.
→ 街道是濕的，一定下過雨
He might have missed the bus.
→ 他可能錯過公車了

更多例句

The cake is gone. Someone must have eaten it.
→ 蛋糕不見了，一定有人吃掉了
He can't have known the answer. He is only five.
→ 他不可能知道答案，他才五歲

後悔與批評：should have、needn't have

should have p.p.

應該做卻沒做

shouldn't have p.p.：不應該做卻做了

I failed the test. I should have studied harder.
→ 我考試不及格，我應該更用功的
You shouldn't have said that.
→ 你不該說那句話

needn't have p.p.

做了，但其實沒必要

對比：didn't need to 是沒做；needn't have 是已經做了

I needn't have hurried. The train was late.
→ 我不必那麼趕的，火車誤點了
You needn't have bought a gift.
→ 你不必買禮物的

偏好、勸告與責任：would rather、had better、be supposed to

句型意思例句
would rather + 原形寧願
I would rather stay home than go out. （我寧願待在家也不要出門）
had better + 原形最好（不然有後果）
You had better take an umbrella. （你最好帶把傘）
be supposed to + 原形應該（規定或預期）
Students are supposed to arrive by eight. （學生應該八點前到校）
had better 的否定是 had better not + 原形：You had better not be late.

對比頁：容易搞混的兩組

must have been 與 can't have been

It must have been Tom.
→ 一定是 Tom（肯定的推測）
It can't have been Tom.
→ 不可能是 Tom（否定的推測）

should have 與 needn't have

I should have called you.
→ 我應該打給你的（沒打）
I needn't have called you.
→ 我不必打給你的（打了，白打）

現在推測與過去推測

He must be tired.
→ 他現在一定很累
He must have been tired.
→ 他當時一定很累

had better 與 would rather

You had better go.
→ 你最好去（勸告）
I would rather go.
→ 我寧願去（偏好）

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
She must has left early. → She must have left early.
modal 後面一律接原形，要用 have，不是 has 或 had。
2
He can't have known, so he must have told us. → He can't have known, so he couldn't have told us.
前後的推測要一致，不能一句說不可能，一句又說一定。
3
I should studied harder. → I should have studied harder.
should 後面要接 have + 過去分詞，才能表示過去應該做的事。
4
You had better to go now. → You had better go now.
had better 後面接原形，不加 to。
5
She supposed to come at eight. → She is supposed to come at eight.
be supposed to 要有 be 動詞，不能少。

快問快答 8 題

1The street is wet. It ____ last night. (must have rained / must rain)must have rained
2He ____ be at home. I just saw him at the station. (can't / must)can't
3She isn't answering. She ____ be sleeping. (might / needn't)might
4I failed. I ____ harder. (should have studied / must have studied)should have studied
5I ____ hurried. The train was late. (needn't have / can't have)needn't have
6You ____ go now, or you'll be late. (had better / would better)had better
7I ____ stay home than go out. (would rather / had better)would rather
8Students ____ arrive by eight. (are supposed to / supposed to)are supposed to

挑錯練習與句型轉換

挑錯：每句有一個錯

1She must had left early.had → have
2You had better to take a coat.刪掉 to
3I should called him yesterday.should called → should have called

轉換

1I'm sure he left. （用 must）He must have left.
2It's impossible that she lied. （用 can't）She can't have lied.
3I didn't study, and I regret it. （用 should）I should have studied.

在閱讀與聽力裡找到它

“The thief must have come in through the window,” she said. The guard shook his head. “It can't have been the window. I stood beside it all night.” “I should have locked that door,” he said sadly.

閱讀怎麼用

  • 看到 must have、can't have，找出推測的線索（because 後面）
  • should have 表示說話的人後悔或批評
  • 題目問「作者怎麼想」，看推測的強弱

聽力怎麼用

  • 聽到 must 與 can't 相反，判斷說話者的立場
  • 聽到 should have，表示事情沒做
  • needn't have 表示做了但沒必要

口說句型跟讀

先按 🔊 聽，再跟著大聲念，最後換成自己的事。

He might have caught a cold.
→ 可能的推測
She must be teaching another class.
→ 現在的推測
I should have gone to bed earlier.
→ 後悔
I would rather stay home and read a book.
→ 偏好
Students are supposed to arrive before eight.
→ 責任

重點整理與測驗說明

句型意思
must / may / might / can't + 原形推測現在
must / may / might / can't + have p.p.推測過去
should / shouldn't have p.p.過去該做沒做／不該做卻做
needn't have p.p.做了但沒必要
would rather / had better / be supposed to偏好／勸告／責任
口訣：現在推測接原形，過去推測接 have p.p.；後悔用 should have，白做用 needn't have。
