本週目標與暖身

本週目標

  • 分辨只接 V-ing 與只接 to V 的動詞
  • 搞懂 stop、remember、forget、try、regret 後面接 V-ing 或 to V 意思不同
  • 用 to have p.p. 與 to be p.p. 表達「已經」與「被」
  • 分辨 be used to V-ing 與 used to V

回收：第 8 週的假設語氣

上週學的 wish 之後，這週繼續用動詞形式說「想做的事」。

wish 回顧

I wish I were taller.
→ 現在的願望
I wish I had practiced more.
→ 過去的遺憾

本週延伸

I enjoy playing basketball.
→ 我喜歡打籃球（動名詞）
I hope to play in the school team.
→ 我希望能加入校隊（不定詞）

只接 V-ing 的動詞

後面一定接 V-ing

口訣：mind finish enjoy avoid consider practice keep give up suggest miss imagine

特色：不能接 to V

I enjoy swimming in summer.
→ 我喜歡夏天游泳
She avoided talking to him.
→ 她避免跟他說話

更多例句

Do you mind opening the window?
→ 你介意開一下窗戶嗎？
He finished doing his homework.
→ 他寫完功課了
They are considering moving to Taipei.
→ 他們正在考慮搬到台北

只接 to V 的動詞

後面一定接 to V

口訣：decide plan hope want expect promise manage refuse agree offer afford learn

特色：不能接 V-ing

He decided to study abroad.
→ 他決定出國讀書
I can't afford to buy a new phone.
→ 我買不起新手機

更多例句

She refused to answer the question.
→ 她拒絕回答問題
We managed to catch the last bus.
→ 我們設法趕上了末班車
He promised to call me.
→ 他答應打電話給我

兩種都能接，意思幾乎一樣

動詞例句意思
like / love / hate
I like to swim / swimming. （我喜歡游泳）
差別很小
start / begin / continue
It started to rain / raining. （開始下雨了）
兩種都可以
prefer
I prefer to walk / walking. （我比較喜歡走路）
兩種都可以
這些動詞兩種都對，選哪個都不會被扣分；但 stop、remember、forget、try、regret 就不一樣了。

stop、remember、forget：接 V-ing 與 to V 意思不同

動詞接 V-ing接 to V
stop
He stopped smoking. （他戒菸了（停止做））
He stopped to smoke. （他停下來去抽菸（停下來做））
remember
I remember locking the door. （我記得鎖過門（做過了））
Remember to lock the door. （記得要鎖門（還沒做））
forget
I forgot meeting her. （我忘了見過她（做過））
I forgot to meet her. （我忘了去見她（還沒做））
口訣：V-ing 是「已經做過」，to V 是「接下來要做」。

try、regret、mean：意思也不同

動詞接 V-ing接 to V
try
Try adding some salt. （試試看加點鹽（嘗試方法））
I tried to open the door. （我努力想開門（盡力去做））
regret
I regret saying that. （我後悔說了那句話（做過））
I regret to tell you. （我很遺憾要告訴你（現在要說））
mean
This means waiting longer. （這意味著要等更久）
I meant to call you. （我本來想打電話給你）

完成式不定詞與被動不定詞

to have p.p.

表示比主要動詞更早發生

意思：已經做過（常表示遺憾或推測）

He seems to have forgotten my name.
→ 他似乎已經忘了我的名字
I'd like to have gone with you.
→ 我本來想跟你一起去（可惜沒去）

to be p.p.

表示「被」

意思：主詞是承受動作的人或事

The letter needs to be sent today.
→ 這封信今天需要被寄出
She hopes to be chosen for the team.
→ 她希望能被選進校隊

介系詞 to 後面接 V-ing

片語意思例句
look forward to期待
I look forward to seeing you. （我期待見到你）
be used to習慣於
I am used to getting up early. （我習慣早起）
get used to逐漸習慣
She got used to living alone. （她漸漸習慣獨居）
object to反對
He objected to paying extra. （他反對多付錢）
這裡的 to 是介系詞，所以後面接 V-ing；要判斷 to 是不是介系詞，可以試著在後面放名詞：look forward to the party。

be used to V-ing 與 used to V

be used to V-ing

習慣於

be 動詞：會隨主詞和時態變化

I am used to waking up early.
→ 我習慣早起
He wasn't used to living in a big city.
→ 他當時不習慣住大城市

used to V

過去常常（現在不了）

特色：用 did 構成問句與否定，used to 後面接原形

I used to play basketball.
→ 我以前常打籃球（現在不打了）
Did you use to live here?
→ 你以前住在這裡嗎？

對比頁：容易搞混的四組

stop V-ing 與 stop to V

He stopped talking.
→ 他停止說話
He stopped to talk to her.
→ 他停下來跟她說話

remember V-ing 與 to V

I remember meeting him.
→ 記得見過他
Remember to meet him.
→ 記得去見他

to 是 to V 還是介系詞

I want to go.
→ to + 原形
I look forward to going.
→ 介系詞 to + V-ing

used to 與 be used to

I used to swim.
→ 以前常游泳
I am used to swimming.
→ 習慣游泳

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
I enjoy to swim. → I enjoy swimming.
enjoy 後面一定接 V-ing，不能接 to V。
2
He decided going abroad. → He decided to go abroad.
decide 後面一定接 to V。
3
I look forward to see you. → I look forward to seeing you.
look forward to 的 to 是介系詞，後面接 V-ing。
4
I stopped to smoke last year. (戒菸) → I stopped smoking last year.
停止做某件事，用 stop V-ing。
5
I am used to get up early. → I am used to getting up early.
be used to 的 to 是介系詞，後面接 V-ing。

快問快答 8 題

1She avoided ____ to him. (talk / talking)talking
2We can't afford ____ a car. (buy / to buy)to buy
3He stopped ____ (戒菸). (smoking / to smoke)smoking
4Remember ____ the door. (lock / to lock)to lock
5I forgot ____ her yesterday (沒見到). (meeting / to meet)to meet
6He seems ____ forgotten my name. (to have / having)to have
7I am used to ____ early. (get up / getting up)getting up
8I ____ play baseball when I was young. (used to / am used to)used to

挑錯練習與句型轉換

挑錯：每句有一個錯

1I finished to do my homework.to do → doing
2She refused answering the question.answering → to answer
3I'm looking forward to meet you.meet → meeting

轉換

1I like swimming.（改用 to V）I like to swim.
2I regret that I said that.（用 regret + V-ing）I regret saying that.
3The letter must be sent.（用 needs）The letter needs to be sent.

在閱讀與聽力裡找到它

Ben used to eat junk food every day. Last year, he decided to change his habits. He stopped drinking soda and started to exercise. “I look forward to feeling healthier,” he said. “I am used to waking up early now.”

閱讀怎麼用

  • 看到 decide、refuse、manage，後面接 to V
  • 看到 enjoy、avoid、finish，後面接 V-ing
  • stop、remember 後面的形式會改變意思

聽力怎麼用

  • 聽到 stopped smoking，是已經戒菸
  • 聽到 look forward to，後面的動詞會加 -ing
  • 聽到 used to，說的是過去的習慣

口說句型跟讀

先按 🔊 聽，再跟著大聲念，最後換成自己的習慣。

I enjoy playing video games.
→ 只接 V-ing
I decided to study harder.
→ 只接 to V
I stopped eating junk food.
→ stop + V-ing
I look forward to seeing you.
→ 介系詞 to
I used to play baseball.
→ used to V

重點整理與測驗說明

類型重點
enjoy 類只接 V-ing
decide 類只接 to V
stop / remember / forgetV-ing 做過了；to V 要去做
to have p.p. / to be p.p.已經發生／被
look forward to / be used toto 是介系詞，接 V-ing
口訣：mind finish enjoy avoid consider 接 V-ing；decide plan hope want 接 to V；介系詞 to 後面接 V-ing。
