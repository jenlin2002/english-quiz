WEEK 18 · GRAMMAR

主詞動詞一致、代名詞與限定詞

Agreement, Pronouns & Determiners
Everyone is…the other / others

本週目標與暖身

本週目標

  • 判斷主詞是單數還是複數：each、every、everyone、a number of、the number of
  • 主詞後面有 with、as well as、along with 時，動詞看前面的主詞
  • 金額、時間、距離、科目名稱當成單數；the + 形容詞當成複數
  • 分清楚 another、the other、others、the others，以及 one / ones
  • 數量詞：few / a few、little / a little、both / all / either / neither / none

暖身 3 題

1Everyone ____ here. (is / are)is
2A number of students ____ absent. (was / were)were
3I have two pens. One is red, and ____ is blue. (the other / another)the other

回收：第 17 週的倒裝與強調

倒裝句的主詞跑到動詞後面，更要看清楚主詞是單數還是複數。

第 17 週回顧

Never have I seen such a big dog.
→ 否定副詞倒裝
It was my mother who cooked dinner.
→ 強調句

這週的延伸

Here comes the bus. Here come the students.
→ 動詞看後面的主詞：bus 單數、students 複數
Each of the students has a book.
→ each 當主詞，動詞用單數

一定用單數的主詞

each、every、-one、-body、-thing

看起來很多，其實是「每一個」

單數：each、every + 名詞、everyone、somebody、nothing…

each of + 複數名詞：動詞還是單數

Everyone is ready for the trip.
→ 每個人都準備好了
Each of the students has a laptop.
→ 每個學生都有一台筆電
Every boy and girl needs love.
→ every A and B 也用單數

金額、時間、距離、學科

當成一個整體

一個整體：Ten dollars、Two hours、Five kilometers → 單數

-s 結尾但是單數：news、mathematics、physics

Ten dollars is too much for a pen.
→ 十元買一支筆太貴了
Two hours is enough for the test.
→ 兩小時考試夠了
The news was surprising.
→ news 是不可數名詞

一定用複數，或要看情況的主詞

主詞動詞例句
a number of + 複數名詞複數（很多）
A number of students are absent today. （今天很多學生缺席）
the number of + 複數名詞單數（數目）
The number of students is forty. （學生的人數是四十人）
the + 形容詞複數（一群人）
The rich are not always happy. （有錢人不一定快樂）
police、people、cattle複數
The police are looking for the thief. （警察正在找小偷）
family、team、class當整體用單數
My family is large. （我家是個大家庭）
口訣：a number of 是「很多」→ 複數；the number of 是「數目」→ 單數。

主詞後面有修飾語：動詞看「真正的主詞」

with、as well as、along with

插入語不影響動詞

規則：動詞看前面的主詞，後面的人不算

The teacher, as well as the students, is excited.
→ 主詞是 the teacher
Tom, along with his friends, was late.
→ 主詞是 Tom
The box of apples is heavy.
→ 主詞是 the box，不是 apples

就近原則（第 16 週回收）

看最靠近動詞的那一個

either…or / neither…nor：動詞看最近的主詞

There is / are：動詞看後面第一個名詞

Neither my parents nor my sister is at home.
→ 看 my sister
There is a pen and two books on the desk.
→ 看 a pen
There are two books and a pen on the desk.
→ 看 two books

another、the other、others、the others

用法意思例句
one…the other兩個中的一個…另一個
I have two cats. One is white, and the other is black. （我有兩隻貓，一隻白的，另一隻黑的）
another（不特定的）另一個
This cake is great. Can I have another? （這蛋糕很好吃，可以再一個嗎？）
some…others一些…另一些（不是全部）
Some students like math; others like English. （有些學生喜歡數學，有些喜歡英文）
some…the others一些…其餘全部
Five boys came. Two stayed, and the others left. （五個男生來了，兩個留下，其餘的離開）
有 the 就是「剩下的全部」：兩個用 the other，三個以上剩下的全部用 the others。

代名詞 one / ones、it 與反身代名詞

one / ones 與 it

同一個還是同一類

one / ones：同類的另一個（複數用 ones）

it：就是「那一個」（同一個）

I lost my umbrella. I need to buy a new one.
→ 買一把新的（不是同一把）
I lost my umbrella, but I found it later.
→ 找到的是同一把
I don't like the red shoes. I like the black ones.
→ ones = shoes

反身代名詞與 each other

主詞和受詞是同一人

反身：myself、yourself、himself、themselves…

each other：彼此（兩者以上都可以）

She taught herself to play the guitar.
→ 她自學吉他
Help yourself to some cake.
→ 自己拿蛋糕吃
The two friends help each other.
→ 兩個朋友互相幫忙

限定詞：few、little、both、all、either、neither、none

few / a few、little / a little

有 a 正面、沒 a 負面

可數：a few（一些）、few（幾乎沒有）

不可數：a little（一點）、little（幾乎沒有）

I have a few friends here.
→ 我在這裡有幾個朋友（正面）
Few people came to the party.
→ 幾乎沒什麼人來（負面）
There is little milk left.
→ 幾乎沒剩牛奶了

兩個 vs 三個以上

先數一數有幾個

兩個：both（都）、either（任一）、neither（都不）

三個以上：all（都）、any（任一）、none（都不）

Both of my parents are teachers.
→ 兩個都
Neither of the answers is correct.
→ 兩個都不對（neither of + 複數，動詞常用單數）
None of the five students was late.
→ 五個學生中沒有人遲到

對比頁：容易搞混的四組

a number of 與 the number of

A number of cars are on the road.
→ 很多車，複數
The number of cars is growing.
→ 車的數目，單數

the other 與 another

One is mine; the other is yours.
→ 兩個中剩下的那個
Can I have another cookie?
→ 再一個（不特定）

a few 與 few

I have a few questions.
→ 我有幾個問題
Few students knew the answer.
→ 幾乎沒有學生知道

one 與 it

I need a pen. Can you lend me one?
→ 任何一支筆
Where is my pen? I can't find it.
→ 就是我那支筆

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
Everyone are happy today. → Everyone is happy today.
everyone 是單數，動詞用 is。
2
The number of students are forty. → The number of students is forty.
the number of 指「數目」，動詞用單數。
3
The teacher, as well as the students, are here. → The teacher, as well as the students, is here.
as well as 不影響動詞，主詞是 the teacher。
4
I have two sisters. One is a nurse, and another is a cook. → I have two sisters. One is a nurse, and the other is a cook.
兩個中的另一個用 the other。
5
There is little apples in the box. → There are few apples in the box.
apples 可數，要用 few，動詞也要用 are。

挑錯練習與句型轉換

挑錯：每句有一個錯

1Five hundred dollars are too expensive.are → is
2Tom, along with his parents, are coming.are → is
3None of the two answers is correct.None → Neither

依提示改寫

1Many students are absent.（用 A number of）A number of students are absent.
2I have three pens: one red pen and two blue pens.（用 the others）One is red, and the others are blue.
3There are almost no people in the park.（用 few）There are few people in the park.

在閱讀與聽力裡找到它

The number of students who walk to school is falling. A number of parents drive their children every day. Each of these trips adds to the traffic. Some families have started walking groups; others share cars. Neither idea is perfect, but both help.

閱讀怎麼用

  • 先找真正的主詞，跳過 of…、as well as…、with… 這些修飾語
  • The number of 常出現在報告和圖表題
  • some…others 表示兩種不同的人，題目常考「其他人做什麼」

聽力怎麼用

  • 聽到 a few / a little 是「有一些」，few / little 是「幾乎沒有」
  • 聽到 neither / none，表示都沒有
  • 聽到 the other，就是兩個中的另一個

口說句型跟讀

先按 🔊 聽，再跟著大聲念，最後換成自己的句子。

Everyone in my family loves music.
→ everyone + 單數動詞
A number of my classmates play basketball.
→ a number of + 複數動詞
I have two hobbies. One is reading, and the other is swimming.
→ one…the other
Some of my friends like rock music; others like pop.
→ some…others
I have a few good friends, and we help each other.
→ a few、each other

重點整理與測驗說明

重點規則
單數主詞each、every、-one、-body、金額、時間、news、學科
複數主詞a number of、the + 形容詞、police、people
插入語as well as、with、along with 不影響動詞
the other / others有 the 是剩下全部；兩個用 the other
one / itone 是同類，it 是同一個
few / little有 a 正面、沒 a 負面；few 可數、little 不可數
口訣：找主詞、跳插入；每一個用單數；剩下全部加 the；有 a 是有、沒 a 是沒有。
