回收：第 18 週的主詞動詞一致

英檢文法題最常考的陷阱，就是「主詞和動詞」離很遠。

第 18 週回顧

The number of students is growing.
→ the number of + 單數動詞
The teacher, as well as the students, is here.
→ 插入語不影響動詞

這週的延伸

The books that my teacher gave me are interesting.
→ 跳過關係子句，主詞是 books
Walking to school every day is good for you.
→ 動名詞當主詞，用單數

文法題四步驟診斷法

四步驟

Four-Step Check

1. 看空格：空格前後是什麼詞性？缺主詞、動詞還是修飾語？

2. 數動詞：一個句子只有一個主要動詞；多一個動詞就要連接詞或關代

3. 找時間：yesterday、since、by the time、next week → 決定時態

4. 看連接：有沒有 although、if、who、that、however？

示範

Example
By the time we arrived, the movie had started.
→ 步驟 3：by the time + 過去 → 過去完成式
The girl sitting next to me is my cousin.
→ 步驟 2：已經有動詞 is，sitting 是分詞修飾
Although he was tired, he kept working.
→ 步驟 4：前後是兩個子句，要連接詞

診斷 1：動詞形式（第 2–4、9 週）

考點線索例句
完成進行式for、since、all day
I have been waiting for an hour. （我已經等了一個小時）
過去完成式by the time、before + 過去
The train had left before we got there. （我們到之前火車就開走了）
被動語態主詞是「被」做的
The bridge was built in 1990. （這座橋建於 1990 年）
使役動詞have / make / let + O + V
My mom made me clean my room. （我媽叫我打掃房間）
V-ing / to V動詞後面接哪一種
I enjoy reading, and I hope to travel. （我喜歡閱讀，也希望去旅行）

診斷 2：假設與情態（第 6–8 週）

考點公式例句
推測過去must / can't have + p.p.
He must have forgotten the meeting. （他一定是忘了開會）
後悔should have + p.p.
I should have studied harder. （我當初應該更用功）
與現在相反If + 過去式, would + V
If I had more time, I would learn Japanese. （如果我有更多時間，我會學日文）
與過去相反If + had p.p., would have p.p.
If you had asked, I would have helped. （如果你當時有問，我就會幫忙）
wish / as ifwish + 過去式 / had p.p.
I wish I were taller. （真希望我高一點）

診斷 3：子句與連接（第 11–14 週）

考點判斷方法例句
關係代名詞先行詞是人 / 物；有沒有逗號
My aunt, who lives in Tainan, is a nurse. （我住在台南的阿姨是護理師）
what / that前面有沒有先行詞
What he said is true. （他說的是真的）
間接問句疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞
Do you know where he lives? （你知道他住哪裡嗎？）
分詞構句主動 V-ing / 被動 p.p.
Seen from the hill, the city looks beautiful. （從山上看，這座城市很美）
連接詞 / 轉折語子句、名詞，還是分號後
It rained; however, we still went out. （下雨了，但我們還是出門了）

診斷 4：形容詞、比較、倒裝、一致（第 1、16–18 週）

考點判斷方法例句
-ed / -ing 形容詞人感到 -ed；事物令人 -ing
The movie was boring, so I felt bored. （電影很無聊，所以我覺得很無聊）
as…as / 比較級中間原級；比較級用 much 加強
This road is much wider than that one. （這條路比那條寬得多）
the 比較級兩邊都要 the
The earlier you leave, the sooner you arrive. （你越早出發就越早到）
否定倒裝否定副詞在句首
Rarely does she complain. （她很少抱怨）
強調句It is / was…that
It was in the library that I met her. （我是在圖書館遇見她的）
主詞動詞一致找真正的主詞
Each of the rooms has a window. （每個房間都有一扇窗）

閱讀題型策略

四種常見題目

Reading Strategies

主旨題：What is the passage mainly about? → 看第一段和最後一段

細節題：According to the passage… → 回原文找關鍵字

推論題：What can we infer…? → 找證據，不要憑感覺

字義題：The word "…" means… → 看前後文

實際示範

找線索
Although the plan sounded perfect, few people signed up.
→ 推論：這個計畫其實不成功
The museum, which opened in 2010, attracts many tourists.
→ 細節：博物館 2010 年開幕
看到 however、but、although 後面，常常就是答案所在。

聽力題型策略

看圖辨義與問答

Part 1 & 2

看圖辨義：先看圖：人在哪裡、做什麼、有幾個

問答：聽清楚第一個字：Who、When、Why、How long

How long have you lived here? — For five years.
→ How long → 一段時間
Why are you late? — Because I missed the bus.
→ Why → 原因

簡短對話

Part 3

先看選項：猜會問人、地點、時間還是原因

注意轉折：but、actually、however 後面常是答案

I wanted to take the bus, but actually I walked.
→ 答案是 walked
Not until Friday will the package arrive.
→ 答案是 Friday

學生最常錯的 5 句（全期總整理）

1
I have lived here since five years. → I have lived here for five years.
一段時間用 for，時間點用 since。（第 2 週）
2
If I was you, I will study harder. → If I were you, I would study harder.
與現在事實相反：If + were, would + V。（第 7、8 週）
3
Do you know where does he live? → Do you know where he lives?
間接問句用直述語序。（第 13 週）
4
Although it rained, but we went out. → Although it rained, we went out.
although 和 but 不能同時用。（第 14 週）
5
The number of cars are increasing. → The number of cars is increasing.
the number of + 單數動詞。（第 18 週）

口說：回答問題與看圖敘述的架構

回答問題：三句法

Answer + Reason + Example

1. 直接回答：Yes, I do. / My favorite… is…

2. 給理由：because…、The reason is that…

3. 舉例或感受：For example…、That's why…

My favorite place is the library because it is quiet. For example, I often read there on weekends.
→ 回答 + 理由 + 例子

看圖敘述：由大到小

Where → Who → What → Guess

1. 地點：This picture shows…、In this picture, …

2. 人物動作：There are…、A girl is…

3. 推測：They might be…、It seems that…

In this picture, some students are eating lunch. They seem to be enjoying their food.
→ 地點 + 動作 + 推測

口說句型跟讀

先按 🔊 聽，再跟著大聲念，最後換成自己的句子。

I have been learning English for six years.
→ 完成進行式
If I had more free time, I would learn to play the piano.
→ 與現在相反的假設
The person who helps me the most is my mother.
→ 關係代名詞
The more I practice, the more confident I feel.
→ the 比較級
This picture shows a girl standing next to a school bus.
→ 看圖敘述開頭
