分詞構句、連接詞與轉折語

Participle Clauses & Linking Words
Having finished…however…

本週目標與暖身

本週目標

  • 把副詞子句改成分詞構句：V-ing、p.p.、having p.p.
  • 分辨 although、though、even though、whereas、while
  • 分辨 despite、in spite of 和 although，以及 so that、in order to
  • 用 however、therefore、moreover、otherwise 連接兩句話

暖身 3 題

1____ the news, she cried. (Hearing / Heard)Hearing
2____ it was raining, we went out. (Although / Despite)Although
3It was late; ____, we went home. (therefore / however)therefore

回收：第 13 週的名詞子句與轉述

這週把「兩個句子」變成「一個更有層次的句子」。

第 13 週回顧

She asked me whether I had finished.
→ 名詞子句：轉述問句
The teacher told us to be quiet.
→ 轉述命令：tell + 人 + to V

這週的延伸

Having finished my homework, I went out.
→ 分詞構句：省略連接詞與主詞
I was tired; however, I kept working.
→ 轉折語：連接兩個完整句子

分詞構句：V-ing（主動）

改寫步驟

副詞子句 → 分詞構句

1.去掉連接詞（when、while、because、after…）

2.主詞和主句相同 → 去掉主詞

3.動詞改成 V-ing

While I was walking down the street, I saw an accident.
→ 完整版
Walking down the street, I saw an accident.
→ 分詞構句：走在街上時，我看到一場意外

更多例句

Hearing the news, she burst into tears.
→ 聽到消息，她哭了出來（= When she heard…）
Feeling tired, he went to bed early.
→ 覺得累，他很早就睡了（= Because he felt…）

分詞構句：p.p.（被動）與 being 的省略

被動：p.p.

主詞是被動作的對象

公式：(Being) + 過去分詞

being：常常省略

Written in simple English, the book is easy to read.
→ 用簡單英文寫成，這本書很容易讀（= Because it is written…）
Surprised by the noise, he jumped up.
→ 被噪音嚇到，他跳了起來

being 保留的情況

Being a doctor, she knows a lot about health.
→ 身為醫生，她懂很多健康知識（名詞要保留 being）
Being tired, he went to bed.
→ 累了，他去睡了（形容詞可省略 being）

完成式分詞構句與否定

having p.p.

比主句動詞更早發生

公式：Having + 過去分詞

意思：「已經…之後」

Having finished his homework, he went out to play.
→ 寫完功課後，他出去玩（= After he had finished…）
Having lived here for ten years, she knows the town well.
→ 住在這裡十年了，她很了解這個小鎮

否定：not 放前面

公式：Not + V-ing / Not having p.p.

Not knowing the answer, he kept silent.
→ 不知道答案，他保持沉默
Not having studied, he failed the test.
→ 沒有念書，他考試沒過

分詞構句的陷阱：主詞要一致

規則

分詞構句的意思上的主詞 = 主句的主詞

檢查：問「誰在做這個動作？」

Walking down the street, a dog bit me.
→ × 走在街上的是我，不是狗
Walking down the street, I was bitten by a dog.
→ 走在街上，我被狗咬了

主詞不同時

主詞不同時，要保留自己的主詞，這叫獨立分詞構句，考試較少考。
The rain having stopped, we went out.
→ 雨停了，我們就出去了（兩個主詞不同）

although、though、even though、whereas、while

連接詞意思例句
although / though雖然（後面接子句）
Although it was raining, we went out. （雖然下雨，我們還是出去了）
even though即使、儘管（語氣更強）
He bought it even though he had little money. （即使沒什麼錢，他還是買了）
whereas / while然而、相對於（對比兩件事）
I like tea, whereas my sister prefers coffee. （我喜歡茶，而我妹妹喜歡咖啡）
although 後面不能再接 but：Although it was raining, but … 是錯的，用了 although 就不用 but。

despite、in spite of：後面接名詞

despite / in spite of

後面接名詞、V-ing、the fact that

意思：儘管…

注意：不能直接接子句

Despite the rain, we went out.
→ 儘管下雨，我們還是出去了
In spite of being tired, he kept working.
→ 儘管很累，他還是繼續工作

改寫對照

Although it was raining, we went out. (子句)
→ although + 子句
Despite the fact that it was raining, we went out.
→ despite + the fact that + 子句
Despite it was raining, we went out.
→ × despite 後面不能接子句

表示目的：so that 與 in order to

so that + 子句

主詞可以相同或不同

子句裡：常有 can、could、will、would

意思：為了…

He spoke slowly so that everyone could understand.
→ 他說得很慢，讓大家都聽得懂
I took an umbrella so that I wouldn't get wet.
→ 我帶了傘，這樣才不會淋溼

in order to + 原形

主詞必須相同

否定：in order not to V

She saved money in order to buy a new bike.
→ 她存錢為了買新腳踏車
He left early in order not to be late.
→ 他提早離開，為了不要遲到

轉折語：however、therefore、moreover、otherwise

轉折語意思例句
however然而（轉折）
It was late; however, we kept working. （很晚了，然而我們繼續工作）
therefore因此（結果）
It rained; therefore, the game was cancelled. （下雨了，因此比賽取消）
moreover而且（補充）
The room is small; moreover, it is dirty. （房間很小，而且很髒）
otherwise否則
Hurry up; otherwise, you'll be late. （快一點，否則你會遲到）
這些是副詞，不是連接詞：前面要用分號或句號分開，不能只用逗號把兩個句子接起來。

對比頁：容易搞混的四組

although 與 despite

Although it rained, we went out.
→ 後面接子句
Despite the rain, we went out.
→ 後面接名詞

so that 與 in order to

He studied so that he could pass.
→ 子句，主詞可不同
He studied in order to pass.
→ 後面接原形，主詞相同

however 與 although

It rained; however, we went out.
→ however 是副詞，前面用分號
Although it rained, we went out.
→ although 是連接詞，連接兩個子句

Having p.p. 與 V-ing

Having eaten, he left.
→ 吃完之後才離開（較早）
Eating lunch, he read.
→ 邊吃邊讀（同時）

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
Although it rained, but we went out. → Although it rained, we went out.
although 和 but 不能同時用。
2
Despite it was raining, we went out. → Despite the rain, we went out.
despite 後面要接名詞或 V-ing，不能接子句。
3
Walking down the street, a dog bit me. → Walking down the street, I was bitten by a dog.
分詞構句的主詞要和主句主詞相同。
4
It was late, however we kept working. → It was late; however, we kept working.
however 前面要用分號或句號。
5
She saved money in order she can buy a bike. → She saved money in order to buy a bike.
in order 後面接 to V，不接子句。

挑錯練習與句型轉換

挑錯：每句有一個錯

1Although he was tired, but he kept working.刪掉 but
2In spite of he was sick, he went to school.he was sick → being sick / his illness
3It rained all day, however we stayed home.however 前面改分號或句號

改寫成分詞構句

1When she heard the news, she cried.Hearing the news, she cried.
2After he had finished his homework, he went out.Having finished his homework, he went out.
3Because he didn't know the answer, he kept silent.Not knowing the answer, he kept silent.

在閱讀與聽力裡找到它

Although many students dislike uniforms, supporters say they save money; moreover, students feel equal. Having worn uniforms for years, many graduates miss the simple morning. Critics, however, say uniforms limit self-expression. Despite the low cost, some families complain.

閱讀怎麼用

  • however、but 之後常是作者或另一方的重點
  • moreover、therefore 是在加強或總結前一句
  • 句首的 V-ing 或 p.p. 是分詞構句，要先找主句主詞

聽力怎麼用

  • 聽到 however，後面常和前面意思相反
  • 聽到 although、despite，後面才是重點
  • 聽到 therefore，後面是結果

口說句型跟讀

先按 🔊 聽，再跟著大聲念，最後換成自己的句子。

Having finished my homework, I watched TV.
→ 分詞構句
Although it was difficult, I finished the project.
→ although
Despite the rain, we went to the park.
→ despite
I study English in order to travel around the world.
→ in order to
Homework is useful; however, it takes too much time.
→ however

重點整理與測驗說明

重點規則
分詞構句去連接詞與相同主詞：V-ing、p.p.、having p.p.
although / even though接子句；不和 but 同時用
despite / in spite of接名詞或 V-ing
so that / in order toso that + 子句；in order to + 原形
however / therefore副詞，前面用分號或句號
口訣：連接詞接子句，despite 接名詞；however 前面要分號；分詞的主詞要跟主句一樣。
