WEEK 3 · GRAMMAR

未來進行式、未來完成式與時態一致

Future Continuous, Future Perfect & Tense Agreement
will be V-ingwill have p.p.

本週目標與暖身

本週目標

  • 用 will be V-ing 說明未來某一刻正在做的事
  • 用 will have p.p. 說明到某個時間之前會完成的事
  • when、if 等子句裡用現在式代替未來式
  • 主句是過去式時，子句的時態怎麼配合

暖身 3 題

1This time tomorrow, I ____ on a beach. (will lie / will be lying)will be lying
2By next June, she ____ her degree. (will finish / will have finished)will have finished
3I will call you when I ____ home. (get / will get)get

三個「未來」的時間軸

will V 是一個點；will be V-ing 是「那一刻正在做」；will have p.p. 是「那一刻之前已經做完」。

will V

Simple future
I will call you tomorrow.
→ 我明天會打給你

will be V-ing

Future continuous
I will be studying at 8 p.m.
→ 明天晚上八點，我會正在讀書

will have p.p.

Future perfect
I will have finished by June.
→ 到六月之前，我就會完成了

未來進行式：will be V-ing

意思

Future Continuous

強調：未來某個時間點，動作正在進行

常搭配：this time tomorrow、at 8 p.m. tonight、this time next week

例句

This time tomorrow, I will be lying on a beach.
→ 明天這個時候，我會正躺在海灘上
At 8 p.m. tonight, we will be eating dinner.
→ 今晚八點，我們會正在吃晚餐
Don't call me at nine. I will be sleeping.
→ 九點別打給我，我會在睡覺

未來完成式：will have p.p.

意思

Future Perfect

強調：到未來某個時間之前，已經完成

常搭配：by + 未來時間、by the time + 現在式

例句

By next June, she will have finished her degree.
→ 到明年六月，她就會拿到學位了
By the time you arrive, I will have finished cleaning.
→ 你到的時候，我已經打掃完了
Next year, he will have worked here for ten years.
→ 明年，他就在這裡工作滿十年了

時間與條件副詞子句：用現在式代替未來式

連接詞子句裡用現在式主要子句
when / as soon as
When I get home,
→ 我到家的時候
I will call you.
before / after / until
She will wait until you come.
→ 她會等到你來
（until 後面用現在式）
if / unless
If it rains tomorrow,
→ 如果明天下雨
we will stay home.
不能在 when、if 的子句裡用 will：When I will get home → When I get home

時態一致：主句是過去式

規則

Sequence of tenses

主句是過去式：後面的子句也要往過去推一格

現在 → 過去：is → was、can → could、will → would

He says he is tired.
→ 他說他累了（說的當下）
He said he was tired.
→ 他說他累了（過去說的）

更多例句

I thought you were at home.
→ 我以為你在家
She told me she would buy a new bike.
→ 她告訴我她會買一輛新腳踏車
They said they could come.
→ 他們說他們可以來

時態一致的例外：事實與諺語

永遠成立的事實

不用往後推：科學事實、自然現象、諺語、現在仍然成立的事

The teacher said that the earth goes around the sun.
→ 老師說地球繞著太陽轉
He told me that he lives in Taipei.
→ 他告訴我他住在台北（現在還住）

對比

She said she was tired.
→ 她說她累了（當時的狀態）
She said that water boils at 100°C.
→ 她說水在攝氏一百度沸騰（永遠成立）

對比頁：容易搞混的三組

will V 與 will be V-ing

I will call you at 8.
→ 八點我會打給你（做一件事）
I will be calling clients at 8.
→ 八點我會正在打電話給客戶（動作進行中）

will be V-ing 與 will have p.p.

At 5, I will be working.
→ 五點時我正在工作
By 5, I will have finished.
→ 五點之前我就完成了

when 子句

I will call when I arrive.
→ 我到了就會打給你
when I will arrive
→ × 子句裡不用 will

says 與 said

He says he is tired.
→ 現在說，現在累
He said he was tired.
→ 當時說，當時累

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
I will call you when I will arrive. → I will call you when I arrive.
時間副詞子句裡用現在式，不用 will。
2
If it will rain tomorrow, we stay home. → If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home.
if 子句用現在式，主要子句才用 will。
3
By next June, she will finish her degree. → By next June, she will have finished her degree.
by 加未來時間，表示到那之前已完成，用 will have p.p.。
4
He said that he is tired. → He said that he was tired.
主句 said 是過去式，子句也要往過去推成 was。
5
The teacher said the earth went around the sun. → The teacher said the earth goes around the sun.
永遠成立的事實，不用往後推。

挑錯練習與句型轉換

挑錯：每句有一個錯

1I will visit you when I will have time.when I will have → when I have
2By next year, he will work here for ten years.will work → will have worked
3She said that she will come.will → would

轉換

1I will study at nine tomorrow.（強調那時正在做）I will be studying at nine tomorrow.
2She finishes the work before five.（用 will have p.p.）She will have finished the work before five.
3"I am tired," he said.（轉述）He said that he was tired.

在閱讀與聽力裡找到它

By 2040, scientists will have invented cars that drive themselves. Many people will be working from home. When you get into the car, it will take you to work.

閱讀怎麼用

  • 看到 by + 年份，想 will have p.p.
  • 看到 at this time、at 8 p.m.，想 will be V-ing
  • when、if 後面是現在式，但意思是未來

聽力怎麼用

  • 聽到 will be 加 -ing，表示那個時間正在做
  • 聽到 by 加時間，答案常是「在那之前已完成」
  • 聽到 said that，注意後面的時態往後推了

口說句型跟讀

先按 🔊 聽，再跟著大聲念，最後換成自己的計畫。

This time tomorrow, I will be studying English.
→ 未來進行式
In ten years, I will be working as a doctor.
→ 十年後的自己
By the end of this year, I will have finished my book.
→ 未來完成式
When I get home, I will do my homework.
→ when 子句用現在式
My teacher said that she was very proud of us.
→ 時態一致

重點整理與測驗說明

句型公式重點
未來進行式will be + V-ing未來某一刻正在做
未來完成式will have + p.p.到某時之前已完成
時間、條件子句when / if + 現在式不用 will
時態一致said + 過去式事實不用往後推
口訣：那一刻正在做用 will be V-ing，那之前已做完用 will have p.p.；when 與 if 後面不放 will。
