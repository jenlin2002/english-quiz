PART 1

兩種片語動詞

片語動詞＝動詞 ＋ 副詞 或 動詞 ＋ 介系詞，整組當成一個動詞，意思常常和原本的動詞差很多：give（給）→ give up（放棄）。

兩種最大的差別在於受詞放哪裡：

動詞 ＋ 副詞（可分開）動詞 ＋ 介系詞（不可分開）
例子turn on, pick up, give uplook at, listen to, wait for
受詞是名詞turn on the light ✓
turn the light on ✓		look at the bird ✓
look the bird at ✗
受詞是代名詞turn it on ✓
turn on it ✗		look at it ✓
look it at ✗
口訣：副詞型「代名詞夾中間」；介系詞型「受詞永遠在後面」。
PART 2

動詞 ＋ 副詞（可分開）

片語意思例句（注意 it / them 的位置）
turn on / off打開／關掉（電器）It's dark. Turn the light on. → Turn it on.
turn up / down開大聲／關小聲The music is too loud. Please turn it down.
put on / take off穿上／脫掉It's cold. Put your coat on. → Put it on.
pick up撿起；接（人）Dad will pick me up at five.
put down放下；寫下Put your pencils down, please.
put away收好Put your toys away before dinner.
give up放棄；戒掉Don't give up your dream. Don't give it up.
give back歸還I'll give the book back tomorrow.
find out查出、發現Let's find out the answer. → find it out
look up（在字典、網路）查Look the word up in a dictionary.
make up編造（故事、藉口）He made up an excuse.
call up打電話給I'll call Amy up tonight. → call her up
call off取消They called the game off because of the rain.
put off延後Don't put off your homework.
hand in / out繳交／分發Please hand in your homework. The teacher handed out the tests.
wake up叫醒Please wake me up at six.
throw away丟掉Don't throw the bottles away. Recycle them.
try on試穿Can I try these shoes on?
沒有受詞的也很多：get up（起床）、grow up（長大）、come back（回來）、go out（出門）、sit down（坐下）、stand up（站起來）、hurry up（快一點），這些後面不接受詞。

片語意思例句

look for尋找I'm looking for my keys. Have you seen them?
look after照顧Can you look after my cat this weekend?
listen to聽I like listening to music.
wait for等待We're waiting for the bus.
talk about談論What are you talking about?
think about / of考慮；想到I'm thinking about your idea.
belong to屬於This bag belongs to Lisa.
take care of照顧She takes care of her little brother.
get on / off上／下（公車、火車）Get off the bus at the next stop.
call on拜訪（人）We called on our teacher last week.

PART 4

三個字的片語動詞（不可分開）

片語意思例句
run out of用完We've run out of milk.
look forward to期待（to 後接 V-ing）I look forward to seeing you.
get along with和…相處Do you get along with your classmates?
catch up with趕上Walk faster, or we can't catch up with them.

PART 5

容易混淆的幾組

比較例句
look at / look for / look up / look afterLook at me.（看）／ I'm looking for my phone.（找）／ Look it up.（查）／ Look after the baby.（照顧）
put on / take offPut on your shoes.（穿上）／ Take off your shoes.（脫掉）
put on / wearPut on your coat.（穿上的「動作」）／ She is wearing a red coat.（穿著的「狀態」）
turn up / turn downTurn it up.（開大聲）／ Turn it down.（關小聲；也有「拒絕」的意思）
put off / call offPut off the meeting.（延後，之後還會開）／ Call off the meeting.（取消，不開了）
hand in / hand outHand in your work.（交上去）／ Hand out the papers.（發下去）
SUMMARY

句型整理表

類型名詞當受詞代名詞當受詞
動詞 ＋ 副詞pick up the pen ／ pick the pen uppick it up（只能這樣）
動詞 ＋ 介系詞look for the penlook for it
動詞 ＋ 副詞 ＋ 介系詞run out of paperrun out of it
不接受詞get up, grow up, come back, sit down, hurry up

怎麼知道是副詞型還是介系詞型？先背常用的：on、off、up、down、away、back、out 在這些片語裡多半是副詞（可分開）；at、for、to、after、of、with 是介系詞（不可分開）。

COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

The light is on. Please turn off it.Please turn it off.副詞型片語，代名詞要夾在中間。
This is your jacket. Put on it.Put it on.同上，put on 是副詞型。
I lost my wallet. I'm looking it for.I'm looking for it.look for 是介系詞型，受詞放後面。
I'm listening music.I'm listening to music.listen 後面要有 to 才能接受詞。
We are waiting the bus.We are waiting for the bus.wait for ＋ 受詞。
She is putting on a red dress at the party.She is wearing a red dress at the party.「穿著」的狀態用 wear；put on 是「穿上」的動作。
The game was put off. We will never play it.The game was called off.不再舉行是「取消」call off；put off 是延後。
