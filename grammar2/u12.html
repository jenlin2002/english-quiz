介系詞

放在名詞前面，說明「在哪裡、什麼時候、怎麼去、和誰、關於什麼」

PART 1

介系詞的基本規則

介系詞後面接名詞、代名詞（受格）或 V-ing，組成「介系詞片語」。

She sat next to me.代名詞用受格 me（不是 I）
Thank you for coming.接動作用 V-ing（見 Unit 3）
PART 2

地方：in、at、on

介系詞想像成例子
in在一個空間、範圍「裡面」in the classroom, in the box, in the park, in Taipei, in Taiwan
at在一個「點」（地址、某個地方）at the bus stop, at the door, at home, at school, at 25 Main Street
on在一條「線」或一個「面」上on the wall, on the floor, on the table, on the beach, on Main Street
I'll meet you at the station.車站當作一個碰面的「點」
There's a picture on the wall.貼在牆「面」上
交通工具：可以站著走動的大型交通工具用 on：on the bus, on the train, on the plane；小型的用 in：in a car, in a taxi。

不加介系詞的地方副詞

go home, come here, go there, go downtown, go abroad

Let's go home.（不說 go to home；但 at home 要用 at）
PART 3

位置與方向

介系詞意思例句
in front of在…前面I'll wait in front of the bookstore.
behind在…後面The cat is hiding behind the sofa.
next to / beside / by在…旁邊Come and sit next to me.
near在…附近We live near the beach.
between A and B在（兩者）之間The bank is between the post office and the school.
among在（三者以上）之中She is popular among her classmates.
across from / opposite在…對面The library is across from the park.
under / over / above在…下面／正上方／上方The dog is under the table.
into / out of進入／從…出來（動作）He ran into the room. / She got out of the car.
across / through / along橫越（表面）／穿過（空間）／沿著walk across the street / through the forest / along the river
to / toward(s)到／朝向walk to school / run toward the door
in 和 into：in 是「在裡面」（狀態）；into 是「進到裡面」（移動）。The fish is in the water. / The boy jumped into the water.
PART 4

時間：at、on、in

介系詞用在例子
at時刻、一天中的某個點、節日（整段期間）at 7:30, at noon, at night, at midnight, at Christmas
on某一天、星期幾、日期on Monday, on May 5, on my birthday, on Christmas Day, on Sunday morning
in月、季節、年、早上／下午／晚上、一段時間之後in July, in summer, in 2026, in the morning, in two days
口訣：由小到大「at 點、on 天、in 長」。
特別注意：in the morning，但 on Sunday morning（指定某一天就用 on）。
這些前面不加介系詞：this、next、last、every、yesterday、today、tomorrow
I'll see you next week.（不說 in next week）　We met last Friday.（不說 on last Friday）
PART 5

for、since、during、until、by、before、after

介系詞意思例句
for ＋ 一段時間持續多久I've lived here for five years.
since ＋ 起點自從I've lived here since 2021.
during ＋ 期間在…期間Don't talk during the test.
from … to …從…到…The shop is open from 9 to 5.
until (till)一直到（持續）I waited until six.
by在…之前（最晚期限）Hand in your report by Friday.
before / after在…之前／之後Brush your teeth before bed.
until 和 by：until 是動作「一直持續」到那時；by 是「最晚」在那時之前完成。
I'll stay here until five.（一直待到五點）　I'll be back by five.（五點前會回來）
PART 6

by、with、without、in、for、about、like

介系詞意思例句
by ＋ 交通工具搭乘（不加 a / the）by bus, by train, by bike, by car（但 on foot 走路）
with和…一起；帶著；用（工具）；有…特徵I live with my grandparents. / Cut it with scissors. / a girl with long hair
without沒有We can't live without water.
in ＋ 衣服／顏色／語言穿著；用（語言）the man in the black coat / Write it in English.
for為了；給；（目的）This gift is for you. / He works for a bank.
about關於a book about dinosaurs
like像She sings like a bird.
PART 7

片語中的介系詞（要整組背）

介系詞常見片語
tobe kind / nice / polite to, listen to, belong to, talk to
forbe ready for, be late for, wait for, look for, be famous for, thank … for
withbe angry with（人）, be patient with, be familiar with, be filled with, What's wrong with you?
ofbe afraid of, be full of, be proud of, be tired of, take care of
aboutbe curious about, be worried about, be excited about, talk about
atbe good at, be surprised at, look at, laugh at
inbe interested in, believe in, in time（及時）, in fact
onon time（準時）, on foot, depend on, on sale
in time 和 on time：on time ＝ 準時（剛好在那時）；in time ＝ 及時（還來得及）。
SUMMARY

句型整理表

用途重點例子
地方in 空間／at 點／on 面、線in the room / at the door / on the wall
時間at 時刻／on 天／in 月年季at 6 / on Monday / in May
不加介系詞this、next、last、every、homenext week / go home
持續for 一段時間／since 起點for two hours / since 2020
期限until 一直到／by 最晚wait until 5 / finish by 5
交通by ＋ 交通工具（無冠詞）／on footby bus / on foot
移動into／out of／across／throughwalk into the room
後面接名詞、受格、V-ingwith me / before eating
COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

I was born on 2012.I was born in 2012.年份用 in。
We have P.E. in Monday.We have P.E. on Monday.星期幾用 on。
See you in next week.See you next week.next、last、this、every 前面不加介系詞。
I go to school by a bus.I go to school by bus.by ＋ 交通工具，不加 a / the。
I walk to school by foot.I walk to school on foot.走路是 on foot。
Let's go to home.Let's go home.home 當副詞，go home 不加 to。
Sit between Tom and I.Sit between Tom and me.介系詞後面用受格。
He jumped in the pool from the side.He jumped into the pool.「進到裡面」的移動用 into。
Please finish it until Friday.Please finish it by Friday.最晚期限用 by；until 是持續到那時。
