PART 1

什麼是動名詞？

一個句子通常只有一個主要動詞。想再放進另一個「動作」時，要把它變成名詞：V-ing（動名詞）或 to V（不定詞，見 Unit 4）。

動名詞＝動詞 ＋ -ing，當名詞用，意思是「做某件事」。它可以放在名詞會出現的位置：

① 當主詞：動名詞當主詞時，動詞用單數。

Swimming in the sea is fun.在海裡游泳很好玩。（不是 are）

② 當補語（放在 be 動詞後面，說明主詞是什麼）

My hobby is collecting stamps.我的嗜好是收集郵票。

③ 當動詞的受詞

Ella enjoys baking cookies.Ella 喜歡烤餅乾。

④ 當介系詞的受詞

Thank you for helping me.謝謝你幫我。

⑤ 告示上的禁止：No ＋ V-ing

No parking. / No swimming.禁止停車。／禁止游泳。
分清楚：動名詞和現在進行式長得一樣，但進行式前面有 be 動詞、表示「正在做」。
He is reading.（正在讀＝進行式）　His job is reading news on TV.（工作是播新聞＝動名詞當補語）
PART 2

後面只能接 V-ing 的動詞

下面這些動詞後面如果要接另一個動作，只能用 V-ing，不能用 to V。它們多半是在講「已經在做、正在做、或做過的事」。

動詞意思例句
enjoy喜歡、享受We enjoy camping by the river.
finish做完Have you finished cleaning your room?
mind介意Do you mind opening the window?
practice練習She practices speaking English every day.
keep一直、持續The baby kept crying all night.
avoid避免Try to avoid eating too much sugar.
quit / give up戒掉、放棄My uncle quit smoking last year.
dislike不喜歡Tom dislikes getting up early.
admit / deny承認／否認He denied breaking the vase.
consider考慮We are considering moving to Taichung.
miss懷念；錯過I miss living near the beach.
suggest建議Dad suggested taking a taxi.
口訣：「享受完成、介意練習、一直避免、放棄討厭、承認否認、考慮懷念、建議」＋ V-ing。
PART 3

介系詞後面一律接 V-ing

in、on、at、of、for、about、without、before、after… 這些介系詞後面要接名詞，所以接動作時一定用 V-ing。

Wash your hands before eating.吃東西前要洗手。
She left without saying goodbye.她沒說再見就走了。

常考的「形容詞＋介系詞」

片語意思例句
be good at擅長Ken is good at drawing.
be interested in對…有興趣I'm interested in learning Japanese.
be tired of厭倦I'm tired of hearing the same song.
be afraid of害怕My sister is afraid of swimming in deep water.
be fond of喜歡Grandpa is fond of growing vegetables.
be worried about擔心She is worried about failing the test.
be busy (in)忙著Mom is busy cooking dinner.
be worth值得This museum is worth visiting.

特別注意：這些 to 是「介系詞」

下面片語裡的 to 不是不定詞，後面要接 V-ing：

I'm looking forward to seeing you.我很期待見到你。（不是 to see）
Amy is used to getting up at six.Amy 習慣六點起床。
The key to learning English is practice.學好英文的關鍵是練習。

be used to V-ing 和 used to V 不一樣

句型意思例句
be used to ＋ V-ing習慣於（現在的習慣）I am used to walking to school.
used to ＋ 原形以前常常（現在已經不會）I used to walk to school, but now I ride a bike.
PART 4

接 V-ing 的常用片語

片語意思例句
feel like ＋ V-ing想要I don't feel like going out tonight.
can't help ＋ V-ing忍不住We couldn't help laughing at his joke.
put off ＋ V-ing延後Don't put off doing your homework.
spend 時間／錢 (in) ＋ V-ing花時間／錢做…He spends two hours practicing the piano.
have fun / a good time (in) ＋ V-ing…得很開心We had fun playing in the snow.
have trouble / difficulty (in) ＋ V-ing做…有困難I have trouble remembering names.
How / What about ＋ V-ing?…如何？How about going hiking this Sunday?
It's no use ＋ V-ing…也沒有用It's no use crying about it now.
go ＋ V-ing去做（休閒活動）Let's go swimming / shopping / fishing.
PART 5

接 to V 和接 V-ing 意思不同的動詞

有些動詞兩種都能接，但意思不一樣。記住大原則：V-ing 偏向「已經做的事」，to V 偏向「還沒做、要去做的事」。

動詞＋ V-ing＋ to V
stopHe stopped talking.
停止（正在做的事）→ 他不說話了		He stopped to talk to me.
停下來「去做」另一件事 → 他停下來跟我說話
rememberI remember locking the door.
記得「做過」→ 我記得鎖過門了		Remember to lock the door.
記得「要去做」→ 記得要鎖門
forgetI'll never forget meeting you.
忘記「做過」→ 我永遠不會忘記遇見你		Don't forget to call Grandma.
忘記「要去做」→ 別忘了打給奶奶
tryTry adding some salt.
試試看（做了看結果）		I tried to open the box.
努力設法（不一定成功）
意思一樣的：like、love、hate、begin、start、continue 後面接 to V 或 V-ing 都可以，意思幾乎相同。It started raining. = It started to rain.　更多比較見 Unit 4。

COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

I enjoy to play basketball.I enjoy playing basketball.enjoy 後面只能接 V-ing。
Do you mind to close the door?Do you mind closing the door?mind 後面只能接 V-ing。
Playing video games are fun.Playing video games is fun.動名詞當主詞是「一件事」，動詞用單數。
She is good at swim.She is good at swimming.介系詞 at 後面接 V-ing。
I'm looking forward to see you.I'm looking forward to seeing you.look forward to 的 to 是介系詞，接 V-ing。
He spends a lot of time to play games.He spends a lot of time playing games.spend 時間 (in) ＋ V-ing。
I used to getting up early. （我以前常早起）I used to get up early.used to（以前常常）後面接原形；be used to（習慣）才接 V-ing。
Remember turning off the lights when you leave.Remember to turn off the lights when you leave.「記得要去做」還沒做的事，用 remember to V。
