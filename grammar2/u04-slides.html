UNIT 4 · GRAMMAR

不定詞

Infinitives
to V用途最廣

本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 用不定詞當名詞、形容詞、副詞
  • 記住後面接 to V 的動詞，和「動詞 + 人 + to V」
  • 用「疑問詞 + to V」說「該…」
  • 會用 It is…to V、too…to、enough to
  • 分清楚什麼時候用 to V、什麼時候用 V-ing

暖身 3 題

1I want ____ a pilot. (be / to be)to be
2Is there anything ____? (eat / to eat)to eat
3She went out ____ some bread. (buy / to buy)to buy

不定詞的三種用法

不定詞 = to + 原形動詞。to 後面一定是原形。

① 當名詞

做某件事

做某件事

Leo wants to be a doctor.
→ 當受詞
It takes time to learn a language.
→ 主詞常用 It 代替

② 當形容詞

用來做…的

放在名詞後面

Is there anything to eat?
→ 可以吃的東西
I have a lot of homework to do.
→ 要做的功課

③ 當副詞

為了…、因為…

目的、情緒的原因

I got up early to catch the bus.
→ 為了趕公車
We were happy to hear the news.
→ 因為聽到而高興
當形容詞時，介系詞要保留：a chair to sit on、someone to talk to、paper to write on

後面接 to V 的動詞

常考動詞

打算、還沒做的事
動詞意思
want / would like想要
hope希望
decide決定
plan計畫
agree / refuse同意／拒絕
promise答應

例句

examples
They decided to stay home.
→ 決定待在家
He refused to answer the question.
→ 拒絕回答
Dad promised to take us to the zoo.
→ 答應帶我們去
對照記：enjoy、finish、mind、keep、avoid 接 V-ing（Unit 3）。

動詞 + 人 + to V

要別人去做

人放中間

ask、tell、want、would like、expect、teach、remind

Mom asked me to buy some eggs.
→ 媽媽叫我去買蛋
The coach wants us to practice more.
→ 教練要我們多練習

否定：not 放在 to 前面

not to V
The teacher told us not to run.
→ 叫我們不要跑
Please remind me not to forget my key.
→ 提醒我別忘了鑰匙

例外：make、let、have、see、hear + 人 + 原形（不加 to，見 Unit 5）

疑問詞 + to V：該…

what / how / where / when / which

常接在 know、decide、show、tell 後面
I don't know what to say.
→ 不知道該說什麼
Can you show me how to use this app?
→ 怎麼用
We haven't decided where to go.
→ 要去哪裡

改寫成名詞子句

should

疑問詞 + to V = 疑問詞 + 主詞 + should + V

I don't know what I should say.
→ 和 what to say 意思一樣

注意：why 不能接 to V

I don't know why to go.
→ 要說 why I should go

It is…to V 與 too…to

It is + 形容詞 + (for 人) + to V

It 代替後面的 to V
It is important to wear a helmet.
→ 戴安全帽很重要
It is hard for me to wake up early.
→ 對我來說早起很難

too + 形容詞 + to V：太…而不能

沒有 not，卻是否定意思
The box is too heavy for me to carry.
→ 太重了，搬不動
= The box is so heavy that I can't carry it.
→ 改成 so…that 要補 can't 和 it

enough 的位置與 need + V-ing

enough

放前面還是後面？

形容詞 + enough + to V：夠…可以…

enough + 名詞 + to V：有足夠的…可以…

Tim is old enough to ride the roller coaster.
→ enough 在形容詞後面
We don't have enough time to finish.
→ enough 在名詞前面
He is too young to drive. = He is not old enough to drive.
→ 互換

need + V-ing = need to be p.p.

主動的樣子、被動的意思

主詞是「東西」，意思是被動

My bike needs fixing.
→ 腳踏車需要修理
= My bike needs to be fixed.
→ 被動的寫法
I need to fix my bike.
→ 主詞是人：need to V

不定詞 vs 動名詞

用法to VV-ing
當主詞To travel is fun.（常改 It is fun to travel.）Traveling is fun.
當動詞受詞want、hope、decide、plan、agreeenjoy、finish、mind、keep、avoid
介系詞後面✗✓ good at singing
當形容詞✓ something to drink✗
表示目的✓ I went there to study.✗
兩種都可以、意思一樣like、love、hate、begin、start、continue
兩種都可以、意思不同stop、remember、forget、try：to V＝要去做V-ing＝已經做
判斷順序：① 前面是介系詞嗎？→ V-ing　② 前面的動詞是哪一組？　③ 是不是「為了…」或「…的東西」？→ to V

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
I want to buying a new phone. → I want to buy a new phone.
to 後面一定是原形。
2
She decided going to Japan. → She decided to go to Japan.
decide 後面接 to V。
3
Mom asked me buy some milk. → Mom asked me to buy some milk.
ask + 人 + to V。
4
The teacher told us to not talk. → The teacher told us not to talk.
not 放在 to 前面。
5
She is enough old to go alone. → She is old enough to go alone.
enough 放在形容詞後面。

快問快答 8 題

1We hope ____ you soon. (to see / seeing)to see
2He enjoys ____ soccer. (to play / playing)playing
3I need a pen ____ with. (write / to write)to write
4I don't know ____ to go. (where / why)where
5It's too late ____ the bus. (to catch / catching)to catch
6She isn't ____ to vote. (old enough / enough old)old enough
7The room needs ____. (to clean / cleaning)cleaning
8Dad told me ____ worry. (not to / to not)not to
