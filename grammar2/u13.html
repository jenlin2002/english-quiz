PART 1

對等連接詞：and、but、or、so

連接「地位相等」的兩部分（字對字、句子對句子）。

連接詞關係例句
and而且、和（加上）I like cats, and my sister likes dogs.
but但是（相反）The bag is nice, but it's too expensive.
or或者（選擇）Do you want tea or coffee?
so所以（結果）It was raining, so we stayed home.

祈使句 ＋ and ／ or

Study hard, and you will pass.用功讀書，你就會及格。（and ＝ 那麼就會）＝ If you study hard, you will pass.
Hurry up, or you will be late.快一點，否則你會遲到。（or ＝ 否則）＝ If you don't hurry up, you will be late.
PART 2

成對的連接詞：both…and、either…or…

句型意思動詞跟誰例句
both A and BA 和 B 都複數Both Ken and Amy are here.
either A or B不是 A 就是 B靠近的 BEither you or Tom has to go.
neither A nor BA 和 B 都不靠近的 BNeither my parents nor I am at home.
not only A but (also) B不但 A 而且 B靠近的 BNot only the kids but also the teacher was excited.
A as well as BA 和 B 都（強調 A）前面的 AThe teacher as well as the students likes it.
口訣：both 一定複數；either／neither／not only 看「靠近動詞的 B」；as well as 看「前面的 A」。
She can either walk or take the bus.A 和 B 的形式要一樣（walk／take 都是原形）。
PART 3

附屬連接詞：帶出一個「子句」

附屬連接詞後面接一個完整句子（主詞＋動詞），這個子句說明主要句子的時間、原因、條件、讓步、目的。

用途連接詞例句
原因because, since, asI stayed home because I was sick.
讓步（雖然）although, though, even thoughAlthough it was cold, we went swimming.
時間when, while, before, after, until, as soon asCall me as soon as you arrive.
條件if, unless, as long asYou can go out as long as you finish your homework.
目的so thatHe got up early so that he could catch the bus.
結果so … that, such … thatIt was so hot that I couldn't sleep.（見 Unit 11）

逗號的位置

When I got home, my mom was cooking.子句在前 → 中間加逗號
My mom was cooking when I got home.子句在後 → 不加逗號
PART 4

because／so、although／but：不能一起用

中文說「因為…所以…」「雖然…但是…」，英文只能選一個：

Because it rained, we stayed home. = It rained, so we stayed home.（不能說 Because it rained, so we stayed home.）
Although he is rich, he is not happy. = He is rich, but he is not happy.（不能說 Although he is rich, but he…）

連接詞 vs 介系詞：後面接子句還是名詞？

意思連接詞 ＋ 子句（主詞＋動詞）介系詞 ＋ 名詞／V-ing
因為because it rainedbecause of the rain
雖然、儘管although it rainedin spite of / despite the rain
在…期間while I was sleepingduring the night
如果、萬一if it rainsin case of rain
The game was canceled because of the typhoon. = …because there was a typhoon.
PART 5

時間與條件子句

連接詞意思例句
when當…的時候When I was young, I lived in Tainan.
while在…的期間（常接進行式）While I was reading, the phone rang.
before / after在…之前／之後Wash your hands before you eat.
until直到Wait here until I come back.
as soon as一…就…I'll text you as soon as I get there.
if如果If you need help, call me.
unless除非（＝ if … not）You'll miss the bus unless you hurry. = …if you don't hurry.
as long as只要I'll help you as long as you try your best.
現在式代替未來式：時間、條件子句裡說未來的事，用現在式；主要子句才用 will。
I'll call you when I get home.（不是 when I will get）　If it rains tomorrow, we'll stay in.
例外：if 當「是否」（＝ whether）、放在 know／ask／tell 後面當受詞時，是名詞子句，未來要用 will：
I don't know if he will come tomorrow.（我不知道他明天會不會來）
PART 6

特別注意：not … until、since 的兩個意思

not … until：直到…才…

I didn't go to bed until midnight.我直到半夜才睡覺。
She didn't know the truth until her friend told her.直到朋友告訴她，她才知道真相。

since：「自從」和「既然」

I've lived here since I was five.自從我五歲就住在這裡。（時間，常配現在完成式）
Since you're here, let's start.既然你來了，我們開始吧。（原因）
SUMMARY

句型整理表

句型意思例句
祈使句, and ……，就會…Try again, and you'll do it.
祈使句, or ……，否則…Hurry, or you'll be late.
both A and B ＋ 複數動詞A 和 B 都Both he and I are tired.
either / neither / not only … B ＋ 動詞跟 B就近原則Neither he nor I am tired.
Because …, … ／ …, so …只能用一個Because I was tired, I slept.
Although …, … ／ …, but …只能用一個Although I was tired, I worked.
because of / despite ＋ 名詞介系詞because of the noise
when / if / unless ＋ 現在式代替未來If it rains, I'll stay home.
unless = if … not除非Unless you go, I won't go.
not … until直到…才He didn't come until ten.
so that ＋ 子句（常有 can / could）為了Speak loudly so that we can hear.
COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

Because I was sick, so I didn't go to school.Because I was sick, I didn't go to school.because 和 so 只能用一個。
Although it was late, but he kept working.Although it was late, he kept working.although 和 but 只能用一個。
We stayed home because the rain.We stayed home because of the rain.後面是名詞，用介系詞 because of。
I'll call you when I will arrive.I'll call you when I arrive.時間子句用現在式代替未來式。
Both Tom and Amy is from Tainan.Both Tom and Amy are from Tainan.both A and B 用複數動詞。
Neither Ken nor I are hungry.Neither Ken nor I am hungry.動詞看靠近的 I → am。
Unless you don't hurry, you'll be late.Unless you hurry, you'll be late.unless 本身就是「如果不」，不再加 not。
Work hard, or you will succeed.Work hard, and you will succeed.「就會」用 and；or 是「否則」。
