可數名詞與不可數名詞

種類說明例子
可數：普通名詞一個一個的人或東西book, apple, student
可數：集合名詞一群組成的整體family, class, team, police
不可數：物質名詞材料、液體、食物water, milk, rice, bread, money
不可數：抽象名詞看不到的概念love, fun, homework, advice, information
不可數：專有名詞特定的名字（大寫）Taipei, Amy, Christmas
不可數名詞：① 不加 a / an　② 沒有複數　③ 當主詞用單數動詞。易錯：homework、information、advice、furniture、news

可數名詞變複數

規則

regular
字尾規則
一般加 -s：books
s, x, sh, ch加 -es：boxes, dishes
子音 + o加 -es：tomatoes
子音 + y去 y 加 -ies：babies
f / fe去 f(e) 加 -ves：leaves, knives

不規則與同形

irregular
單數複數
man / womanmen / women
childchildren
foot / toothfeet / teeth
mousemice
fish / sheep / deer不變
My new jeans are too long.
→ jeans、shoes、glasses 常用複數

容易混淆的名詞

字尾有 s 卻是單數

news, math, physics
The news is surprising.
→ news 不可數
Math is my favorite subject.
→ math、physics 單數

people、police、family

集合名詞
There are five people in my family.
→ people 是 person 的複數
The police are looking for the thief.
→ police 一定用複數
My family is big.
→ 看成整體用單數

數量詞：many、much、few、little

意思可數（複數）不可數兩種都可以
很多manymucha lot of / lots of
一些（還有）a fewa littlesome / any
很少（幾乎沒有）fewlittle—
問多少How many…?How much…?—

a few 和 few

I have a few friends here.
→ 有幾個（正面）
He has few friends.
→ 幾乎沒有（負面）

a little 和 little

There is a little milk left.
→ 還有一點
We have little time.
→ 沒什麼時間了

There is / There are

動詞看後面第一個名詞

There + be
There is some milk in the fridge.
→ 不可數用 is
There are a few eggs in the basket.
→ 複數用 are
There is a pen and two books on the desk.
→ 緊接的是 a pen

問句和否定句用 any

some → any
Is there any juice?
→ 不可數
Are there any apples?
→ 複數
There aren't any chairs.
→ 否定

不可數名詞的計量單位

單位例子例句
a glass of / a cup oftwo glasses of water
I drink two glasses of water every morning. （我每天早上喝兩杯水）
a bottle of / a bowl ofa bowl of rice
He ate two bowls of rice. （他吃了兩碗飯）
a piece of / a slice ofa piece of cake
Can I have a piece of cake? （我可以吃一塊蛋糕嗎？）
a pair ofa pair of shoes
I need a new pair of shoes. （我需要一雙新鞋）
變複數的是單位，不是名詞：two cups of tea（不是 teas）

名詞的所有格

情況寫法例子
單數名詞加 'sthe boy's hat
字尾有 s 的複數只加 'my parents' car
不規則複數加 'sthe children's toys
共同擁有最後一個加 'sTom and Amy's mother
各自擁有每個都加 'sTom's and Amy's bikes
時間加 'stoday's newspaper
無生物the … of …the name of the school

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
I have a lot of homeworks. → I have a lot of homework.
homework 不可數。
2
The news are exciting. → The news is exciting.
news 用單數動詞。
3
How many money do you have? → How much money do you have?
money 不可數用 much。
4
I'd like two cups of teas. → I'd like two cups of tea.
單位變複數，tea 不變。
5
This is my parent's car.（爸媽的） → This is my parents' car.
parents 字尾有 s，只加 '。
