PART 1

可數名詞與不可數名詞

中文說「一本書、兩本書」「一些水」都不用改名詞；英文卻要先判斷這個東西能不能一個一個數。

種類說明例子
可數：普通名詞一個一個的人或東西，有單複數book, apple, student, house
可數：集合名詞一群人或東西組成的整體family, class, team, police, people
不可數：物質名詞材料、液體、氣體、食物water, milk, rice, bread, paper, money, air
不可數：抽象名詞看不到摸不到的概念love, fun, luck, homework, information, advice, time
不可數：專有名詞特定的人名、地名、節日（第一個字母大寫）Taipei, Amy, Christmas, Monday
不可數名詞的規則：① 前面不能加 a / an；② 沒有複數，不加 -s；③ 當主詞時動詞用單數。
Milk is good for you.　I have a lot of homework.（不是 homeworks）
特別容易錯的不可數名詞：homework、information、advice、furniture、money、bread、news、weather、luggage
PART 2

可數名詞變複數的規則

字尾規則例子
一般加 -sbooks, girls, machines
s, x, sh, ch加 -esbuses, boxes, dishes, watches
子音 ＋ o多半加 -estomatoes, potatoes, heroes（但 pianos, photos 加 s）
子音 ＋ y去 y 加 -iesbabies, cities, countries（母音＋y：boys, days）
f / fe去 f(e) 加 -vesleaf → leaves, knife → knives, wife → wives（例外：roofs）
不規則要背man → men, woman → women, child → children, foot → feet, tooth → teeth, mouse → mice
單複數同形不變fish, sheep, deer, Chinese, Japanese

常用複數形的名詞

兩個部分組成一組的東西，平常就用複數，動詞也用複數：pants、jeans、shorts、glasses（眼鏡）、shoes、socks、scissors

My new jeans are too long.要說「一件」時用 a pair of：a pair of jeans / two pairs of shoes
PART 3

容易混淆的名詞

family：看成整體或成員

My family is big.我家是個大家庭。（整體 → 單數）
My family are all early risers.我的家人都很早起。（每個成員 → 複數）

people：「人們」是複數；「民族」才是單數

There are five people in my family.我家有五個人。（person 的複數是 people）
The Taiwanese are a friendly people. / the peoples of Asiapeople 當「民族」時可數，可以加 a 或 -s。

字尾有 s，卻是單數

news（消息）、math / mathematics（數學）、physics（物理）、the United States（美國）

The news is surprising. / Math is my favorite subject.

police 一定用複數

The police are looking for the thief.警方正在找小偷。（一位警察是 a police officer）
PART 4

數量詞與 There is / There are

意思可數（接複數名詞）不可數兩種都可以
很多manymucha lot of / lots of
一些（還有）a fewa littlesome（肯定）／ any（否定、問句）
很少（幾乎沒有）fewlittle—
問多少How many ＋ 複數?How much ＋ 不可數?—
I have a few friends here, so I'm not lonely.我在這裡有幾個朋友，所以不寂寞。（a few：有一些）
He has few friends, so he feels lonely.他幾乎沒有朋友，所以覺得寂寞。（few：很少）
Hurry up! We have little time.快點！我們沒什麼時間了。

There is / There are：動詞看後面第一個名詞

There is some milk in the fridge.不可數 → is
There are a few eggs in the basket.複數 → are
There is a pen and two books on the desk.緊接在後面的是 a pen（單數）→ is
Is there any juice? / Are there any apples?問句、否定句用 any。
PART 5

不可數名詞怎麼「數」：計量單位

不可數名詞要說「幾杯、幾片」時，把單位放前面，變複數的是單位，不是名詞。

單位常搭配例子
a glass of水、果汁、牛奶two glasses of water
a cup of咖啡、茶three cups of tea
a bottle of瓶裝飲料a bottle of milk
a bowl of飯、湯、麵two bowls of rice
a piece of麵包、蛋糕、紙、建議、資訊a piece of cake / two pieces of paper / a piece of advice
a slice of切片（麵包、披薩）three slices of pizza
a bag of / a box of袋裝／盒裝a bag of rice / a box of cookies
a pair of成對的東西a pair of shoes / two pairs of jeans
Two glasses of juice are on the table.主詞的中心是 glasses（複數）→ are
PART 6

名詞的所有格：…的

情況寫法例子
單數名詞加 'sthe boy's hat, my sister's room
字尾是 s 的複數只加 'the girls' school, my parents' car
不規則複數（字尾沒有 s）加 'sthe children's toys, women's shoes
兩人「共同」擁有最後一個加 'sTom and Amy's mother（同一個媽媽）
兩人「各自」擁有每個都加 'sTom's and Amy's bikes（各自的腳踏車）
時間、距離加 'stoday's newspaper, a ten minutes' walk
無生物常用 the … of …the name of the school, the color of the sky
雙重所有格a ＋ 名詞 ＋ of ＋ 所有代名詞／名詞'sa friend of mine, a friend of my brother's

COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

I have a lot of homeworks today.I have a lot of homework today.homework 不可數，沒有複數。
Can you give me some advices?Can you give me some advice?advice 不可數；一個建議是 a piece of advice。
There are five persons in my family.There are five people in my family.person 的複數一般用 people。
The news are very exciting.The news is very exciting.news 字尾有 s，但是不可數、用單數動詞。
I'd like two cups of teas.I'd like two cups of tea.變複數的是單位 cups，tea 不變。
How many money do you have?How much money do you have?money 不可數，用 How much。
There is many students in the gym.There are many students in the gym.後面是複數名詞，用 There are。
This is my parent's car.（爸媽的車）This is my parents' car.parents 是字尾有 s 的複數，只加 '。
The childrens' room is upstairs.The children's room is upstairs.children 本身就是複數，直接加 's。
