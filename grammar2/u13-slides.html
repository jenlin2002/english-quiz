UNIT 13 · GRAMMAR

連接詞

Conjunctions
and・butbecause

本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 用 and、but、or、so 連接句子
  • 祈使句 + and（就會）／ or（否則）
  • both…and、either…or、neither…nor 的動詞一致
  • because／so、although／but 只能用一個
  • 時間、條件子句用現在式代替未來式

暖身 3 題

1I was hungry, ____ I ate a sandwich. (so / but)so
2____ it was cold, we went out. (Although / Because)Although
3Hurry, ____ you'll be late. (and / or)or

對等連接詞：and、but、or、so

連接詞關係例句
and加上
I like cats, and my sister likes dogs. （我喜歡貓，我妹妹喜歡狗）
but相反
The bag is nice, but it's too expensive. （包包很好看，但太貴了）
or選擇
Do you want tea or coffee? （你要茶還是咖啡？）
so結果
It was raining, so we stayed home. （下雨了，所以我們待在家）

祈使句 + and：就會

Study hard, and you will pass.
→ 用功就會及格

祈使句 + or：否則

Hurry up, or you will be late.
→ 快一點，否則會遲到

成對的連接詞

句型意思動詞跟誰
both A and BA 和 B 都複數
either A or B不是 A 就是 B靠近的 B
neither A nor BA 和 B 都不靠近的 B
not only A but also B不但 A 而且 B靠近的 B
A as well as BA 和 B 都前面的 A

例句

Both Ken and Amy are here.
→ 複數

就近原則

Neither my parents nor I am home.
→ 看靠近的 I

附屬連接詞：帶出子句

用途連接詞例句
原因because, since, as
I stayed home because I was sick. （我因為生病待在家）
讓步although, though
Although it was cold, we went swimming. （雖然很冷，我們還是去游泳）
時間when, while, before, after, until
Call me as soon as you arrive. （你一到就打給我）
條件if, unless, as long as
You can go as long as you finish your homework. （只要寫完功課就可以去）
目的so that
He got up early so that he could catch the bus. （他早起為了趕公車）
子句在前加逗號：When I got home, …；子句在後不加：… when I got home.

不能一起用：because／so、although／but

只能選一個

中文「因為…所以」英文只用一個
Because it rained, we stayed home.
→ 不能再加 so
It rained, so we stayed home.
→ 或只用 so
Although he is rich, he is not happy.
→ 不能再加 but

連接詞 vs 介系詞

看後面接什麼
子句（主詞＋動詞）名詞
because it rainedbecause of the rain
although it rainedin spite of the rain
while I was sleepingduring the night
The game was canceled because of the typhoon.
→ 後面是名詞

時間與條件子句：現在代未來

子句用現在式

when / if / unless / before / after
I'll call you when I get home.
→ 不是 will get
If it rains tomorrow, we'll stay in.
→ 主要子句才用 will
You'll be late unless you hurry.
→ unless = if … not

例外：if = 是否

名詞子句
I don't know if he will come.
→ 我不知道他會不會來

判斷：if 放在 know、ask 後面，意思是「是否」，未來就用 will

not … until 與 since 的兩個意思

not … until：直到…才

not … until
I didn't go to bed until midnight.
→ 直到半夜才睡
She didn't know until her friend told her.
→ 朋友說了她才知道

since：自從／既然

since
I've lived here since I was five.
→ 自從（時間）
Since you're here, let's start.
→ 既然（原因）

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
Because I was sick, so I stayed home. → Because I was sick, I stayed home.
because 和 so 只用一個。
2
Although it was late, but he worked. → Although it was late, he worked.
although 和 but 只用一個。
3
We stayed home because the rain. → We stayed home because of the rain.
名詞前用 because of。
4
I'll call you when I will arrive. → I'll call you when I arrive.
時間子句用現在式。
5
Unless you don't hurry, you'll be late. → Unless you hurry, you'll be late.
unless 不再加 not。

快問快答 8 題

1It was hot, ____ I turned on the fan. (so / but)so
2Try again, ____ you'll do it. (and / or)and
3Both Amy and I ____ ready. (am / are)are
4Either you or he ____ wrong. (is / are)is
5____ the rain, we stayed in. (Because / Because of)Because of
6If it ____ tomorrow, I'll stay home. (rains / will rain)rains
7I'll wait ____ you come back. (until / unless)until
8____ she was tired, she kept working. (Although / Because)Although

同義改寫

1Because it was noisy, I couldn't sleep.It was noisy, so I couldn't sleep.
2If you don't hurry, you'll be late.Hurry, or you'll be late.
3If you don't study, you won't pass.Unless you study, you won't pass.
4We stayed home because it rained.We stayed home because of the rain.
