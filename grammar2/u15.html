PART 1

肯定的附和句：…也是

前面的句子是肯定，說「…也是」有兩種寫法：

寫法順序例句
too主詞 ＋ 助動詞, tooKen likes cats, and I do, too.
soso ＋ 助動詞 ＋ 主詞（倒裝）Ken likes cats, and so do I.
My dad was at home last night, and so was I. = …, and I was, too.我爸昨晚在家，我也是。＝ Both my dad and I were at home last night.
so 後面要倒裝：先助動詞、再主詞 → so do I（不是 so I do）。
PART 2

否定的附和句：…也不

前面的句子是否定，說「…也不」：

寫法順序例句
either主詞 ＋ 助動詞 ＋ not, eitherI can't swim, and Amy can't, either.
neitherneither ＋ 助動詞 ＋ 主詞（倒裝）I can't swim, and neither can Amy.
nornor ＋ 助動詞 ＋ 主詞（倒裝）I can't swim, nor can Amy.
neither 本身就是否定，後面的助動詞不能再加 not：neither can't I ✗ → neither can I ✓
否定句用 either，不用 too：I don't like it, and he doesn't, either.（不是 too）
I haven't finished my homework, and neither has Ben.我還沒寫完功課，Ben 也還沒。
never、no、seldom 開頭的否定句也用 neither／either：Tom never cries, and neither does his sister.
PART 3

助動詞怎麼選？

和附加問句（Unit 14）一樣，看前面句子的動詞，時態要相同，單複數看新的主詞。

前面的句子用附和句用例句
be 動詞be 動詞She is tired, and so am I.
現在式一般動詞do / doesI love pizza, and so does my brother.
過去式一般動詞didLeo went to the zoo, and so did Amy.
can / will / should…同一個助動詞Mia can't drive, and neither can her dad.
have / has ＋ p.p.have / hasI've been to Japan, and so has Ken.
My parents like jazz, and so does my uncle.前面是 like（複數），但新主詞 my uncle 是單數 → does。
PART 4

對話裡的附和與不同意

對方說我也是／我也不我不一樣
I like spicy food.So do I. / Me too.Oh, I don't.
I'm not tired.Neither am I. / Me neither.Really? I am.
I can't sing.Neither can I. / Me neither.I can.
I went to the party.So did I.I didn't.
Me too／Me neither 是口語簡短說法；考試寫句子時多用 So do I／Neither do I。

COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

Amy likes music, and so I do.Amy likes music, and so do I.so 後面倒裝：助動詞在前、主詞在後。
Amy likes dogs, and so am I.Amy likes dogs, and so do I.likes 是一般動詞 → do。
I don't eat fish, and Ken doesn't, too.I don't eat fish, and Ken doesn't, either.否定句用 either。
He can't swim, and neither can't I.He can't swim, and neither can I.neither 本身是否定，不再加 not。
She went home early, and so does Tom.She went home early, and so did Tom.時態要相同，went → did。
My sisters love K-pop, and so do my brother.…, and so does my brother.單複數看新主詞 my brother（單數）。
Ella never eats meat, and so does Lily.Ella never eats meat, and neither does Lily.never 是否定 → neither。
