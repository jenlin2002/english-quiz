UNIT 17 · GRAMMAR

形容詞子句

Relative Clauses
who・whichthat・whose

本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 用 who、which、that、whose 把兩句合成一句
  • 分辨主格、受格（可省略）、所有格
  • 知道什麼時候一定用 that、什麼時候不能用 that
  • 用 what = the thing that
  • 用 where、when 說地方和時間

暖身 3 題

1The boy ____ sings well is Ken. (who / which)who
2The book ____ I read is fun. (who / that)that
3The girl ____ dog is big is Amy. (who / whose)whose

觀念：一個句子當形容詞

形容詞子句放在名詞後面

who / which / that + 子句
a tall boy
→ 一個字：放前面
the boy who is playing tennis
→ 一整句：放後面

先行詞：被修飾的名詞（the boy）

關係代名詞總表

relative pronouns
先行詞主格受格所有格
人who / thatwhom / thatwhose
物which / thatwhich / thatwhose
人＋物thatthat—

合併兩句：三個步驟

步驟

steps
  • ① 找出兩句相同的人或物
  • ② 第二句的那個字換成關係代名詞
  • ③ 整個子句放在先行詞後面
The girl is my cousin. The girl sings well.
→ 原本兩句

合併結果

result
The girl who sings well is my cousin.
→ 人、主格 → who
The bike which my dad bought is mine.
→ 物、受格 → which，搬到子句前面

主格與受格

主格：後面接動詞

who / which / that + V
I have a friend who lives in Japan.
→ who 當主詞
the girls who are… / the girl who is…
→ 動詞跟先行詞

注意：主格關代不能省略

受格：後面接主詞＋動詞

(whom / which / that) + S + V
The man (whom) I met is a doctor.
→ 可以省略
The movie (that) we watched was boring.
→ 可以省略

注意：子句裡不能再有 it / him（關代已經代替）

介系詞 + whom / which 與 whose

介系詞 + 關代

with whom / in which
The man with whom she works is kind.
→ = The man she works with
This is the house in which I lived.
→ = where I lived

注意：介系詞後面不能用 that 或 who

所有格 whose：…的

whose + 名詞
The girl whose dog is big is my classmate.
→ whose 後面接名詞
I have a book whose cover is red.
→ 物也可以用

that 的特別規則

一定（最好）用 that

must use that
She is the kindest person that I know.
→ 最高級
This is all that I have.
→ all、the only、the first
the boy and his dog that are running
→ 人＋物

不能用 that

can't use that

① 介系詞後面：with whom（不是 with that）

② 逗號後面（非限定）：My dad, who is a cook, …

③ 所有格：用 whose

what、限定與非限定

what = the thing that

沒有先行詞
What you said is true.
→ 你說的話是真的
I don't understand what he means.
→ 他的意思

注意：前面有名詞就不能用 what：the book that I want

逗號：補充說明

有沒有逗號
The man who is standing there is my dad.
→ 限定：指出是哪一個
My dad, who loves hiking, is a teacher.
→ 非限定：補充，不能用 that

關係副詞：where、when

關係副詞代替例句
where地方（= in / at which）
This is the park where I often play. （這是我常玩的公園）
when時間（= on / in which）
I remember the day when we first met. （我記得我們初次見面那天）
which（比較）子句缺受詞
This is the park which I visited. （這是我參觀過的公園）
看子句缺什麼：缺地方 → where；缺主詞或受詞 → which。

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
The girl which sings well is Amy. → The girl who sings well is Amy.
人用 who／that。
2
The movie which we watched it was great. → The movie which we watched was great.
不能重複 it。
3
The girl who her dog is big is Amy. → The girl whose dog is big is Amy.
「她的」用 whose。
4
This is the book what I want. → This is the book that I want.
有先行詞不用 what。
5
This is the park which I often play. → This is the park where I often play.
缺地方用 where。

快問快答 8 題

1the man ____ lives here (who / which)who
2the bag ____ I bought (who / that)that
3the boy ____ mom is a nurse (who / whose)whose
4the town ____ I grew up (which / where)where

6____ you need is sleep. (What / That)What
7the friend with ____ I live (whom / that)whom
8the day ____ we met (when / which)when

合併練習與下一步

用關係詞合併

1The lady is my aunt. She is wearing a hat.The lady who is wearing a hat is my aunt.
2I lost the pen. I bought it yesterday.I lost the pen (that) I bought yesterday.
3I know a boy. His dad is a pilot.I know a boy whose dad is a pilot.
4This is the café. We met there.This is the café where we met.
