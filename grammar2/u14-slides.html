本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 用三個規則寫出附加問句
  • 依照 be 動詞、一般動詞、助動詞、完成式選對字
  • 處理 this、there、everyone 等特別的主詞
  • never、nothing 句子的附加問句、Let's 和祈使句
  • 正確回答附加問句（看事實說 Yes / No）

暖身 3 題

1It's cold, ____? (isn't it / is it)isn't it
2You like music, ____? (aren't you / don't you)don't you
3He can't swim, ____? (can he / can't he)can he

附加問句的三個規則

規則

rules
  • ① 前肯後否、前否後肯
  • ② 主詞換成代名詞（Ken → he）
  • ③ 用原句的 be／助動詞；一般動詞借 do / does / did
  • ④ 否定一律縮寫：isn't、don't、can't

例句

examples
Ken is your classmate, isn't he?
→ 前肯後否
Amy isn't here, is she?
→ 前否後肯
Your parents like fish, don't they?
→ 一般動詞借 do
Ben lost his key, didn't he?
→ 過去式借 did

各種時態與助動詞

前面的句子附加問句例句
was / were同一個 be 動詞
It was cold, wasn't it? （那時很冷，對吧？）
現在式一般動詞do / does
She plays tennis, doesn't she? （她打網球，對吧？）
can / will / should同一個助動詞
It will rain, won't it? （會下雨，對吧？）
have / has + p.p.have / has
They have finished, haven't they? （他們做完了，對吧？）
have（擁有）do / does
You have a dog, don't you? （你有養狗，對吧？）
I amaren't I（較正式 am I not）
I'm late, aren't I? （我遲到了，對吧？）

特別的主詞

主詞附加問句用例句
this / thatit
This is your pen, isn't it? （這是你的筆，對吧？）
these / thosethey
Those are your shoes, aren't they? （那些是你的鞋，對吧？）
everything / nothingit
Everything is ready, isn't it? （都準備好了，對吧？）
everyone / nobodythey
Everyone likes her, don't they? （大家都喜歡她，對吧？）
There is / arethere
There is a park nearby, isn't there? （附近有公園，對吧？）

句子裡有否定的字

never、nothing、seldom…

negative words

雖然沒有 not，句子已經是否定 → 附加問句用肯定

She never eats meat, does she?
→ 她從不吃肉，對吧？
There is nothing in the box, is there?
→ 箱子裡沒東西，對吧？

字裡面的否定不算

unhappy、dislike
He is unhappy, isn't he?
→ unhappy 仍算肯定句
Nobody came, did they?
→ nobody 用 they

祈使句與 Let's

句型附加問句例句
Let's …shall we?
Let's go to the movies, shall we? （我們去看電影好嗎？）
祈使句（請對方做）will you?
Close the door, will you? （把門關上好嗎？）
否定祈使句will you?
Don't be late, will you? （別遲到喔）
邀請won't you?
Have some cake, won't you? （吃塊蛋糕吧！）

含子句的句子與怎麼回答

跟著主要子句

main clause
You know where he lives, don't you?
→ 主要子句 You know
She was sleeping when you called, wasn't she?
→ 主要子句 She was sleeping

例外：I think … 跟著後面的子句：I think Ken is right, isn't he?

回答：看事實說 Yes / No

和中文的「對／不對」不同
— You aren't hungry, are you?
→ 問句
— Yes, I am.
→ 我餓（事實是肯定）
— No, I'm not.
→ 我不餓（事實是否定）

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
Amy is your sister, isn't Amy? → Amy is your sister, isn't she?
主詞換成代名詞。
2
You like pizza, aren't you? → You like pizza, don't you?
一般動詞借 do。
3
He went home, doesn't he? → He went home, didn't he?
過去式借 did。
4
He never lies, doesn't he? → He never lies, does he?
never 是否定 → 後面肯定。
5
Let's have lunch, don't we? → Let's have lunch, shall we?
Let's → shall we。
