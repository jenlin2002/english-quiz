PART 1

Yes／No 問句

把 be 動詞或助動詞移到主詞前面；一般動詞就借 do / does / did，動詞變回原形。

原句問句簡答
She is a nurse.Is she a nurse?Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
They can swim.Can they swim?Yes, they can.
He likes cats.Does he like cats?No, he doesn't.
You went out.Did you go out?Yes, I did.
Ken has finished.Has Ken finished?Yes, he has.

選擇問句：A or B?

Do you want tea or coffee? — Tea, please.不能用 Yes／No 回答，要選一個。
PART 2

疑問詞問句：疑問詞 ＋ Yes／No 問句

Will you graduate next year? → When will you graduate?
Is David studying in the library? → Where is David studying?
Does Nancy drive carefully? → How does Nancy drive?
公式：疑問詞 ＋ be／助動詞／do ＋ 主詞 ＋ …?　回答時不用 Yes／No，直接回答問的內容。
PART 3

問「主詞」時不借 do

如果問的就是主詞（誰做了…？什麼東西…？），疑問詞直接當主詞，後面接動詞，不用 do／does／did，也不用倒裝。

問主詞問受詞
Who broke the window?
誰打破窗戶？（Who 是主詞）		Who(m) did you see?
你看到誰？（Who 是 see 的受詞）
What happened?
發生什麼事？		What did you eat?
你吃了什麼？
Who lives here?
誰住這裡？（單數 lives）		Who(m) do you live with?
你和誰住？
問主詞的 who 後面動詞通常用單數：Who knows the answer?
PART 4

各個疑問詞問什麼

疑問詞問例句回答
when時間When did she arrive?Last night. / At seven.
where地點Where does Tom live?In Tainan.
who人（身分、主詞）Who is that man?He is my uncle.
whom人（受詞，較正式）Whom should I ask?Ms. Lin.
whose誰的Whose bag is this?It's mine.
what什麼What is he doing?He's cooking.
which哪一個（有範圍可選）Which color do you like, red or blue?Blue.
why原因Why are you late?Because I missed the bus.
how方法、狀況How do you go to school?By bus.
Who 和 What 問人：Who is he? 問「他是誰」（名字、關係）；What is he? / What does he do? 問「他做什麼工作」。

提議與回應

Why don't we go to the park? = How about going to the park?我們去公園好不好？（提議）
— Would you like some cake? — Why not?好啊，有何不可？
PART 5

How 家族

問句問例句回答
How many ＋ 複數名詞數量（可數）How many kids are there?Twenty.
How much ＋ 不可數數量（不可數）／價錢How much is this shirt?NT$300.
How often頻率How often do you swim?Twice a week.
How long多久（一段時間）；多長How long have you lived here?For five years.
How soon還要多久（才…）How soon can you come?In ten minutes.
How far距離How far is it to the station?About two kilometers.
How old / tall年紀／身高How old is your sister?She's ten.
How方法；狀況How did you get here? / How was the trip?By taxi. / Great!
How long vs How soon：回答 for ＋ 一段時間 → How long；回答 in ＋ 一段時間（…之後）→ How soon。
PART 6

常用的 What 問句

問句問回答
What time is it?幾點It's three o'clock.
What day is today?星期幾It's Monday.
What's the date today?幾月幾號It's May 5.
What's the weather like?天氣It's sunny.
What does he do?職業He's a lawyer.
What does she look like?外表She's tall with long hair.
What's wrong (with you)?怎麼了I have a headache.
PART 7

依「畫線部分」造問句（最常考）

  1. 看畫線部分是什麼 → 選疑問詞（時間 when、地點 where、人 who…）。
  2. 畫線部分是主詞？→ 疑問詞直接取代，其他不動。
  3. 不是主詞？→ 把句子改成 Yes／No 問句，刪掉畫線部分，疑問詞放最前面。
Ken bought a new bike yesterday.
→ Did Ken buy a new bike yesterday? → What did Ken buy yesterday?
Amy broke the cup.
→ Who broke the cup?畫線是主詞 → 直接換成 Who，不借 did。
They go swimming twice a week.
→ How often do they go swimming?

COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

Where you live?Where do you live?一般動詞要借 do。
What did you bought?What did you buy?借了 did，動詞用原形。
Who did break the window?Who broke the window?問主詞不借 did。
How many water do you drink?How much water do you drink?water 不可數 → How much。
How long do you visit your grandma? — Once a month.How often do you visit your grandma?回答次數 → How often。
Whose bag this is?Whose bag is this?問句要把 is 放到主詞前。
— Do you want juice or milk? — Yes.— Milk, please.選擇問句要選一個回答。
