副詞

PART 1

副詞的種類

形容詞修飾名詞；副詞修飾名詞以外的字：動詞、形容詞、副詞、整個句子。

種類回答的問題例子
狀態（情狀）副詞怎麼做？slowly, carefully, well, hard
地方副詞在哪裡？here, there, home, upstairs, in the park
時間副詞什麼時候？now, today, yesterday, soon, last night
頻率副詞多常？always, usually, often, sometimes, seldom, never
程度副詞多麼？very, quite, too, so, enough, almost
修飾整句說話者的看法luckily, sadly, fortunately
She sings beautifully.修飾動詞 sings → 副詞（不是 beautiful）
The test was really hard.修飾形容詞 hard
Luckily, nobody was hurt.修飾整個句子
PART 2

副詞的形成：形容詞 ＋ -ly

規則例子
一般：加 -lyquick → quickly, careful → carefully, sad → sadly
子音 ＋ y：去 y 加 -ilyhappy → happily, easy → easily, angry → angrily
-le 結尾：去 e 加 ygentle → gently, terrible → terribly
-ue 結尾：去 e 加 lytrue → truly
不規則good → well

形容詞、副詞同形的字

fast, hard, early, late, high, near, far, enough

He is a fast runner.（形容詞）/ He runs fast.（副詞）不說 fastly。

加了 -ly 意思完全不同

原字加 -ly例句
hard 努力地hardly 幾乎不She works hard. / I can hardly hear you.
late 晚lately 最近He got up late. / Have you seen Ken lately?
near 近nearly 幾乎He lives near. / I nearly fell.
high 高highly 非常、高度地Birds fly high. / This book is highly recommended.
-ly 結尾卻是形容詞：friendly、lovely、lonely、lively → 要說「友善地」用 in a friendly way。
PART 3

副詞放在哪裡？

① 狀態副詞：通常在動詞（或受詞）後面

He finished his work carefully.副詞不放在動詞和受詞中間：He finished carefully his work ✗

② 好幾個副詞一起出現：狀態 → 地方 → 時間

Bob studied hard at home yesterday.口訣「怎樣、哪裡、何時」

③ 強調時可以放句首

Tomorrow I have to see a doctor. / Slowly, she opened the box.

④ 遇到片語動詞

可拆開的（turn on、put on…）：副詞放前面或句尾，不能塞在中間；不可拆開的（look at、listen to…）：副詞可以放在中間。

She quickly took off her coat. = She took off her coat quickly.She took quickly off her coat ✗
Sam looked carefully at the map.look at 中間可以放副詞
PART 4

頻率副詞

由多到少：always（100%）→ usually → often → sometimes → seldom / rarely → never（0%）

位置例句
be 動詞後面He is always on time.
一般動詞前面She usually takes the bus.
助動詞和動詞中間You should always tell the truth.
口訣：「be 後、一般前、助動詞中間」。
seldom、never 本身是否定的意思，句子不再加 not：He never eats breakfast.（不是 never doesn't）

問頻率：How often…?

How often do you exercise? — Three times a week.回答可以用 once a day、twice a month、every Sunday…
PART 5

still、already、yet

副詞意思用在例句
still仍然、還在肯定、否定（放 not 前面）It's still raining. / He still can't swim.
already已經（比預期早）肯定句I've already eaten.
yet還（沒）；已經…了嗎否定句、問句，放句尾I haven't eaten yet. / Have you eaten yet?
PART 6

程度副詞：too、enough、so…that、such…that

句型意思例句
too ＋ adj./adv. ＋ to V太…而不能The soup is too hot to eat.
adj./adv. ＋ enough ＋ to V夠…可以He ran fast enough to win.
so ＋ adj./adv. ＋ that ＋ 子句如此…以致於The soup is so hot that I can't eat it.
such ＋ (a/an) ＋ adj. ＋ 名詞 ＋ that如此…的（名詞）以致於It was such a hot day that we stayed inside.
so 和 such：後面沒有名詞 → so（so hot）；後面有名詞 → such（such a hot day）。
She is so kind. / She is such a kind girl. = She is so kind a girl.
too…to 改成 so…that 要補上 can't 和受詞：The box is too heavy for me to carry. = The box is so heavy that I can't carry it.
PART 7

副詞的比較級與最高級

規則原級 → 比較級 → 最高級
和形容詞同形的單音節字：加 -er／-estfast → faster → fastest、hard → harder → hardest
early：去 y 加 -ier／-iestearly → earlier → earliest
-ly 副詞：more／mostcarefully → more carefully → most carefully
不規則well → better → best、badly → worse → worst、far → farther → farthest
Amy runs faster than Ben.
Please drive more slowly.（不說 slowlier）
Steve works (the) hardest in the company. = Steve works harder than any other worker in the company.副詞的最高級，the 可以省略。
SUMMARY

句型整理表

重點規則例句
修飾動詞用副詞，不用形容詞He speaks English well.
多個副詞狀態 → 地方 → 時間She sang happily on stage last night.
頻率副詞be 後、一般動詞前、助動詞中間He is often late. / He often walks.
問頻率How often…?How often do you swim?
太…不能too ＋ adj. ＋ to Vtoo tired to walk
夠…可以adj. ＋ enough ＋ to Vold enough to drive
如此…以致so ＋ adj.／such ＋ (a) adj. ＋ N ＋ thatso cold that… / such a cold day that…
比較faster／more carefully／better ＋ thanShe sings better than me.
COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

She speaks English very good.She speaks English very well.修飾動詞 speaks，用副詞 well。
He drives careful.He drives carefully.修飾動詞要用副詞。
He runs very fastly.He runs very fast.fast 本身就是副詞，沒有 fastly。
I studied hardly for the test.I studied hard for the test.hardly 是「幾乎不」；努力地是 hard。
He goes always to school by bus.He always goes to school by bus.頻率副詞放在一般動詞前面。
I never don't eat breakfast.I never eat breakfast.never 已經是否定，不再加 not。
It was so a nice day that we went out.It was such a nice day that we went out.後面有名詞（a nice day）用 such。
Please speak slowlier.Please speak more slowly.-ly 副詞的比較級用 more。
