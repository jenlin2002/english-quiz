UNIT 10 · GRAMMAR

形容詞

Adjectives
比較級最高級

本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 把形容詞放在正確的位置（something cold、a ten-year-old boy）
  • 寫出比較級和最高級（含不規則變化）
  • 用 as…as、比較級 than、the 最高級 in / of
  • 用 than any other、one of the 最高級 表達「最…」
  • 用 much 修飾比較級、說「越來越…」「越…越…」

暖身 3 題

1A cat is ____ than a mouse. (big / bigger)bigger
2He is the ____ in his class. (tallest / taller)tallest
3I want something ____. (hot / hotly)hot

形容詞的位置

三個位置

position

① 名詞前面

This is an easy question.
→ 最常見

② be、連綴動詞後面

You look tired.
→ look、feel、get…

③ something、anything 後面

I want something cold.
→ 不說 cold something

數字 + 名詞當形容詞

a ten-year-old boy

名詞用單數、加連字號

a ten-year-old boy
→ 不說 ten-years-old
a two-week vacation
→ 兩週的假期
The boy is ten years old.
→ 放在後面才用複數

分詞當形容詞與形容詞片語

V-ing 和 p.p.

participles

V-ing：主動、正在…

p.p.：被動、已經…

a crying baby
→ 正在哭的寶寶
a broken window
→ 被打破的窗戶
The girl playing the piano is my sister.
→ 分詞片語放名詞後面

be + 形容詞 + 介系詞

常考片語
片語意思
be proud of以…為傲
be famous for以…聞名
be late for…遲到
be different from和…不同
be full of充滿
be kind to對…好

原級、比較級、最高級

規則變化

regular
規則例子
加 -er / -esttall → taller → tallest
e 結尾加 -r / -stnice → nicer → nicest
子音 + y → -ier / -iesteasy → easier → easiest
重複字尾big → bigger → biggest
多音節 more / mostbeautiful → more beautiful

不規則變化

一定要背
原級比較級最高級
good / wellbetterbest
badworseworst
many / muchmoremost
littlelessleast
farfartherfarthest

原級與比較級

as … as：一樣

中間用原級
Ken is as tall as his father.
→ 一樣高
This test is not as hard as the last one.
→ 沒有那麼難
My room is twice as big as yours.
→ 倍數放 as 前面

比較級 than：兩個比

comparative
Tom is taller than Ben.
→ 比較高
Math is more difficult than English.
→ 多音節用 more
Amy is the taller of the two.
→ 兩者中比較高的
Taipei is hotter than that in Tokyo.
→ 比同類：that = the weather

最高級的各種說法

句型例句
the + 最高級 + in / of
Jade Mountain is the highest mountain in Taiwan. （玉山是台灣最高的山）
比較級 + than any other + 單數
He is taller than any other boy in our class. （他比班上其他男生都高）
No other + 單數 + 比較級 + than
No other boy in our class is taller than he. （班上沒有男生比他高）
one of the + 最高級 + 複數
She is one of the best singers in Taiwan. （她是台灣最棒的歌手之一）
than any other 後面接單數名詞，而且一定要有 other（不能和自己比）。

修飾比較級、越來越、越…越…

much / far / a lot + 比較級

…得多
Today is much colder than yesterday.
→ 冷得多
I feel a little better now.
→ 好一點

注意：very 只能修飾原級：very cold ✓　very colder ✗

越來越、越…越…

比較級 and 比較級
It's getting colder and colder.
→ 越來越冷
English is more and more important.
→ 越來越重要
The more you practice, the better you get.
→ 越練越好

It is + 形容詞 + of / for + 人 + to V

介系詞形容詞描述例句
of人的個性：kind、nice、polite、careless
It is kind of you to help me. （你真好心幫我）
for事情的性質：easy、hard、important
It is hard for me to wake up early. （對我來說早起很難）
判斷方法：You are kind. 說得通 → of；I am hard. 說不通 → for。

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
She is more taller than me. → She is taller than me.
-er 和 more 不能一起用。
2
He is as taller as his brother. → He is as tall as his brother.
as…as 中間用原級。
3
Today is very hotter than yesterday. → Today is much hotter than yesterday.
比較級用 much 修飾。
4
He is taller than any boy in his class. → He is taller than any other boy in his class.
要加 other。
5
She is one of the best singer. → She is one of the best singers.
one of the 最高級 + 複數。

同義改寫

2Amy is not as tall as Lily.Lily is taller than Amy.
