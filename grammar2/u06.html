PART 1

助動詞的四大特性

常見的助動詞：can、could、may、might、will、would、shall、should、must。它們有共同的規則：

  1. 後面一定接原形動詞：She can swim.（不是 can swims、can to swim）
  2. 不因主詞改變：沒有 -s，He can、They can 都一樣。
  3. 否定直接加 not：cannot (can't)、should not (shouldn't)、must not (mustn't)
  4. 問句把助動詞移到最前面：Can you swim?　不用再借 do / does。
一句只能有一個助動詞：will can ✗ → will be able to ✓；must can ✗ → must be able to ✓
PART 2

can、could、be able to：能力

用法例句
現在的能力Mia can speak three languages.　＝ Mia is able to speak…
過去的能力I could read when I was four.　＝ I was able to read…
未來的能力You will be able to drive next year.（不能說 will can）
允許You can sit here.（你可以坐這裡）
請求Can / Could you help me?　could 比較客氣
PART 3

must 和 have to：必須

musthave to / has to
語氣說話的人覺得一定要（規定、命令）外在情況逼得非做不可
例句You must wear a seat belt.I have to get up at six for the trip.
過去式沒有，用 had toI had to walk home yesterday.
未來式沒有，用 will have toYou will have to wait.
問句Must I go?Do I have to go?

否定的意思完全不同（最常考！）

You mustn't touch the paintings.不可以碰畫。（禁止）
You don't have to bring lunch. We'll buy pizza.你不必帶午餐。（不需要，但要帶也可以）
回答 Must I…?：肯定 Yes, you must.；否定用 No, you don't have to. / No, you needn't.（不說 No, you mustn't，那是「禁止」）
PART 4

should、ought to、had better、shall：建議

助動詞意思例句
should = ought to應該You should drink more water. = You ought to drink more water.
should not / ought not to不應該You shouldn't stay up so late.
had better ＋ 原形最好（不然會有麻煩）You'd better take an umbrella. It's going to rain.
had better not ＋ 原形最好不要You'd better not be late again.
Shall I / we …?提議（要不要…？）Shall we go for a walk?
had better 不是過去式，說的是現在或未來；否定的 not 放在 better 後面（had better not），縮寫是 'd better。

should have ＋ p.p.：過去本來應該做，卻沒做

You should have told me earlier.你早該告訴我的。（可是你沒說）
PART 5

may、might：可能、允許

It may rain this afternoon.今天下午可能會下雨。
Ken might come to the party.Ken 也許會來。（might 的可能性比 may 再低一點）
May I use your phone? — Yes, you may. / Sure.我可以用你的電話嗎？（客氣地請求允許）

may be 和 maybe

寫法詞性位置例句
may be助動詞 ＋ be 動詞句中，當句子的動詞He may be at home now.
maybe副詞（＝ perhaps）通常放句首，句子還需要動詞Maybe he is at home now.
PART 6

will、would

I will call you tonight.我今晚會打給你。（未來，見 Unit 1）
Dad said he would buy me a new bike.爸爸說他會買新腳踏車給我。（轉述過去說的話，will 變 would）
Would you open the window, please?可以請你開窗嗎？（比 Will you…? 客氣）
I would like to try the cake. / I'd like a glass of water.would like ＝ want，但比較有禮貌；後面接 to V 或名詞。
PART 7

need、used to

need 有兩種身分

一般動詞（最常用）助動詞（只用在否定、問句）
肯定He needs to rest.—
否定He doesn't need to rest.He needn't rest.
問句Does he need to rest?Need he rest?

當助動詞時後面直接接原形、不加 -s；當一般動詞時要加 to、第三人稱要加 -s。兩種不能混用（He needn't to rest ✗）。

used to ＋ 原形：以前常常（現在不會了）

I used to be afraid of dogs.我以前很怕狗。（現在不怕了）
Did you use to live here? / I didn't use to like vegetables.問句、否定借助 did，後面變 use to。

和 be used to ＋ V-ing（習慣於）不一樣，見 Unit 3。

PART 8

用助動詞表示推測

依照「有多確定」排列：

把握度助動詞例句
非常確定「是」must ＋ VThe lights are on. They must be home.
燈亮著，他們一定在家。
有可能may / might / could ＋ VShe may be in the library.
她可能在圖書館。
非常確定「不是」can't ＋ VHe just ate. He can't be hungry.
他剛吃過，不可能餓。
推測的否定用 can't，不用 mustn't：He mustn't be hungry. 的意思不是「一定不餓」。

推測過去的事：助動詞 ＋ have ＋ p.p.

The ground is wet. It must have rained last night.地上是濕的，昨晚一定下過雨。
She was in Japan last week. You can't have seen her here.她上週在日本，你不可能在這裡看到她。
PART 9

請求與允許：越過去式越客氣

目的一般比較客氣
請別人做事Can you…? / Will you…?Could you…? / Would you…?
請求允許Can I…?Could I…? / May I…?
Could you please pass me the salt?可以請你把鹽遞給我嗎？
May I come in? — Of course.我可以進來嗎？——當然。

COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

She can speaks English.She can speak English.助動詞後面一定接原形。
You should to see a doctor.You should see a doctor.should 後面直接接原形（ought 才要加 to）。
I will can go with you tomorrow.I will be able to go with you tomorrow.兩個助動詞不能連用，can 改成 be able to。
Does he must go now?Must he go now? / Does he have to go now?助動詞自己移到句首當問句，不再借 does。
— Must I finish it today? — No, you mustn't.— No, you don't have to. / No, you needn't.mustn't 是「禁止」，「不必」要用 don't have to 或 needn't。
You had not better go out.You had better not go out.not 放在 had better 後面。
He just had lunch. He mustn't be hungry.He just had lunch. He can't be hungry.「一定不是」的推測用 can't。
He maybe at school now.He may be at school.maybe 是副詞，不能當動詞；句中要用 may be。
