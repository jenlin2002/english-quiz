UNIT 19 · GRAMMAR

直接問句與間接問句

Direct & Indirect Questions
Where is he?where he is

本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 把直接問句放進 Do you know…? 變成間接問句
  • 間接問句用「疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞」
  • 拿掉 do／does／did，把時態還給動詞
  • Yes／No 問句改用 if／whether
  • 轉述別人說的話（said that、asked if）

暖身 3 題

1Do you know where ____? (is he / he is)he is
2I don't know what ____. (does she want / she wants)she wants
3I wonder ____ it will rain. (if / what)if

直接問句 vs 間接問句

直接問句間接問句
Who is the girl? （那女孩是誰？）
Do you know who the girl is?
Where is the restroom? （廁所在哪？）
Can you tell me where the restroom is?
What time will he come? （他幾點來？）
I don't know what time he will come.
間接問句的語序：疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞（像直述句，不倒裝）

拿掉 do／does／did

時態還給動詞

does → -s；did → 過去式
直接問句間接問句
Where does he live?where he lives
What did she buy?what she bought
When do they leave?when they leave

例句

examples
Do you know where he lives?
→ 不是 where does he live
I wonder what she bought.
→ 不是 what did she buy
Tell me when they leave.
→ do 拿掉、原形

Yes／No 問句與疑問詞當主詞

Yes／No 問句：if / whether

if = whether（是否）
Is he a doctor? → I'm not sure if he is a doctor.
→ 是否
Did she pass? → I don't know if she passed.
→ 拿掉 did
Will he come? → Nobody knows whether he will come.
→ 是否：未來用 will

疑問詞當主詞：語序不變

Who / What + V
Who broke the window? → Do you know who broke the window?
→ 不用改
What happened? → Tell me what happened.
→ 不用改

問號還是句號？時態一致

看前面的主要句子

? or .
Do you know where he lives?
→ 主要句子是問句：問號
I don't know where he lives.
→ 直述句：句號
Tell me where it is.
→ 祈使句：句號

主要句子是過去式

時態往過去退
Where is the station? → He asked where the station was.
→ is → was
Can you swim? → She asked if I could swim.
→ can → could

事實可保留現在式：asked what the capital of Japan is

轉述別人說的話

原本的話轉述
Amy said, "I am tired." （Amy 說：「我累了。」）
Amy said (that) she was tired.
Ken said to me, "I like your bag." （Ken 對我說：「我喜歡你的包包。」）
Ken told me (that) he liked my bag.
Mom asked, "Are you hungry?" （媽媽問：「你餓嗎？」）
Mom asked me if I was hungry.
He asked, "Where do you live?" （他問：「你住哪？」）
He asked where I lived.
The teacher said, "Be quiet." （老師說：「安靜。」）
The teacher told us to be quiet.
人稱跟著換（I → she、your → my），時態往過去退（am → was、can → could、will → would）。

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
Do you know where is the post office? → Do you know where the post office is?
主詞 + 動詞。
2
I don't know where does he live. → I don't know where he lives.
拿掉 does，加 -s。
3
Can you tell me what did she buy? → Can you tell me what she bought?
拿掉 did，改過去式。
4
I wonder is he at home. → I wonder if he is at home.
Yes／No 問句加 if。
5
I don't know where he lives? → I don't know where he lives.
直述句用句號。
