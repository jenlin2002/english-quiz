UNIT 2 · GRAMMAR

被動語態

The Passive Voice
主動被動

本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 知道什麼時候要用被動語態
  • 用四個步驟把主動句改成被動句
  • 寫出各種時態的被動（being、been、助動詞 + be）
  • 處理疑問句、授與動詞、使役／感官動詞的被動
  • 背熟不用 by 的被動片語

暖身 3 題

1This cake ____ by my mom. (made / was made)was made
2English ____ in many countries. (speaks / is spoken)is spoken
3I am interested ____ science. (in / by)in

什麼時候用被動？

公式：be 動詞 + 過去分詞（p.p.）（+ by 做動作的人）

① 不知道是誰做的

unknown
My bike was stolen last night.
→ 不知道小偷是誰

② 誰做的不重要

not important
English is spoken in many countries.
→ 說英語的人不用講

③ 想強調「東西」

focus on the thing
This temple was built 200 years ago.
→ 重點是這座廟
只有後面有受詞的「及物動詞」才有被動。happen、arrive、die、sleep 沒有被動：The accident happened at noon.（不說 was happened）

主動改被動：四個步驟

步驟

口訣：時態看舊句，單複數看新主詞
  • ① 受詞搬到最前面當主詞
  • ② 時態看「原來的句子」
  • ③ 單複數看「新的主詞」
  • ④ 動詞改 p.p.，原主詞放到 by 後面（代名詞改受格）

跟著做一次

Kevin cleans the classroom.
→ 主動：現在式
The classroom is cleaned by Kevin.
→ 被動：classroom 單數 → is
They planted these trees.
→ 主動：過去式
These trees were planted by them.
→ 複數 → were；they → them

各時態的被動

時態被動公式例句
現在簡單式am / is / are + p.p.
The store is opened at nine every day. （這家店每天九點開門）
過去簡單式was / were + p.p.
The windows were broken by the storm. （窗戶被暴風雨打破了）
未來式will be + p.p.
The results will be announced tomorrow. （結果明天公布）
現在進行式am / is / are being + p.p.
The road is being repaired now. （這條路正在整修）
過去進行式was / were being + p.p.
The dog was being washed when I came in. （我進來時狗正在被洗澡）
現在完成式have / has been + p.p.
The tickets have been sold out. （票已經賣完了）
過去完成式had been + p.p.
The cake had been eaten before we arrived. （我們到之前蛋糕已經被吃光）
助動詞can / must / should + be + p.p.
Phones must be turned off in class. （上課手機一定要關機）

看出規律：being、been、be

進行式 → being

正在被…

be + being + p.p.

The bridge is being built.
→ 橋正在被蓋
The room was being cleaned.
→ 房間當時正在被打掃

完成式 → been

已經被…

have / has / had + been + p.p.

My wallet has been found.
→ 錢包已經被找到了
The work had been done by noon.
→ 中午前已經做完

助動詞 → be

可以／應該被…

can / will / must / should + be + p.p.

This problem can be solved.
→ 這問題可以被解決
Homework should be handed in on time.
→ 作業應該準時交
檢查方法：被動一定有「be 的某個樣子」+ p.p.。只有 p.p. 沒有 be，或 be 後面接 V-ing，都不是被動。

疑問句與祈使句的被動

疑問句：先改肯定句，再把 be 搬前面

Questions

Yes / No 問句

Did Ann make this card?
→ 主動問句
Was this card made by Ann?
→ 被動問句

疑問詞 Who

Who invented the telephone?
→ 主動
By whom was the telephone invented?
→ who 變成 by whom

祈使句：Let + 受詞 + be + p.p.

Commands

比較正式，考試偶爾出現

Close the door.
→ 主動
Let the door be closed.
→ 被動

授與動詞：兩種被動都可以

give、send、show、teach、tell 後面有「人」和「東西」兩個受詞，哪一個都能當新主詞。

「人」當主詞

人 + be + p.p. + 東西
Grandma gave me a watch.
→ 主動
I was given a watch by Grandma.
→ 我被給了一支手錶
Tom was shown the way by a police officer.
→ Tom 被指引了方向

「東西」當主詞：人前面補介系詞

東西 + be + p.p. + to / for + 人
A watch was given to me by Grandma.
→ give、send、show、teach 用 to
A cake was made for me by Mom.
→ make、buy、cook 用 for

使役動詞、感官動詞的被動：要補 to

主動接原形 → 被動補 to

最容易漏掉的 to

make、see、hear

The teacher made us clean the room.
→ 主動：make + 人 + 原形
We were made to clean the room.
→ 被動：be made + to + 原形
I heard him sing. → He was heard to sing.
→ 感官動詞也一樣

主動接 V-ing → 被動照用 V-ing

V-ing 不用改
I saw him crossing the street.
→ 主動
He was seen crossing the street.
→ 被動：照用 crossing

by 可以省略，與不用 by 的被動片語

by 什麼時候省略

by + 泛指的人

做動作的是 people、someone、they 這種泛指的人，或不知道是誰

Rice is grown in Taiwan.
→ 不說 by people
My umbrella has been taken.
→ 不知道誰拿走

固定片語（要整組背）

不用 by
片語意思
be interested in對…有興趣
be surprised at對…感到驚訝
be pleased / satisfied with對…滿意
be covered with被…覆蓋
be filled with充滿…

made 與 known 的不同介系詞

片語意思例句
be made of由…製成（看得出原料）
This table is made of wood. （這張桌子是木頭做的）
be made from由…製成（看不出原料）
Paper is made from trees. （紙是用樹做的）
be made in在…（地方）製造
This phone was made in Taiwan. （這支手機是台灣製造的）
be known to為…（人）所知
The singer is known to everyone. （大家都認識這位歌手）
be known for因…而有名
Tainan is known for its snacks. （台南以小吃聞名）
be known as以…（身分、名字）為人所知
Hualien is known as a great place to hike. （花蓮是有名的登山好地方）

對比頁：容易搞混的四組

interested 與 interesting

I am interested in music.
→ 人「感到」有興趣：-ed
The book is interesting.
→ 東西「令人」覺得有趣：-ing

happened 與 was happened

The accident happened last night.
→ happen 沒有被動
The car was hit by a bus.
→ hit 是及物動詞，可以被動

is building 與 is being built

The workers are building a bridge.
→ 工人在蓋：主動
A bridge is being built.
→ 橋被蓋：被動

made us clean 與 were made to clean

Mom made us clean up.
→ 主動：原形
We were made to clean up.
→ 被動：補 to

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
The car washed by my dad. → The car was washed by my dad.
被動一定要有 be 動詞。
2
These cookies was made by Amy. → These cookies were made by Amy.
單複數看新主詞 these cookies。
3
The letter is sent by she. → The letter is sent by her.
by 後面用受格。
4
The bridge is building now. → The bridge is being built now.
進行式被動是 is being + p.p.。
5
We were made clean the room. → We were made to clean the room.
使役動詞改被動要補 to。
