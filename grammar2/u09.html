She gave me her notebook, so now it's mine. I wrote my name on it myself.一句話裡用到五種：主格、受格、所有格、所有代名詞、反身代名詞。
位置決定用哪一格：動詞前面當主詞 → 主格；動詞或介系詞後面 → 受格（with him、for us、between you and me）。
注意 its（它的）沒有撇號；it's 是 it is。
PART 2

所有代名詞：mine、yours、hers…

所有代名詞＝所有格 ＋ 名詞，本身就包含名詞，後面不能再接名詞。

This is my bag. = This bag is mine.（不能說 This is mine bag.）
I found my umbrella, but Sara can't find hers.hers = her umbrella

比較時要比「同類」的東西

My phone is newer than yours.我的手機比你的（手機）新。（不是 than you，不能拿手機和人比）

雙重所有格：a ＋ 名詞 ＋ of ＋ 所有代名詞

Kevin is a friend of mine.Kevin 是我的一個朋友。（不說 a my friend）
PART 3

反身代名詞：-self／-selves

① 主詞和受詞是同一個人

Be careful! Don't cut yourself.小心！別割到自己。
He looked at himself in the mirror.（如果寫 him，就變成看著「另一個男生」）

② 強調「親自、本人」：放在主詞後面或句尾，拿掉句子仍然完整。

I made this cake myself. = I myself made this cake.這個蛋糕是我自己做的。

常用片語

片語意思例句
by oneself獨自（= alone）She lives by herself.
enjoy oneself玩得愉快We enjoyed ourselves at the party.
help oneself (to)自己拿（食物）Help yourself to some cookies.
teach oneself自學He taught himself to play the guitar.
talk to oneself自言自語Grandpa often talks to himself.
反身代名詞不能當主詞：Myself went there. ✗
PART 4

one、another、the other、others、the others

關鍵：有沒有 the＝範圍是不是「固定、說完就沒了」。

情況句型例句
兩個：一個…另一個one … the otherI have two cats. One is black, and the other is white.
三個：一個…另一個…最後一個one … another … the otherI have three pens. One is red, another is blue, and the other is green.
一個…其餘全部one … the othersOne of the boys is from Japan. The others are from Taiwan.
一些…其餘全部some … the othersSome students walk to school; the others take the bus.
一些…另一些（沒說完）some … othersSome people like coffee; others like tea.
再一個（不限定）another ＋ 單數This shirt is too small. Can I try another?
記法：the other＝剩下的「最後一個」；the others＝剩下的「全部」；another＝「再一個」（還可能有更多）；others＝「其他一些」（沒說完）。
another = an ＋ other，後面接單數名詞；other 後面接複數名詞：other students。
PART 5

one、ones 和 it

代名詞代替例句
it前面提過的「那一個」（同一個東西）I lost my pen. I can't find it.
one同一類的「任何一個」（不是同一個）I lost my pen. I need to buy a new one.
ones同一類的複數These shoes are too small. I want bigger ones.
— Do you have an eraser? — Yes, I have one.隨便一個橡皮擦 → one
— Do you like the red bike? — Yes, I want to buy it.就是那台紅色腳踏車 → it
PART 6

both、either、neither 與 all、any、none

全部都是其中任一個全部都不是
兩個both（複數動詞）either（單數動詞）neither（單數動詞）
三個以上allanynone
Both of my sisters are teachers.我兩個姊姊都是老師。
— Tea or coffee? — Either is fine.兩個選一個都可以。
Neither of the answers is correct.兩個答案都不對。
— Apple, orange, or banana? — Any will do.三個以上選一個都可以。
None of my friends knows the answer.我的朋友沒有一個知道。（none 後面單、複數動詞都有人用，考試以單數較保險）
PART 7

部分否定：not all、not every、not both

all、every、both 加上 not，意思是「不是全部都…」，不是「全部都不」。

Not all students like math.並不是所有學生都喜歡數學。（有些喜歡、有些不喜歡）
I don't know both of them.我不是兩個都認識。（只認識其中一個）

要說「全部都不」：兩個用 neither，三個以上用 none。

I know neither of them. / None of them came.兩個我都不認識。／他們一個都沒來。
PART 8

數量代名詞 ＋ of ＋ the／所有格 ＋ 名詞

all / most / many / some / several / a few / both / half / one fourth ＋ of the (my…) ＋ 名詞

Most of my classmates have smartphones.我大部分的同學都有智慧型手機。
Some of the milk is gone.有些牛奶不見了。（動詞看 of 後面的名詞：milk 不可數 → is）

each、every、one：一定是單數

Each of the students has a locker.每個學生都有一個置物櫃。
One of my friends lives in Japan.我的一個朋友住在日本。（主詞是 one）
Every student has a locker.every 後面直接接單數名詞，不能說 every of。

COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

This is mine book.This is my book. / This book is mine.mine 後面不接名詞；接名詞用所有格 my。
My bike is faster than you.My bike is faster than yours.拿腳踏車和腳踏車比 → yours（= your bike）。
The dog is wagging it's tail.The dog is wagging its tail.「它的」是 its；it's = it is。
She cut her when she was cooking.She cut herself when she was cooking.主詞和受詞是同一人，用反身代名詞。
I have two brothers. One is tall, and another is short.One is tall, and the other is short.只有兩個時，剩下的那一個用 the other。
I lost my umbrella. I have to buy it.I have to buy a new one.買的是「另一把同類的傘」，不是原本那把 → one。
Both of them is my classmates.Both of them are my classmates.both 用複數動詞。
Each of the boys have a bike.Each of the boys has a bike.each 是單數，動詞用 has。
