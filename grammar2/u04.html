PART 1

不定詞的三種用法

不定詞＝to ＋ 原形動詞。to 後面一定是原形，不管主詞是誰、時態是什麼。

① 當名詞：「做某件事」

可以當主詞、受詞、補語。當主詞時通常改用 It 放前面，把 to V 移到後面。

To learn a new language takes time. = It takes time to learn a new language.學一種新語言需要時間。（主詞）
Leo wants to be a doctor.Leo 想當醫生。（受詞）
My plan is to save money for a trip.我的計畫是存錢去旅行。（補語）

② 當形容詞：放在名詞後面，說明「用來做…的」

I'm hungry. Is there anything to eat?有什麼可以吃的嗎？
She has a lot of homework to do tonight.她今晚有很多功課要做。
注意介系詞：原本要介系詞的，要保留在句尾。
write on paper → some paper to write on　／　talk to someone → someone to talk to　／　sit on a chair → a chair to sit on

③ 當副詞：說明「為了…」（目的）或「因為…」（情緒的原因）

I got up early (in order) to catch the first bus.我很早起床，為了趕上第一班公車。（目的）
We were happy to hear the good news.我們聽到這個好消息很高興。（情緒的原因）
PART 2

後面接 to V 的動詞

這些動詞多半在講「還沒做、打算要做」的事：想要、希望、決定、計畫、同意、拒絕…

動詞意思例句
want / would like想要I'd like to order a salad.
hope / wish希望We hope to see you again.
decide決定They decided to stay home.
plan計畫Ken plans to visit Tainan.
agree / refuse同意／拒絕He refused to answer the question.
promise答應Dad promised to take us to the zoo.
learn學會My sister is learning to drive.
need需要You need to drink more water.
expect預期I didn't expect to win.
afford負擔得起We can't afford to buy a new car.
fail沒能He failed to catch the train.
seem似乎The kids seem to be tired.
對照記：Unit 3 的 enjoy、finish、mind、keep、avoid 接 V-ing；這裡的 want、hope、decide、plan、agree 接 to V。
PART 3

動詞 ＋ 人 ＋ to V

要別人去做某事時，人放中間，後面接 to V：ask、tell、want、would like、expect、teach、remind、encourage

Mom asked me to buy some eggs.媽媽叫我去買蛋。
The coach wants us to practice more.教練要我們多練習。（不是 wants that we…）

否定：not 放在 to 前面

The teacher told us not to run in the hallway.老師叫我們不要在走廊跑。
例外：make、let、have（使役動詞）和 see、hear（感官動詞）後面接原形，不加 to：Mom let me go out.（詳見 Unit 5）
PART 4

疑問詞 ＋ to V

what、how、where、when、which、who 後面加 to V，表示「該…」，常放在 know、learn、decide、tell、show、ask 後面。

I don't know what to say.我不知道該說什麼。
Can you show me how to use this app?你可以教我怎麼用這個 App 嗎？
We haven't decided where to go this summer.我們還沒決定今年夏天要去哪裡。

可以改寫成名詞子句（主詞 ＋ should）：

I don't know what to say. = I don't know what I should say.
why 不能接 to V：I don't know why to go. ✗ → I don't know why I should go. ✓
PART 5

重要句型

It is ＋ 形容詞 ＋ (for 人) ＋ to V

It is important to wear a helmet.戴安全帽很重要。（= To wear a helmet is important.）
It is hard for me to wake up early.對我來說早起很難。

too ＋ 形容詞／副詞 ＋ to V：太…以致於不能…

The box is too heavy for me to carry.這箱子太重了，我搬不動。（句子本身沒有 not，但意思是否定的）
= The box is so heavy that I can't carry it.改成 so…that 時，要補上 can't 和受詞 it。

形容詞／副詞 ＋ enough ＋ to V：夠…可以…

Tim is old enough to ride the roller coaster.Tim 年紀夠大，可以坐雲霄飛車。（enough 放在形容詞「後面」）

enough ＋ 名詞 ＋ to V：有足夠的…可以…

We don't have enough time to finish the game.我們沒有足夠的時間把比賽打完。（enough 放在名詞「前面」）
互換：He is too young to drive. = He is not old enough to drive.
PART 6

need ＋ V-ing：主動的樣子，被動的意思

主詞是「東西」時，need ＋ V-ing 等於 need to be ＋ p.p.，表示「需要被…」。

My bike needs fixing. = My bike needs to be fixed.我的腳踏車需要修理。
The plants need watering. = The plants need to be watered.這些植物需要澆水。

主詞是「人」、自己去做時，用 need to V：I need to fix my bike.

PART 7

不定詞 vs 動名詞

用法不定詞 to V動名詞 V-ing
當主詞To travel is fun.（多改成 It is fun to travel.）Traveling is fun.
當補語My goal is to win.My hobby is drawing.
當動詞受詞want、hope、decide、plan、agree…enjoy、finish、mind、keep、avoid…
介系詞後面✗ 不能用✓ good at singing
當形容詞✓ something to drink✗
表示目的✓ I went there to study.✗

兩種都可以、意思一樣

like、love、hate、begin、start、continue

It began to snow. = It began snowing.開始下雪了。

兩種都可以、意思不同

動詞＋ to V（還沒做、要去做）＋ V-ing（已經做、正在做）
stop停下來去做另一件事停止正在做的事
remember記得要去做記得做過
forget忘記要去做忘記做過
try努力設法試試看

COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

I want to buying a new phone.I want to buy a new phone.to 後面一定是原形。
She decided going to Japan.She decided to go to Japan.decide 後面接 to V。
Mom asked me buy some milk.Mom asked me to buy some milk.ask ＋ 人 ＋ to V，to 不能少。
The teacher told us to not talk.The teacher told us not to talk.否定的 not 放在 to 前面。
There is no chair to sit.There is no chair to sit on.坐在椅子上是 sit on a chair，介系詞 on 要保留。
He is too young to not drive.He is too young to drive.too…to 本身就有否定意思，不再加 not。
She is enough old to go alone.She is old enough to go alone.enough 放在形容詞「後面」。
I don't know why to do it.I don't know why I should do it.why 不能接 to V。
