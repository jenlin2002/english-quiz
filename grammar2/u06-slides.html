UNIT 6 · GRAMMAR

助動詞

Modal Verbs
能力・義務推測・請求

本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 記住助動詞的四大特性
  • 分清楚 mustn't（禁止）和 don't have to（不必）
  • 用 should、had better、shall 給建議
  • 用 must、may、can't 推測「一定是／可能／不可能」
  • 用 could、would、may 客氣地請求

暖身 3 題

1She can ____ well. (sings / sing)sing
2You ____ wear a seat belt. It's the law. (must / may)must
3It's Sunday. You ____ get up early. (mustn't / don't have to)don't have to

助動詞的四大特性

規則

can、could、may、might、will、would、shall、should、must
  • ① 後面一定接原形動詞
  • ② 不因主詞改變，沒有 -s
  • ③ 否定直接加 not：can't、shouldn't
  • ④ 問句把助動詞移到最前面

例句

examples
She can swim.
→ 不是 can swims
He shouldn't eat so much.
→ 否定加 not
Can you swim?
→ 不用 Do you can
I will be able to go.
→ 兩個助動詞不能連用

can、could、be able to

用法說法例句
現在的能力can = am / is / are able to
Mia can speak three languages. （Mia 會說三種語言）
過去的能力could = was / were able to
I could read when I was four. （我四歲就會讀書）
未來的能力will be able to
You will be able to drive next year. （你明年就能開車了）
允許can
You can sit here. （你可以坐這裡）
請求can / could（較客氣）
Could you help me? （可以請你幫我嗎？）

must 和 have to

肯定：必須

must vs have to

must：說話者覺得一定要（規定）

have to：外在情況逼得非做不可

You must wear a seat belt.
→ 規定
I have to get up at six tomorrow.
→ 因為要出遊
I had to walk home yesterday.
→ must 沒有過去式

否定：意思完全不同

最常考
You mustn't touch the paintings.
→ 不可以（禁止）
You don't have to bring lunch.
→ 不必（要帶也可以）

回答 Must I…?No, you don't have to. / No, you needn't.

建議：should、had better、shall

助動詞意思例句
should = ought to應該
You should drink more water. （你應該多喝水）
had better最好（不然會有麻煩）
You'd better take an umbrella. （你最好帶把傘）
had better not最好不要
You'd better not be late again. （你最好別再遲到）
Shall I / we…?要不要…？
Shall we go for a walk? （我們去散步好嗎？）
should have p.p.早該…（卻沒做）
You should have told me earlier. （你早該告訴我）
had better 不是過去式；否定的 not 放在 better 後面。

may、might 與 may be／maybe

可能、允許

may / might
It may rain this afternoon.
→ 可能會下雨
Ken might come to the party.
→ 也許會來（可能性更低）
May I use your phone?
→ 客氣地請求允許

may be 和 maybe

分開寫還是連在一起？

may be：助動詞 + be，當句子的動詞

maybe：副詞（= perhaps），常放句首

He may be at home now.
→ 句中
Maybe he is at home now.
→ 句首，還要有動詞 is

will、would、need、used to

will、would

未來、請求
Would you open the window, please?
→ 比 Will you 客氣
I'd like a glass of water.
→ would like = want，較禮貌
Dad said he would buy me a bike.
→ 轉述時 will 變 would

need、used to

兩種身分

need 當助動詞：只用在否定和問句，後接原形

He needn't worry. = He doesn't need to worry.
→ 兩種不能混用
I used to be afraid of dogs.
→ 以前常常，現在不會
Did you use to live here?
→ 問句變 use to

用助動詞推測：有多確定？

把握度助動詞例句
一定是must + V
The lights are on. They must be home. （燈亮著，他們一定在家）
有可能may / might / could + V
She may be in the library. （她可能在圖書館）
不可能can't + V
He just ate. He can't be hungry. （他剛吃過，不可能餓）
過去一定must have p.p.
It must have rained last night. （昨晚一定下過雨）
過去不可能can't have p.p.
You can't have seen her here. （你不可能在這裡看到她）
推測「一定不是」用 can't，不用 mustn't。

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
She can speaks English. → She can speak English.
助動詞後面接原形。
2
You should to see a doctor. → You should see a doctor.
should 後面直接接原形。
3
I will can go with you. → I will be able to go with you.
兩個助動詞不能連用。
4
Must I go? — No, you mustn't. → No, you don't have to.
mustn't 是禁止，不必用 don't have to。
5
He just ate. He mustn't be hungry. → He can't be hungry.
推測「不可能」用 can't。

快問快答 8 題

1I ____ swim when I was five. (can / could)could
2You ____ park here. It's a fire lane. (mustn't / don't have to)mustn't
3We ____ wear uniforms on Fridays. (don't have to / mustn't)don't have to
4You'd better ____ late. (not be / not to be)not be
5____ he is sick. (Maybe / May be)Maybe
6She ____ be tired. She slept all day. (can't / mustn't)can't
7I ____ finish it yesterday. (must / had to)had to
8____ you pass the salt, please? (Could / Must)Could
