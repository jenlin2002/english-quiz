Gerunds
V-ing當名詞

本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 知道動名詞（V-ing）可以放在哪些位置
  • 記住後面只能接 V-ing 的動詞
  • 介系詞後面一律接 V-ing（包括 look forward to）
  • 分清楚 be used to V-ing 和 used to V

暖身 3 題

1I enjoy ____ music. (listen / listening to)listening to
2____ is good exercise. (Swim / Swimming)Swimming
3Thank you for ____ me. (help / helping)helping

什麼是動名詞？

一個句子通常只有一個主要動詞。想再放進另一個動作，就把它變成名詞：V-ing。

位置說明例句
主詞動詞用單數
Swimming in the sea is fun. （在海裡游泳很好玩）
補語放在 be 後面
My hobby is collecting stamps. （我的嗜好是收集郵票）
動詞的受詞enjoy、finish…
Ella enjoys baking cookies. （Ella 喜歡烤餅乾）
介系詞的受詞for、at、in…
Thank you for helping me. （謝謝你幫我）
禁止告示No + V-ing
No parking. （禁止停車）
進行式也是 V-ing，但前面有 be 動詞、表示「正在做」：He is reading.（進行式）／His job is reading news.（動名詞當補語）

後面只能接 V-ing 的動詞

常考動詞

已經在做、做過的事
動詞意思
enjoy喜歡、享受
finish做完
mind介意
keep一直
avoid避免
quit / give up戒掉、放棄

例句

examples
We enjoy camping by the river.
→ 喜歡露營
Do you mind opening the window?
→ 介意開窗嗎？
The baby kept crying all night.
→ 一直哭
口訣：享受完成、介意練習、一直避免、放棄討厭、承認否認、考慮懷念、建議 ＋ V-ing

介系詞後面一律接 V-ing

介系詞 + V-ing

preposition + V-ing

in、on、at、of、for、about、without、before、after

Wash your hands before eating.
→ 吃東西前
She left without saying goodbye.
→ 沒說再見就走了

形容詞 + 介系詞

常考片語
片語意思
be good at擅長
be interested in對…有興趣
be tired of厭倦
be afraid of害怕
be worried about擔心
be worth值得
Ken is good at drawing.
→ 擅長畫畫

陷阱：這些 to 是介系詞

to + V-ing

to 後面接 V-ing

look forward to、be used to、the key to

I'm looking forward to seeing you.
→ 不是 to see
Amy is used to getting up at six.
→ 習慣六點起床

be used to 和 used to

意思完全不同

be used to + V-ing：習慣於（現在）

used to + 原形：以前常常（現在不會）

I am used to walking to school.
→ 我習慣走路上學
I used to walk to school.
→ 我以前都走路上學

接 V-ing 的常用片語

片語意思例句
feel like + V-ing想要
I don't feel like going out tonight. （我今晚不想出門）
can't help + V-ing忍不住
We couldn't help laughing. （我們忍不住笑了）
spend 時間 (in) + V-ing花時間做…
He spends two hours practicing the piano. （他花兩小時練鋼琴）
have fun (in) + V-ing…得很開心
We had fun playing in the snow. （我們在雪裡玩得很開心）
have trouble (in) + V-ing做…有困難
I have trouble remembering names. （我很難記住名字）
How about + V-ing?…如何？
How about going hiking? （去爬山如何？）
go + V-ing去做（休閒活動）
Let's go swimming. （我們去游泳吧）

接 to V 和 V-ing 意思不同

大原則：V-ing 是「已經做的事」，to V 是「還沒做、要去做的事」。

stop

停止 vs 停下來去做
He stopped talking.
→ 停止說話
He stopped to talk to me.
→ 停下來去跟我說話

remember

做過 vs 要去做
I remember locking the door.
→ 記得鎖過了
Remember to lock the door.
→ 記得要去鎖

forget

做過 vs 要去做
I'll never forget meeting you.
→ 忘不了遇見你
Don't forget to call Grandma.
→ 別忘了要打電話

try

試試看 vs 努力設法
Try adding some salt.
→ 試試看加鹽
I tried to open the box.
→ 努力想打開

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
I enjoy to play basketball. → I enjoy playing basketball.
enjoy 後面只能接 V-ing。
2
Playing video games are fun. → Playing video games is fun.
動名詞當主詞用單數動詞。
3
She is good at swim. → She is good at swimming.
介系詞後面接 V-ing。
4
I'm looking forward to see you. → I'm looking forward to seeing you.
look forward to 的 to 是介系詞。
5
Remember turning off the lights when you leave. → Remember to turn off the lights when you leave.
「要去做」還沒做的事用 to V。
