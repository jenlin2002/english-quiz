UNIT 18 · GRAMMAR

疑問句

Questions
Yes / NoWh- / How

本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 把直述句改成 Yes／No 問句
  • 用「疑問詞 + 問句」問時間、地點、人、原因…
  • 知道問主詞時不借 do（Who broke it?）
  • 分清楚 How many / much / often / long / soon / far
  • 依畫線部分造問句

暖身 3 題

1— ____ do you live? — In Taipei. (Where / When)Where
2— ____ is it? — NT$100. (How many / How much)How much
3____ she like cats? (Does / Is)Does

Yes／No 問句

原句問句簡答
She is a nurse.
Is she a nurse? （她是護理師嗎？）
Yes, she is.
They can swim.
Can they swim? （他們會游泳嗎？）
Yes, they can.
He likes cats.
Does he like cats? （他喜歡貓嗎？）
No, he doesn't.
You went out.
Did you go out? （你出門了嗎？）
Yes, I did.
選擇問句（A or B?）不能用 Yes／No 回答：Do you want tea or coffee? — Tea, please.

疑問詞問句：疑問詞 + 問句

公式

Wh- + question

疑問詞 + be／助動詞／do + 主詞 + …?

Will you graduate next year? → When will you graduate?
→ 問時間
Is he studying in the library? → Where is he studying?
→ 問地點

問主詞：不借 do

Who / What as subject
Who broke the window?
→ Who 是主詞
Who(m) did you see?
→ Who 是受詞，要借 did
What happened?
→ What 是主詞

各個疑問詞

疑問詞問例句
when / where時間／地點
When did she arrive? / Where does Tom live? （她何時到？／Tom 住哪？）
who / whom人
Who is that man? （那個人是誰？）
whose誰的
Whose bag is this? （這是誰的包包？）
what / which什麼／哪一個
Which color do you like, red or blue? （你喜歡紅色還是藍色？）
why原因
Why are you late? （你為什麼遲到？）
how方法、狀況
How do you go to school? （你怎麼去上學？）
Who is he? 問身分；What does he do? 問職業。

How 家族

問句問回答
How many + 複數數量（可數）Twenty.
How much數量（不可數）／價錢NT$300.
How often頻率Twice a week.
How long多久（for + 一段時間）For five years.
How soon還要多久（in + 一段時間）In ten minutes.
How far距離About two kilometers.
How old / tall年紀／身高She's ten.

常用的 What 問句

問句問回答
What time is it? （現在幾點？）
幾點It's three o'clock.
What day is today? （今天星期幾？）
星期幾It's Monday.
What's the weather like? （天氣如何？）
天氣It's sunny.
What does he do? （他做什麼工作？）
職業He's a lawyer.
What does she look like? （她長什麼樣子？）
外表She's tall.
提議：Why don't we go? = How about going?　回應：Why not?（好啊！）

依畫線部分造問句

三個步驟

steps
  • ① 看畫線部分 → 選疑問詞
  • ② 畫線是主詞 → 疑問詞直接取代
  • ③ 不是主詞 → 改成 Yes／No 問句，刪掉畫線部分，疑問詞放最前面

例子

examples
Ken bought a bike. → What did Ken buy?
→ 受詞：借 did
Amy broke the cup. → Who broke the cup?
→ 主詞：直接換
They swim twice a week. → How often do they swim?
→ 頻率

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
Where you live? → Where do you live?
一般動詞要借 do。
2
What did you bought? → What did you buy?
借了 did 用原形。
3
Who did break the window? → Who broke the window?
問主詞不借 did。
4
How many water do you drink? → How much water do you drink?
water 不可數。
5
How long do you swim? — Twice a week. → How often do you swim?
回答次數用 How often。
