直接問句與間接問句

Where does he live?（直接問）→ Do you know where he lives?（間接問）

PART 1

直接問句：動詞在主詞前面

直接問句就是 Unit 18 學的問句：be 動詞、助動詞或 do 放在主詞前面。

Is he a doctor? / Where does he live? / What is she doing?
PART 2

間接問句：放進另一個句子裡，變回「主詞 ＋ 動詞」

把問句放在 Do you know…? / Can you tell me…? / I don't know… / I wonder… 等句子後面，就變成間接問句。間接問句的語序是：

疑問詞 ＋ 主詞 ＋ 動詞（像一般直述句，不倒裝）
直接問句間接問句
Who is the girl?Do you know who the girl is?
Where is the restroom?Can you tell me where the restroom is?
What time will he come?I don't know what time he will come.
How old are you?May I ask how old you are?
I don't know where she is.不是 where is she。
PART 3

直接問句有 do／does／did：拿掉，動詞還原時態

間接問句不需要 do 幫忙，所以要把 do／does／did 拿掉，它代表的時態和單複數還給動詞。

直接問句拿掉 do 之後間接問句
Where does he live?does → 第三人稱單數現在式Do you know where he lives?
What did she buy?did → 過去式I wonder what she bought.
When do they leave?do → 原形（複數）Tell me when they leave.
PART 4

Yes／No 問句：用 if 或 whether（是否）

Yes／No 問句沒有疑問詞，就用 if 或 whether 帶出，後面一樣是「主詞 ＋ 動詞」。

Is he a doctor? → Are you sure if / whether he is a doctor?
Did she pass the test? → I don't know if she passed the test.
Will he come tonight? → Nobody knows whether he will come tonight.
Has Ken come back? → I wonder if Ken has come back.
這裡的 if 是「是否」，未來就用 will（和 Unit 13「如果」的 if 不同）。
whether … or not 可以放一起：I don't know whether he will come or not.
PART 5

疑問詞本身就是主詞：語序不變

問「誰／什麼」做了某事時（見 Unit 18），疑問詞就是主詞，間接問句的語序和直接問句一樣。

Who broke the window? → Do you know who broke the window?
What happened? → Please tell me what happened.
PART 6

句尾是問號還是句號？

看前面的主要句子：主要句子是問句 → 問號；是直述句或祈使句 → 句號。

Do you know where he lives?主要句子 Do you know 是問句
I don't know where he lives.主要句子 I don't know 是直述句
Tell me where the restroom is.主要句子是祈使句
PART 7

時態一致：主要句子是過去式

主要句子用過去式（asked、wanted to know、didn't know）時，間接問句通常也往過去退一格。

直接問句主要句子是過去式
Where is the station?He asked me where the station was.
Can you swim?She asked if I could swim.
Will it rain?I didn't know if it would rain.
說的是不變的事實時可以保留現在式：The teacher asked us what the capital of Japan is.
PART 8

轉述別人說的話（間接引語基礎）

把別人說的話「轉告」第三個人時，引號拿掉，人稱、時態跟著調整。

原本的話轉述說明
Amy said, "I am tired."Amy said (that) she was tired.直述句：said (that)；I → she；am → was
Ken said to me, "I like your bag."Ken told me (that) he liked my bag.said to me → told me；your → my
Mom asked me, "Are you hungry?"Mom asked me if I was hungry.Yes／No 問句：asked if
He asked, "Where do you live?"He asked where I lived.疑問詞問句：asked ＋ 疑問詞 ＋ S ＋ V
The teacher said, "Be quiet."The teacher told us to be quiet.祈使句：told ＋ 人 ＋ to V（見 Unit 4）

句型整理表

直接問句間接問句

COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

Do you know where is the post office?Do you know where the post office is?間接問句用「主詞 ＋ 動詞」。
I don't know where does he live.I don't know where he lives.拿掉 does，動詞加 -s。
Can you tell me what did she buy?Can you tell me what she bought?拿掉 did，動詞改過去式。
I wonder is he at home.I wonder if he is at home.Yes／No 問句要加 if／whether。
I don't know where he lives?I don't know where he lives.主要句子是直述句，用句號。
She asked me where I live.（昨天問的）She asked me where I lived.主要句子是過去式，間接問句也用過去式。
Amy said she is tired yesterday.Amy said (that) she was tired.轉述過去說的話，時態往過去退。
