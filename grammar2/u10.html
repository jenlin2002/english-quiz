PART 1

形容詞放在哪裡？

① 名詞前面：最常見。

She has a red bike. / This is an easy question.

② be 動詞、連綴動詞後面：look、sound、feel、smell、taste、become、get、seem（見 Unit 5）

The test was easy. / You look tired. / It's getting dark.

③ something、anything、nothing、someone 這類字的後面

I want something cold to drink.我想喝點冰的。（不說 cold something）
Is there anything interesting on TV?

數字 ＋ 名詞 當形容詞：名詞用單數、加連字號

a ten-year-old boy / a two-week vacation / a five-story building（不說 a ten-years-old boy）比較：The boy is ten years old.
PART 2

分詞當形容詞：V-ing 和 p.p.

形式意思例句
V-ing主動、正在…a crying baby（正在哭的寶寶）
the rising sun（正在升起的太陽）
p.p.被動、已經…a broken window（被打破的窗戶）
fallen leaves（落下的葉子）

情緒類（interesting／interested、boring／bored…）見 Unit 5。

分詞後面還有其他字時，整組放在名詞後面：

The girl playing the piano is my sister.在彈鋼琴的那個女孩是我妹妹。
This is a book written by a high school student.這是一本高中生寫的書。
PART 3

常用的形容詞片語：be ＋ 形容詞 ＋ 介系詞

片語意思片語意思
be afraid of害怕be proud of以…為傲
be full of = be filled with充滿be famous for以…聞名
be good at擅長be bad at不擅長
be kind to / nice to對…好be angry with（人）生…的氣
be late for…遲到be absent from缺席
be ready for準備好be different from和…不同
be popular with受…歡迎be similar to和…相似
be careful with小心處理be busy with忙於
Ms. Chen is proud of her students. / Don't be late for school.
PART 4

原級、比較級、最高級的變化

規則原級 → 比較級 → 最高級
單音節：加 -er／-esttall → taller → tallest
e 結尾：加 -r／-stnice → nicer → nicest
子音 ＋ y：去 y 加 -ier／-iesteasy → easier → easiest、happy → happier → happiest
短母音 ＋ 單子音：重複字尾big → bigger → biggest、hot → hotter → hottest
兩音節以上：more／mostbeautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful、famous → more famous → most famous

不規則變化（一定要背）

原級比較級最高級
good / wellbetterbest
bad / illworseworst
many / muchmoremost
littlelessleast
farfarther（距離）／further（程度）farthest / furthest
oldolder（年紀）／elder（排行）oldest / eldest
few 和 little：可數用 few → fewer → fewest；不可數用 little → less → least。I have fewer books and less money than you.
PART 5

原級：as … as（一樣…）

Ken is as tall as his father.Ken 和他爸爸一樣高。
This test is not as (so) hard as the last one.這次考試沒有上次那麼難。（＝上次比較難）
as 和 as 中間一定是原級：as taller as ✗。
倍數放在 as 前面：My room is twice as big as yours.（我房間是你的兩倍大）
PART 6

比較級：兩個比

Tom is taller than Ben. = Ben is shorter than Tom.
Math is more difficult than English for me.
A bus is less expensive than a taxi.公車比計程車便宜。（less ＋ 原級 ＝ 比較不…）

兩者之中比較…的：the ＋ 比較級 ＋ of the two

Amy is the taller of the two girls.Amy 是兩個女孩中比較高的。（這時候要加 the）

比較的對象要「同類」

The weather in Taipei is hotter than that in Tokyo.that ＝ the weather（天氣和天氣比）；複數用 those。
PART 7

最高級：三個以上比

Jade Mountain is the highest mountain in Taiwan.範圍是地方、團體 → in
Leo is the youngest of the three boys.範圍是一群同類 → of（也可用 among）

最高級的其他說法

句型例句
比較級 ＋ than any other ＋ 單數名詞He is taller than any other boy in our class.
比較級 ＋ than all the other ＋ 複數名詞He is taller than all the other boys in our class.
No other ＋ 單數名詞 ＋ is ＋ 比較級 ＋ thanNo other boy in our class is taller than he (is).
No other ＋ 單數名詞 ＋ is ＋ as ＋ 原級 ＋ asNo other boy in our class is as tall as he (is).
one of the ＋ 最高級 ＋ 複數名詞She is one of the best singers in Taiwan.
PART 8

修飾比較級、越來越…、越…越…

修飾用在例句
very / too / so原級It's very cold today.
much / far / a lot / even（…得多）比較級Today is much colder than yesterday.
a little / a bit（…一點）比較級I feel a little better now.
by far / the very最高級This is by far the best movie this year.
very 不能修飾比較級：very taller ✗ → much taller ✓

比較級 and 比較級：越來越…

It's getting colder and colder. / English is becoming more and more important.

The ＋ 比較級…, the ＋ 比較級…：越…就越…

The more you practice, the better you get.你練習越多，就越進步。
PART 9

It is ＋ 形容詞 ＋ of ／ for ＋ 人 ＋ to V

介系詞形容詞在描述常用形容詞例句
of「人」的個性（可以說 You are kind.）kind, nice, polite, rude, foolish, careless, smartIt is kind of you to help me.
for「這件事」的性質easy, hard, important, necessary, dangerousIt is hard for me to wake up early.
判斷方法：把形容詞直接放在人後面試試：You are kind. 說得通 → of；I am hard. 說不通 → for。
SUMMARY

句型整理表

句型意思例句
A ＋ be ＋ as 原級 as ＋ BA 和 B 一樣She is as busy as me.
A ＋ be ＋ not as/so 原級 as ＋ BA 沒有 B 那麼I'm not as busy as her.
A ＋ be ＋ 比較級 than ＋ BA 比 B 更She is busier than me.
the ＋ 比較級 ＋ of the two兩者中比較…的She is the busier of the two.
the ＋ 最高級 ＋ in / of…中最…She is the busiest in her family.
比較級 than any other ＋ 單數比其他任何一個都She is busier than any other person.
one of the ＋ 最高級 ＋ 複數最…之一one of the busiest people
much / far ＋ 比較級…得多much busier
比較級 and 比較級越來越busier and busier
The 比較級…, the 比較級…越…越…The busier she is, the happier she feels.
COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

I want to drink cold something.I want to drink something cold.形容詞放在 something 後面。
He is a twelve-years-old boy.He is a twelve-year-old boy.數字＋名詞當形容詞，名詞用單數。
She is more taller than me.She is taller than me.-er 和 more 不能同時用。
This book is gooder than that one.This book is better than that one.good 是不規則變化。
He is as taller as his brother.He is as tall as his brother.as…as 中間用原級。
Today is very hotter than yesterday.Today is much hotter than yesterday.比較級用 much／far 修飾，不用 very。
He is taller than any boy in his class.He is taller than any other boy in his class.他自己也在班上，要加 other（不能和自己比）。
She is one of the best singer in Taiwan.She is one of the best singers in Taiwan.one of the ＋ 最高級 ＋ 複數名詞。
It is kind for you to help me.It is kind of you to help me.kind 在描述「人」的個性，用 of。
