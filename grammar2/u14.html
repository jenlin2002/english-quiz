附加問句

句子後面加一個小問句：…, isn't it?　…, don't you?　意思是「…，對吧？」

PART 1

附加問句的三個規則

Ken is your classmate, isn't he?Ken 是你的同學，對吧？
  1. 前肯後否、前否後肯：前面是肯定句，附加問句用否定；前面是否定句，附加問句用肯定。
  2. 主詞換成代名詞：Ken → he、Amy → she、my parents → they、the book → it。
  3. 用前面句子的 be 動詞或助動詞；一般動詞就借 do / does / did。否定一律縮寫（isn't、don't、can't）。
Amy isn't here, is she?前面否定 → 後面肯定
Your parents like fish, don't they?一般動詞 like（複數）→ don't
PART 2

各種時態與助動詞

前面的句子用附加問句用例句
is / are / was / were同一個 be 動詞It was cold, wasn't it?
現在式一般動詞do / doesShe plays tennis, doesn't she?
過去式一般動詞didBen lost his key, didn't he?
can / will / should…同一個助動詞You can swim, can't you? / It will rain, won't it?
have / has ＋ p.p.（完成式）have / hasThey have finished, haven't they?
have（擁有，一般動詞）do / doesYou have a dog, don't you?
had to / has todid / doesShe had to leave early, didn't she?
would likewouldYou'd like some tea, wouldn't you?
I am 的附加問句：日常說 I'm late, aren't I?；課本較正式的寫法是 am I not?（am not 不能縮寫成 amn't）。
前面是否定 I'm not late, am I?

PART 4

句子裡有否定的字

never、no、nothing、nobody、seldom、hardly、few、little 雖然沒有 not，但句子已經是否定，附加問句要用肯定。

She never eats meat, does she?她從不吃肉，對吧？
There is nothing in the box, is there?
Nobody came to the party, did they?
注意：unhappy、dislike 這種「字裡面」的否定不算，附加問句仍用否定：He is unhappy, isn't he?
PART 5

祈使句與 Let's

句型附加問句例句語氣
Let's …shall we?Let's go to the movies, shall we?提議（我們一起）
Let us / Let me …will you?Let me help you, will you?請求對方允許
祈使句（命令、請求）will you? / won't you?Close the door, will you?請對方做
否定祈使句will you?Don't be late, will you?提醒
邀請won't you?Have some cake, won't you?熱情邀請
PART 6

句子裡有兩個子句

附加問句跟著主要子句（真正要問的那一句）。

You know where he lives, don't you?主要子句是 You know → don't you
She was sleeping when you called, wasn't she?主要子句是 She was sleeping → wasn't she
例外：I think、I believe 開頭時，附加問句跟著後面的子句：I think Ken is right, isn't he?
PART 7

怎麼回答附加問句

不管問句怎麼問，事實是肯定就說 Yes，事實是否定就說 No。和中文的「對／不對」不一樣！

— You aren't hungry, are you?
— Yes, I am.（我餓）／ No, I'm not.（我不餓）中文會說「不，我餓」「對，我不餓」，英文看事實，不看問法。
— Ken didn't come, did he?
— No, he didn't.對，他沒來。（事實是沒來 → No）

COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

Amy is your sister, isn't Amy?Amy is your sister, isn't she?附加問句的主詞要換成代名詞。
You like pizza, aren't you?You like pizza, don't you?like 是一般動詞，借 do。
He went home, doesn't he?He went home, didn't he?went 是過去式 → did。
She can dance, can not she?She can dance, can't she?附加問句的否定要縮寫。
He never lies, doesn't he?He never lies, does he?never 已經是否定 → 後面用肯定。
That is your bike, isn't that?That is your bike, isn't it?this／that 在附加問句裡用 it。
Let's have lunch, don't we?Let's have lunch, shall we?Let's 的附加問句用 shall we。
There is a bank here, isn't it?There is a bank here, isn't there?There is／are 的附加問句仍用 there。
