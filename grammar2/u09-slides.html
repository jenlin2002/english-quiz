Pronouns

本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 正確選用主格、受格、所有格、所有代名詞、反身代名詞
  • 分清楚 one … the other、another、others、the others
  • 知道什麼時候用 it、什麼時候用 one
  • 用 both、either、neither、all、any、none 並配對動詞
  • 看懂部分否定 not all、not both

暖身 3 題

1This book is ____. (my / mine)mine
2She hurt ____. (her / herself)herself
3I have two pens. One is red; ____ is blue. (another / the other)the other

動詞前當主詞用主格；動詞、介系詞後面用受格。its（它的）沒有撇號，it's = it is。

所有代名詞

後面不接名詞

所有格 + 名詞 = 所有代名詞
This is my bag. = This bag is mine.
→ 不能說 mine bag
I found my umbrella, but Sara can't find hers.
→ hers = her umbrella

比較同類、雙重所有格

常考
My phone is newer than yours.
→ 手機比手機，不是 than you
Kevin is a friend of mine.
→ 不說 a my friend

反身代名詞

兩種用法

-self / -selves

① 主詞＝受詞

Don't cut yourself.
→ 別割到自己

② 強調「親自」

I made this cake myself.
→ 我自己做的

常用片語

不能當主詞
片語意思
by oneself獨自
enjoy oneself玩得愉快
help oneself (to)自己拿
teach oneself自學
We enjoyed ourselves at the party.
→ 玩得很開心

one、another、the other、others

情況句型例句
兩個one … the other
I have two cats. One is black, and the other is white. （一隻黑、另一隻白）
三個one … another … the other
One is red, another is blue, and the other is green. （紅、藍、最後一個綠）
其餘全部one / some … the others
Some walk to school; the others take the bus. （其餘的人搭公車）
另一些（沒說完）some … others
Some people like coffee; others like tea. （有些人喜歡茶）
再一個another
Can I try another? （我可以試另一件嗎？）
有 the＝範圍固定、說完就沒了：the other（最後一個）、the others（剩下全部）。

one、ones 和 it

it：就是那一個

同一個東西
I lost my pen. I can't find it.
→ 找的是原本那支
Do you like the red bike? I want to buy it.
→ 就是那台

one / ones：同類的任一個

同一類，不是同一個
I need to buy a new one.
→ 另一支筆
These shoes are small. I want bigger ones.
→ 複數用 ones

both、either、neither、all、any、none

全部都是其中任一個全部都不是
兩個both（複數動詞）either（單數動詞）neither（單數動詞）
三個以上allanynone

兩個

Both of my sisters are teachers.
→ 都是
Neither of the answers is correct.
→ 都不對

三個以上

— Apple or banana or grape? — Any will do.
→ 任一個都可以
None of my friends knows it.
→ 一個都不知道

部分否定與數量代名詞

部分否定：不是全部都

not all / not every / not both
Not all students like math.
→ 有些喜歡、有些不喜歡
I don't know both of them.
→ 只認識其中一個

全部都不：兩個 neither；三個以上 none

數量 + of + 名詞

most / some / each / one of
Most of my classmates have phones.
→ 動詞看 of 後面的名詞
Each of the students has a locker.
→ each 一定單數
One of my friends lives in Japan.
→ 主詞是 one

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
This is mine book. → This is my book.
mine 後面不接名詞。
2
My bike is faster than you. → My bike is faster than yours.
腳踏車和腳踏車比。
3
She cut her when cooking. → She cut herself.
主詞＝受詞用反身代名詞。
4
I have two brothers. One is tall, and another is short. → One is tall, and the other is short.
兩個中的最後一個用 the other。
5
Each of the boys have a bike. → Each of the boys has a bike.
each 用單數動詞。

快問快答 8 題

1Is this ____ cap? (your / yours)your
2Come with ____. (we / us)us
3Help ____, kids! (yourself / yourselves)yourselves
4Some like red; ____ like blue. (others / the other)others
5I lost my key. I need a new ____. (it / one)one
6____ of my parents work. (Both / Either)Both
7____ of the two answers is right. (Neither / None)Neither
8Each of us ____ a book. (have / has)has
