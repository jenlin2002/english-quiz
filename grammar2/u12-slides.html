本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 用 in、at、on 表示地方和時間
  • 用 in front of、between、across from 等說位置
  • 分清楚 for / since、until / by、in / into
  • 說交通方式：by bus、on foot
  • 記住片語裡的介系詞

暖身 3 題

1I get up ____ 6:30. (at / on)at
2My birthday is ____ May. (on / in)in
3I go to school ____ bus. (by / on)by

地方：in、at、on

介系詞想像成例句
in空間、範圍裡面
The kids are playing in the park. （孩子們在公園裡玩）
at一個點、地址
I'll meet you at the bus stop. （我在公車站跟你碰面）
on一個面、一條線
There's a picture on the wall. （牆上有一幅畫）
on大型交通工具
I saw Amy on the bus. （我在公車上看到 Amy）
go home、come here、go downtown 不加介系詞；但 at home 要用 at。

位置與方向

位置

where?
介系詞意思
in front of / behind前面／後面
next to / beside旁邊
between A and B兩者之間
among三者以上之中
across from對面
under / above下面／上方

方向、移動

in 是狀態，into 是移動
He ran into the room.
→ 進入（移動）
She got out of the car.
→ 從…出來
Walk across the street.
→ 橫越
We walked along the river.
→ 沿著

時間：at、on、in

介系詞用在例句
at時刻、noon、night、節日
The movie starts at 7:30. （電影七點半開始）
on星期幾、日期、某一天
We have P.E. on Monday. （我們星期一有體育課）
in月、季節、年、早上／晚上
I was born in 2013. （我 2013 年出生）
on指定某天的早上
We left on Sunday morning. （我們星期天早上出發）
口訣：at 點、on 天、in 長。this、next、last、every 前面不加介系詞：next week（不說 in next week）。

for、since、until、by、during

持續多久

for / since / during
I've lived here for five years.
→ for + 一段時間
I've lived here since 2021.
→ since + 起點
Don't talk during the test.
→ 在…期間

until 和 by

持續 vs 期限
I'll stay here until five.
→ 一直待到五點
I'll be back by five.
→ 五點前（最晚）會回來
Hand in your report by Friday.
→ 最晚期限

by、with、in、for、about

介系詞意思例句
by + 交通工具搭乘（不加 a / the）
I go to school by bus. （我搭公車上學）
on foot走路
She goes to school on foot. （她走路上學）
with和…一起；有…特徵
I live with my grandparents. （我和爺爺奶奶住）
in穿著；用（語言）
Who is the man in the black coat? （穿黑外套的人是誰？）
about關於
This is a book about dinosaurs. （這是一本關於恐龍的書）

片語中的介系詞

介系詞常見片語
tobe kind to、listen to、belong to
forbe ready for、be late for、wait for、thank … for
withbe angry with、be patient with、What's wrong with you?
ofbe afraid of、be full of、be proud of、take care of
about / atbe worried about、talk about / be good at、look at
in / onbe interested in、in time（及時）/ on time（準時）、on sale
介系詞後面接名詞、受格（with me）或 V-ing（before eating）。

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
I was born on 2012. → I was born in 2012.
年份用 in。
2
See you in next week. → See you next week.
next 前面不加介系詞。
3
I go to school by a bus. → I go to school by bus.
by + 交通工具不加 a。
4
Let's go to home. → Let's go home.
go home 不加 to。
5
Please finish it until Friday. → Please finish it by Friday.
最晚期限用 by。
