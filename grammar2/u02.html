被動語態

PART 1

什麼時候用被動？

公式：be 動詞 ＋ 過去分詞（p.p.）（＋ by 做動作的人）

中文常常不分主動被動（「信寫好了」），英文一定要分清楚。下面三種情況會用被動：

① 不知道是誰做的

My bike was stolen last night.我的腳踏車昨晚被偷了。（不知道小偷是誰）

② 是誰做的不重要，或大家都知道

English is spoken in many countries.很多國家說英語。

③ 想強調「東西」而不是「人」

This temple was built more than 200 years ago.這座廟是兩百多年前蓋的。
先檢查：只有「及物動詞」（後面有受詞的動詞）才能改被動。happen、arrive、die、sleep 這些後面沒有受詞的動詞，沒有被動。
The accident happened at noon. ✓（不說 was happened）
PART 2

主動改被動：四個步驟

主動：Kevin cleans the classroom.
  1. 受詞搬到最前面當主詞：The classroom …
  2. 看原句時態，決定 be 動詞的時態（cleans 是現在式 → is / am / are）
  3. 看新主詞的單複數，決定 be 動詞的人稱（the classroom 單數 → is）
  4. 動詞改 p.p.，原來的主詞放到 by 後面（代名詞要改受格：he → him、they → them）
被動：The classroom is cleaned by Kevin.
口訣：時態看「舊句子」，單複數看「新主詞」。
They planted these trees. → These trees were planted by them.planted 是過去式 → were／was；these trees 是複數 → were；they 改受格 them。
PART 3

各時態的被動

被動的祕訣：先把 be 動詞放進原本的時態，再加 p.p.

時態被動公式例句
現在簡單式am / is / are ＋ p.p.The store is opened at 9 every day.
過去簡單式was / were ＋ p.p.The windows were broken by the storm.
未來式will be ＋ p.p.
am / is / are going to be ＋ p.p.		The results will be announced tomorrow.
現在進行式am / is / are being ＋ p.p.The road is being repaired now.
過去進行式was / were being ＋ p.p.The dog was being washed when I came in.
現在完成式have / has been ＋ p.p.The tickets have been sold out.
過去完成式had been ＋ p.p.The cake had been eaten before we arrived.
助動詞can / must / should… ＋ be ＋ p.p.Your phone must be turned off in class.
看出規律：進行式多一個 being，完成式多一個 been，助動詞後面是原形 be。
PART 4

特殊句型的被動

疑問句的被動

先把它當成肯定句改被動，再把 be 動詞（或助動詞）搬到前面。

Did Ann make this card? → Was this card made by Ann?
Who invented the telephone? → By whom was the telephone invented?口語也常說 Who was the telephone invented by?

授與動詞（give、send、show、teach…）有兩種被動

這類動詞後面有「人」和「東西」兩個受詞，所以哪一個都可以當新主詞：

Grandma gave me a watch.
→ I was given a watch by Grandma.「人」當主詞
→ A watch was given to me by Grandma.「東西」當主詞，人前面要補介系詞 to（buy、make 用 for）

感官動詞、使役動詞的被動：要補 to

主動時 see / hear / make 後面接原形動詞，改被動時原形前面要加 to：

The teacher made us clean the room. → We were made to clean the room.老師要我們打掃教室。
I heard him sing. → He was heard to sing.

如果主動用 V-ing，被動照樣用 V-ing：He was seen crossing the street.

祈使句的被動：Let ＋ 受詞 ＋ be ＋ p.p.

Close the door. → Let the door be closed.這種說法很正式，日常少用，考試偶爾出現。

by 什麼時候可以省略？

做動作的人是 people、someone、they 這類「泛指的人」，或根本不知道是誰，by 的部分就省略。

Rice is grown in Taiwan. （不說 by people）
PART 5

不用 by 的被動片語

有些被動已經變成固定片語，後面接的是別的介系詞，要整組背：

片語意思例句
be interested in對…有興趣Leo is interested in robots.
be surprised at對…感到驚訝We were surprised at the news.
be pleased with / be satisfied with對…滿意The coach was pleased with our game.
be covered with被…覆蓋The mountain is covered with snow.
be filled with充滿…The box is filled with old toys.
be known to為…所知（對象）The singer is known to everyone.
be known for因…而有名Tainan is known for its snacks.
be known as以…身分／名字為人所知Taipei 101 was once known as the tallest building.
be made of由…製成（看得出原料）This table is made of wood.
be made from由…製成（看不出原料）Paper is made from trees.
be made in在…（地方）製造This phone was made in Taiwan.
SUMMARY

句型整理表

主動被動
She writes the report.The report is written (by her).
She wrote the report.The report was written (by her).
She will write the report.The report will be written (by her).
She is writing the report.The report is being written (by her).
She was writing the report.The report was being written (by her).
She has written the report.The report has been written (by her).
She had written the report.The report had been written (by her).
She must write the report.The report must be written (by her).
Did she write the report?Was the report written (by her)?
She showed me the report.I was shown the report. / The report was shown to me.
She made me rewrite the report.I was made to rewrite the report.
Write the report.Let the report be written.
COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

The car washed by my dad yesterday.The car was washed by my dad yesterday.被動一定要有 be 動詞，只有 p.p. 不算完整的動詞。
These cookies was made by Amy.These cookies were made by Amy.be 動詞的單複數看「新主詞」these cookies（複數）。
The letter is sent by she.The letter is sent by her.by 是介系詞，後面的代名詞用受格。
The bridge is building now.The bridge is being built now.橋是「被蓋」，現在進行式的被動是 is being ＋ p.p.。
The homework must be finish today.The homework must be finished today.助動詞 ＋ be 之後一定是 p.p.。
The accident was happened on Main Street.The accident happened on Main Street.happen 是不及物動詞，沒有被動。
We were made clean the classroom.We were made to clean the classroom.使役、感官動詞改被動，原形前面要補 to。
I am interesting in music.I am interested in music.人「感到」有興趣用 -ed；-ing 是「令人」感到有趣（The book is interesting.）。
