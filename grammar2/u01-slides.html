本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 分辨國中會用到的 7 種時態
  • 看到時間線索（every day、now、yesterday、since…）就知道用哪個時態
  • 正確寫出 -s、-ed、-ing 和過去分詞
  • 分清楚 will / be going to、when / while、for / since
  • 知道什麼時候「不能」用進行式和現在完成式

暖身 3 題

1Look! The dog ____ the ball. (chases / is chasing)is chasing
2We ____ to Kenting last summer. (go / went)went
3I ____ this movie twice. (saw / have seen)have seen

先看全貌：時間 × 樣子

時態＝「什麼時候」加上「什麼樣子」。國中先學這 7 種。

時態公式例句
現在簡單式原形 / V-s
She plays tennis every Sunday. （她每週日打網球）
過去簡單式過去式 V-ed
She played tennis yesterday. （她昨天打網球）
未來式will + 原形
She will play tennis tomorrow. （她明天會打網球）
現在進行式am / is / are + V-ing
She is playing tennis now. （她現在正在打網球）
過去進行式was / were + V-ing
She was playing tennis at 5 yesterday. （她昨天五點正在打網球）
現在完成式have / has + p.p.
She has played tennis for six years. （她打網球打了六年）
過去完成式had + p.p.
She had played tennis before she went home. （她回家前已經打過網球）

現在簡單式：習慣、事實、真理

什麼時候用

Simple Present

① 習慣：usually、often、every day

② 現在的狀態、事實

③ 不變的真理

Ken rides his bike to school every morning.
→ 每天的習慣
My aunt lives in Kaohsiung.
→ 現在的狀態
The earth goes around the sun.
→ 科學事實

第三人稱單數要加 -s

he / she / it / 單數名詞

一般：read → reads

s, x, sh, ch, o：加 -es：watches、goes

子音 + y：study → studies（play → plays）

特殊：have → has

She doesn't eat meat.
→ 有 does，動詞變回原形
Does he like dogs?
→ 不是 Does he likes

現在簡單式也可以講未來

① 照時刻表的行程

timetable

火車、公車、節目、課表

The last bus leaves at 10:30 tonight.
→ 末班公車今晚 10:30 開
The concert starts at seven.
→ 演唱會七點開始

② 時間、條件子句

考試最愛考

when、before、after、until、as soon as、if、unless

I will call you when I get home.
→ 不是 when I will get home
If it rains tomorrow, we will stay inside.
→ 主要子句才用 will
口訣：when / if 子句「現在代未來」，另一半才放 will。

過去簡單式：已經結束的事

什麼時候用

Simple Past

時間線索：yesterday、last week、two days ago、in 2020、when I was young

We visited the science museum last Saturday.
→ 上週六，已經結束
When Dad was a boy, he walked to school.
→ 小時候的事
I didn't see the movie. Did you see it?
→ 有 did，動詞用原形

-ed 的四條規則

規則動詞

一般：clean → cleaned

e 結尾：like → liked（只加 d）

子音 + y：cry → cried（play → played）

短母音 + 單一子音：stop → stopped、plan → planned

-ed 怎麼念？常考的不規則動詞

-ed 的三種念法

/ɪd/：字尾是 t、d 音 → wanted、needed

/t/：字尾是 p、k、s、sh、ch、f 音 → helped、cooked、washed

/d/：其他有聲音 → played、called、lived

She wanted to help, so she cooked and cleaned.
→ /ɪd/、/t/、/d/ 都出現了

不規則動詞（一定要背）

原形過去式過去分詞
gowentgone
seesawseen
eatateeaten
writewrotewritten
buyboughtbought
teachtaughttaught
leaveleftleft
putputput

未來式：will 和 be going to

will + 原形

當下決定

① 說話當下才決定

② 個人的猜測、承諾

The phone is ringing. I'll get it.
→ 聽到電話響，當下決定
I think it will be sunny tomorrow.
→ 我覺得、我猜

be going to + 原形

早有計畫

① 事先就計畫好

② 眼前有明顯跡象

I'm going to visit Grandma this weekend.
→ 早就計畫好
Look at those clouds. It's going to rain.
→ 看到烏雲
已經安排好的行程，也可以用現在進行式：My cousin is arriving tomorrow.（come、go、leave、arrive 最常見）

現在進行式：此刻正在做

什麼時候用

Present Progressive

時間線索：now、right now、at the moment、Look!、Listen!

Listen! Someone is knocking on the door.
→ 此刻正在敲門
Jenny is learning to swim this summer.
→ 這段期間正在學

V-ing 怎麼加

拼字規則

一般：read → reading

不發音的 e：make → making（see → seeing）

ie 結尾：die → dying、lie → lying

短母音 + 單一子音：run → running、sit → sitting

The kids are swimming and writing postcards.
→ 重複 m；去 e

這些動詞通常不用進行式

描述「狀態」（心裡的感覺、知道、擁有）而不是動作，就算有 now 也用簡單式。

類別動詞例句
喜好、情緒like、love、hate、want、need
I want some water now. （不說 I am wanting）
想法、知道know、understand、believe、remember
I know the answer. （不說 I am knowing）
擁有have（擁有）、own、belong to
This bag belongs to Amy. （這個包包是 Amy 的）
感官see、hear、smell、taste
This soup smells great. （這湯聞起來很棒）
例外：have 當「吃、度過」是動作，可以用進行式 → We are having lunch.

過去進行式：when 和 while

過去某一刻正在做

Past Progressive

公式：was / were + V-ing

At nine last night, I was doing my homework.
→ 昨晚九點那一刻
I was taking a shower when the phone rang.
→ 長的動作用進行式，突然的用過去式

兩件事同時進行

while = 在…的期間

while 後面常接「一段時間」

While Mom was cooking, the lights went out.
→ 煮飯中突然停電
While Dad was washing the dishes, I was cleaning the table.
→ 兩邊都是進行式

現在完成式：過去的事和現在有關

① 經驗

做過幾次

ever、never、once、twice、before

Have you ever tried stinky tofu?
→ 到現在有沒有吃過
I've never been to Japan.
→ 從來沒去過

② 完成

做完了沒

just、already、yet

I've already finished my report.
→ already 放中間
Has the bus come yet?
→ yet 放句尾

③ 持續

一直到現在

for、since、How long

We've lived here for ten years.
→ 一直住到現在
She's taught here since 2018.
→ 從 2018 年到現在

現在完成式的四個陷阱

for 和 since

多久 vs 從何時

for + 一段時間：for three days、for a long time

since + 起點：since Monday、since I was ten

I have known her for five years.
→ 五年＝一段時間

been to 和 gone to

回來了沒
Amy has been to Japan.
→ 去過，人已經回來
Amy has gone to Japan.
→ 去了，人現在不在這裡

不和「明確的過去時間」連用

過去式 vs 完成式

yesterday、last year、ago、in 2019、When…? → 用過去式

I lost my key yesterday.
→ 不說 have lost … yesterday

短暫動詞不能接 for / since

改用 be dead、have

die、buy、leave、come → 改成狀態

His dog has been dead for two years.
→ 不說 has died for

過去完成式：過去的過去

兩件過去的事，比較早發生的那一件用 had + p.p.。

看時間線排先後

Past Perfect

較早的事：had + p.p.

較晚的事：過去簡單式

When we got to the theater, the movie had already started.
→ 開演（先）→ 到達（後）
She told me she had lost her wallet.
→ 掉錢包（先）→ 告訴我（後）

常見搭配

時間線索

by the time + 過去式

before / when + 過去式

never…before

By the time the police arrived, the thief had run away.
→ 警察到之前，小偷已經跑了
I had never seen snow before I went to Hokkaido.
→ 去北海道以前沒看過雪

時間線索總整理

時態常見的時間線索
現在簡單式every day、usually、often、always、sometimes、never、on Sundays
過去簡單式yesterday、last…、…ago、in 2020、just now、then、When…?
未來式tomorrow、next…、soon、tonight、in + 一段時間（…之後）
現在進行式now、right now、at the moment、Look!、Listen!、these days
過去進行式at 8 last night、at that time、when + 過去式、while
現在完成式already、yet、just、ever、never、before、for、since、so far、recently
過去完成式by the time + 過去式、before / when + 過去式（較早的事）
解題順序：① 找時間線索 → ② 決定時態 → ③ 看主詞決定單複數 → ④ 檢查動詞形式（-s、-ed、-ing、p.p.）

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
She don't like carrots. → She doesn't like carrots.
第三人稱單數用 does。
2
I didn't went to school yesterday. → I didn't go to school yesterday.
did 後面用原形。
3
I will tell him when he will come back. → I will tell him when he comes back.
when 子句用現在式代替未來式。
4
I have seen him two days ago. → I saw him two days ago.
ago 是明確的過去時間，用過去式。
5
We have lived here since three years. → We have lived here for three years.
一段時間用 for，since 接起點。
