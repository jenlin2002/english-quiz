UNIT 15 · GRAMMAR

附和句

So do I / Neither do I
我也是我也不

本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 用 too 和 so 說「…也是」
  • 用 either、neither、nor 說「…也不」
  • 選對助動詞（be、do、did、can、have）
  • 在對話裡附和或表示不同意

暖身 3 題

1— I like cats. — ____ do I. (So / Neither)So
2— I can't swim. — ____ can I. (So / Neither)Neither
3I don't eat fish, and he doesn't, ____. (too / either)either

肯定的附和句：…也是

too：放句尾

… , too

主詞 + 助動詞, too

Ken likes cats, and I do, too.
→ 我也是
My dad was at home, and I was, too.
→ 我也在家

so：倒裝

so + 助動詞 + 主詞

so + 助動詞 + 主詞

Ken likes cats, and so do I.
→ 不是 so I do
My dad was at home, and so was I.
→ 倒裝

否定的附和句：…也不

either：放句尾

… , either

主詞 + 助動詞 + not, either

I can't swim, and Amy can't, either.
→ Amy 也不會

注意：否定句用 either，不用 too

neither / nor：倒裝

neither + 助動詞 + 主詞

neither + 助動詞 + 主詞

I can't swim, and neither can Amy.
→ 不再加 not
I can't swim, nor can Amy.
→ nor 也可以
Tom never cries, and neither does his sister.
→ never 是否定

助動詞怎麼選？

前面用附和句用例句
be 動詞be 動詞
She is tired, and so am I. （她累了，我也是）
現在式一般動詞do / does
I love pizza, and so does my brother. （我愛披薩，我哥也是）
過去式一般動詞did
Leo went to the zoo, and so did Amy. （Leo 去了動物園，Amy 也是）
can / will同一個
Mia can't drive, and neither can her dad. （Mia 不會開車，她爸也不會）
have / has + p.p.have / has
I've been to Japan, and so has Ken. （我去過日本，Ken 也去過）
時態要和前面相同；單複數看「新的主詞」：My parents like jazz, and so does my uncle.

對話裡的附和與不同意

對方說我也是／我也不我不一樣
I like spicy food. （我喜歡辣）
So do I. / Me too.Oh, I don't.
I'm not tired. （我不累）
Neither am I. / Me neither.Really? I am.
I can't sing. （我不會唱歌）
Neither can I.I can.
I went to the party. （我去了派對）
So did I.I didn't.
Me too／Me neither 是口語；寫句子時多用 So do I／Neither do I。

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
Amy likes dogs, and so am I. → Amy likes dogs, and so do I.
likes 是一般動詞 → do。
2
I don't eat fish, and Ken doesn't, too. → …, and Ken doesn't, either.
否定句用 either。
3
He can't swim, and neither can't I. → …, and neither can I.
neither 不再加 not。
4
She went home, and so does Tom. → She went home, and so did Tom.
時態要相同。
5
Ella never eats meat, and so does Lily. → …, and neither does Lily.
never 是否定。

換一種說法

1Ken is tall, and I am, too.Ken is tall, and so am I.
2Amy can't swim, and Ben can't, either.Amy can't swim, and neither can Ben.
3Both my mom and I like cooking.My mom likes cooking, and so do I.
4Neither Tom nor Jay went.Tom didn't go, and neither did Jay.
