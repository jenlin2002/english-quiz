形容詞子句

PART 1

觀念：一個句子當形容詞用

形容詞放在名詞前面（a tall boy）；但如果要說明的內容是一整句話，就放在名詞後面，用關係代名詞接起來。

The boy who is playing tennis is my brother.在打網球的那個男孩是我弟弟。（who 帶出的子句，修飾 the boy）

被修飾的名詞叫做先行詞。依先行詞是人還是物、在子句裡當什麼，選關係代名詞：

先行詞主格受格所有格
人who / thatwhom / who / thatwhose
物、動物which / thatwhich / thatwhose / of which
人 ＋ 物thatthat—
PART 2

把兩句合成一句：三個步驟

The girl is my cousin. The girl sings well.
  1. 找出兩句裡相同的人或物：the girl。
  2. 把第二句的那個字換成關係代名詞：人、當主詞 → who。
  3. 把整個子句放在先行詞後面。
The girl who sings well is my cousin.很會唱歌的那個女孩是我表妹。
The bike is mine. My dad bought the bike yesterday.
→ The bike which my dad bought yesterday is mine.物、在子句裡當受詞 → which；搬到子句最前面。
PART 3

主格：關代後面直接接動詞

I have a friend who lives in Japan.who 在子句裡當主詞，後面接動詞 lives
This is the bus that goes to the zoo.
子句的動詞跟著先行詞：the girl who is…／the girls who are…／a friend who lives…／friends who live…
主格關代不能省略：The boy plays tennis is… ✗
PART 4

受格：關代後面接「主詞 ＋ 動詞」，可以省略

The man (whom / who / that) I met yesterday is a doctor.我昨天遇到的那個人是醫生。（關代是 met 的受詞 → 可省略）
The movie (which / that) we watched was boring.
子句裡不能再出現原本的受詞：The movie which we watched it was boring. ✗（which 已經代替 it 了）

介系詞 ＋ whom ／ which

關代當介系詞的受詞時，介系詞可以放在句尾，也可以搬到關代前面（這時只能用 whom ／ which，不能用 that 或 who）。

The man (whom) she works with is kind. = The man with whom she works is kind.和她一起工作的那個人很親切。
This is the house (which) I lived in. = This is the house in which I lived.
PART 5

所有格 whose：…的

whose 代替 his、her、its、their、名詞's，後面一定接名詞。人、物都可以用。

The girl is my classmate. Her dog is very big.
→ The girl whose dog is very big is my classmate.那個狗很大的女孩是我同學。
I have a book whose cover is red. = I have a book the cover of which is red.物也可以用 of which（人不能用 of which）。
PART 6

that 的特別規則

一定（或最好）用 that 的情況

  • 先行詞前面有最高級、序數、the only、the same、all、every、no、any：She is the kindest person that I know. / He was the first student that arrived. / This is all that I have.
  • 先行詞是人 ＋ 物：Look at the boy and his dog that are running.
  • 句子已經用 who、which 開頭（避免重複）：Who is the man that is talking to Ms. Lin?

不能用 that 的情況

  • 介系詞後面：with that ✗ → with whom / with which
  • 逗號後面（非限定用法）：My dad, that is a cook,… ✗ → My dad, who is a cook,…
  • 所有格：that 沒有所有格，用 whose。
PART 7

what ＝ the thing(s) that（沒有先行詞）

what 本身已經包含先行詞，意思是「…的東西／事情」，前面不能再有名詞。

What you said is true. = The thing that you said is true.你說的是真的。
I don't understand what he means.我不懂他的意思。
This is the book what I want. ✗ → This is the book that / which I want.（前面已經有先行詞 the book）
PART 8

限定用法與非限定用法（有沒有逗號）

限定用法（沒有逗號）非限定用法（有逗號）
作用指出「是哪一個」補充說明（拿掉句子仍完整）
例句The man who is standing there is my father.
（站在那裡的那個人是我爸）		My father, who loves hiking, is a teacher.
（我爸爸——他喜歡爬山——是老師）
可以用 that？可以不行
先行詞是專有名詞或獨一無二的東西（My father、Taipei、the sun），只能用非限定（加逗號）：Taipei, which is the capital of Taiwan, is a busy city.
PART 9

關係副詞：where（地方）、when（時間）

This is the park. I often play in the park.
→ This is the park where I often play. = …the park in which I often play.where ＝ 介系詞 ＋ which（地方）
I still remember the day when we first met.我還記得我們第一次見面的那一天。（when ＝ on which）
where 還是 which？看子句缺什麼：缺「地方副詞」→ where；缺「主詞或受詞」→ which。
This is the park where I play.（play 不需要受詞）／ This is the park which I visited.（visited 缺受詞）

句型整理表

用法例句

COMMON MISTAKES

常見錯誤

The girl which sings well is my cousin.The girl who sings well is my cousin.先行詞是人，用 who／that。
The boys who lives next door are twins.The boys who live next door are twins.子句的動詞跟著先行詞 the boys（複數）。
The movie which we watched it was great.The movie which we watched was great.關代已經代替 it，不能重複。
The man with that she works is kind.The man with whom she works is kind.介系詞後面不能用 that（也不能用 who）。
The girl who her dog is big is Amy.The girl whose dog is big is Amy.「她的狗」用所有格 whose。
This is the book what I want.This is the book that I want.有先行詞（the book）就不能用 what。
My mom, that is a nurse, works at night.My mom, who is a nurse, works at night.逗號後面（非限定）不能用 that。
This is the park which I often play.This is the park where I often play.play 後面不需要受詞，缺的是地方 → where。
