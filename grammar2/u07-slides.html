Phrasal Verbs

本單元目標與暖身

學完這個單元，你會

  • 分辨「動詞 + 副詞」和「動詞 + 介系詞」
  • 代名詞 it / them 放對位置：turn it on、look for it
  • 記住常考的片語動詞和意思
  • 分清楚 put off / call off、hand in / hand out、put on / wear

暖身 3 題

1It's dark. Turn ____ the light. (on / off)on
2I'm looking ____ my phone. (at / for)for
3Here's your coat. Put ____. (on it / it on)it on

兩種片語動詞：受詞放哪裡？

動詞 + 副詞（可分開）動詞 + 介系詞（不可分開）
例子turn on、pick up、give uplook at、listen to、wait for
名詞受詞turn on the light ✓ / turn the light on ✓look at the bird ✓
代名詞受詞turn it on ✓ / turn on it ✗look at it ✓ / look it at ✗

副詞型：代名詞夾中間

Turn the light on. → Turn it on.
→ it 放中間

介系詞型：受詞永遠在後面

Look at the bird. → Look at it.
→ it 放後面

動詞 + 副詞：開關、穿脫

片語意思例句
turn on / off打開／關掉
It's dark. Turn it on. （太暗了，把它打開）
turn up / down開大聲／關小聲
The music is too loud. Turn it down. （音樂太大聲，關小聲一點）
put on / take off穿上／脫掉
It's cold. Put your coat on. （很冷，把外套穿上）
try on試穿
Can I try these shoes on? （我可以試穿這雙鞋嗎？）
wake up叫醒
Please wake me up at six. （請六點叫我起床）

動詞 + 副詞：放、給、找、交

片語意思例句
pick up撿起；接人
Dad will pick me up at five. （爸爸五點來接我）
put away收好
Put your toys away. （把玩具收好）
give up / give back放棄／歸還
Don't give it up. （不要放棄）
look up / find out查／查出
Look the word up in a dictionary. （在字典裡查這個字）
hand in / hand out繳交／分發
Please hand in your homework. （請交作業）
put off / call off延後／取消
They called the game off. （他們取消了比賽）
沒有受詞的：get up、grow up、come back、go out、sit down、hurry up

動詞 + 介系詞：不可分開

片語意思例句
look at / look for看／找
I'm looking for my keys. （我在找鑰匙）
look after / take care of照顧
Can you look after my cat? （你可以照顧我的貓嗎？）
listen to聽
I like listening to music. （我喜歡聽音樂）
wait for等
We're waiting for the bus. （我們在等公車）
belong to屬於
This bag belongs to Lisa. （這個包包是 Lisa 的）
get on / get off上車／下車
Get off at the next stop. （下一站下車）

三個字的片語動詞

不可分開

受詞放最後面
片語意思
run out of用完
look forward to期待（接 V-ing）
get along with和…相處
catch up with趕上
come up with想出（點子）

例句

examples
We've run out of milk.
→ 牛奶用完了
Do you get along with your classmates?
→ 和同學處得好嗎？
She came up with a great idea.
→ 想出好點子

容易混淆的幾組

put off 和 call off

Put off the meeting.
→ 延後，之後還會開
Call off the meeting.
→ 取消，不開了

hand in 和 hand out

Hand in your work.
→ 交上去
Hand out the papers.
→ 發下去

put on 和 wear

Put on your coat.
→ 穿上的動作
She is wearing a red coat.
→ 穿著的狀態

look at / for / after / up

Look at me. / Look for it.
→ 看／找
Look after the baby. / Look it up.
→ 照顧／查

學生最常錯的 5 句

1
Please turn off it. → Please turn it off.
副詞型，代名詞夾中間。
2
I'm looking it for. → I'm looking for it.
介系詞型，受詞放後面。
3
I'm listening music. → I'm listening to music.
listen 要加 to。
4
We are waiting the bus. → We are waiting for the bus.
wait for + 受詞。
5
She is putting on a red dress in the photo. → She is wearing a red dress.
穿著的狀態用 wear。
